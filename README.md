# al-Khwārizmī — source editions

Source-faithful digital editions of the works of Muḥammad ibn Mūsā al-Khwārizmī (fl. c. 820) that survive in accessible witnesses, each witness in its own language and layer: the Arabic text where it survives, the Latin recensions where the Arabic is lost, and every editor's matter credited as such. The plan, the witnesses and the sessions are in [controls/](controls/), the project packet: [the boundary of the corpus](controls/00_READ_FIRST.md), [the session map](controls/05_SESSION_MAP.tsv), [the source gate](controls/04_SOURCE_GATE_AND_WITNESS_MAP.tsv).

## Published

All the editions are public on GitHub, in [KokunoYumeto/al-khwarizmi-public](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/al-khwarizmi-public). An earlier, incomplete state is on Zenodo, concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23464530](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23464530): thirteen versions of 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20, the last of which ([10.5281/zenodo.23513038](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23513038)) holds the complete algebra and the geography to frame 150. That record is no longer updated.

## Errata of the printed editions

The errors of the printed editions found in the course of the transcription are kept apart from the transcriptions, which keep the print: each is a critical note with the printed reading, the proposed correction, its evidence and a confidence, in the edition's `critical_findings.tsv` and in the section «Critical notes» at the end of its PDF.
