@obs Where the gap lies between the distances inside a word and those between words, a word space is transcribed: سنة ٧٤٣ (L07; 15 between the ة and the first digit), at the boundary of the gaps after ة, 1 to 14 without a space and 16 or more with one; in doubt, the space is written. انظر كم (L01): the ر stands 39 px from the ك above the baseline and its tail 26 px from it, beyond the 8 to 19 px of the tail inside a word.
