@obs The figure: a square divided by two lines into four. The upper right square is marked المال, with ا at its upper right corner and ب at its lower left corner, the corner it shares with the other three; the upper left rectangle is marked ح; the lower right rectangle carries a mark shaped like a question mark, perhaps a ج; the lower left square is marked ٢٥. د stands outside the upper left corner of the whole and ه outside its lower right corner. The figure is drawn, not set in type, and is reproduced as an image.
@obs Letters printed as the labels of the figure carry a short wavy line above them: اب (L06, L08, L09, L10, L18), the line over its ب; ده (L14), the line over its د, where frame 36 has it over the ه; ح (L08), the line over the left half of the letter and the space after it.
@obs L08: the two labels after سطحا are ح and a ج printed upside down: the sort is turned through 180 degrees, so that the bowl of the letter opens to the left, the stroke that tops a ج runs along its foot, and the dot stands inside the bowl. Transcribed {inv:ج} and set upside down in the PDF. See the critical note AK01-CN002.
@obs L20: the statement of the fifth case, from وأما to اجذاره, is printed in a bold face; the rest of the line, from فا نانجعل, in the face of the text. The line is printed with a paragraph indent. In the bold face the joined د and ذ come close to the shape of ر and ز; the letter after the ج of اجذاره reaches as deep below the line as the joined د of وأحد, not as deep as the joined ر of وعشرون, and is read ذ.
@obs Set without a word space after a non-connecting letter, and transcribed so: مازدنا (L17; the ا and the ز stand 11 px apart, nearest approach of the letters at 400 ppi, where إذا زدت on frame 041, L02, sets them 32 px apart). In the bold face of L20 the word spaces are narrow: the ل of مال and the و of وأحد stand 8 px apart; the د of وأحد and the و of وعشرون stand 12 px apart, which does not show whether a space is set, and the space is written: وأحد وعشرون. After a connecting letter, whose final form marks the end of the word, a word space is transcribed where the print sets the words close: أن نزيد and فصارت خمسة (L07), طول كل (L08), سطح ده (L14), مال وأحد, تعدل عشرة, نجعل المال and المال سطحاً (L20).
