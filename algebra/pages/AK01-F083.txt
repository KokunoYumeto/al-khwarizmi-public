@obs Notes (١) and (٢) of the editors. (١) states the principle that they take the chapter to follow: when an heir owes the estate a debt larger than his share, he keeps the whole of it, part as his share and the rest as a gift from the testator; with each son's share x, 2/3 (10 + x) = 2x, x = 5: the legatee receives five dirhams and the other son five. (٢) solves the second problem: the bequest 1/5 (10 + x) + 1, the rest 4/5 (10 + x) − 1 equal to two shares, 2x; x = 5 5/6 and the bequest 4 1/6. Both results check exactly. Neither note is reproduced.
