@critical AK01-CN003 | labels of the text and of the figure disagree | L07 | ع, thirteen times (L07, L08, L09, L15, L16, L21, L22) | ج | The text names with ع the point at which the line حه is halved, and the lines and surfaces that end there. The figure of the demonstration (frame 041) has no ع: at the middle of the line حه it has a letter shaped like ح without a dot, distinct from the ح at the end of the line. Without ع, the labels of the text are ا ب د ه ز ح ط ك ل م ن: the first fourteen letters of the abjad order without و and ي, and without ج, the one letter of that run the text does not use; ع comes later in that order. On frame 036 the same sort names the rectangle that the figure there labels ج (AK01-CN001). The facsimile of this page of the manuscript in the edition (frame 039) writes ج, a jīm with its dot: «على نقطة ج», «خط هج». | high: the manuscript writes ج, and the figure has no ع; whether the printer set ع for ج on purpose or in error is not known | none on the mathematics: the labels only name the points and lines of the figure
