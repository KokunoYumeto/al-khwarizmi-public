@critical AK01-CN035 | omission in the statement | L04 | سبعمائة درهم ونصف شىء | سبعمائة درهم غير شىء ونصف شىء | The master's heirs have four hundred less half a thing (L03), three hundred less a thing of the dead slave's work-off and a hundred and half a thing returned by inheritance (L01-L03), and take the other slave's work-off, three hundred less a thing (L04): together seven hundred less a thing and a half. The print states seven hundred dirhams «and half a thing», without «غير شىء». The next lines restore the amount by a thing and a half, «فأجبر ذلك بشىء ونصف شىء», and find seven hundred equal to five things and a half (L05-L06), which is the equation of seven hundred less a thing and a half equal to four things; its result, a hundred and twenty-seven and three elevenths (L06-L07), is 700 ÷ 5½ (checked exactly). | high that the sense requires غير شىء; whether the words were lost in the print or in the manuscript is not known | none on the result: the equation and the bequest of a hundred and twenty-seven and three elevenths are those of seven hundred less a thing and a half
