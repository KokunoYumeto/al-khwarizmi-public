@obs L05: «أن تزيد عليه ضعفه وأربعة أجزاء من تسعة عشر جزءاً», as printed: the completion turns nineteen forty-seconds of the estate into a whole estate, which multiplies by forty-two nineteenths, two and four nineteenths: the amount is added to itself once, and four nineteenths of it besides.
