@obs Set without a word space after the tā' marbūṭa, and transcribed so: العشرةعشرةناقصةوالاثنان, العشرةعشرون (L04), وعشرةفى (L12, twice), بعشرةأشياء (L12). The ة stands 4 to 9 px from the next letter (nearest approach of the letters at 400 ppi), as on frames 032-043 where the print sets these letters without a space (5 to 10 px); with a space they stand 17 to 46 px apart, and on this page the same words are set spaced in العشرة عشرة (L01, 26 and 34 px), عشرة فى and بعشرة أشياء (L12, 25 and 29 px). In L04 the ة of عشرة stands 13 px from the ن of ناقصة: the ن and the other toothed letters stand some px further from the preceding letter than other letters do (frame 045, L09 and L13: 11 px for أشياءناقصة), and the words are taken as set without a space.
