@obs Set without a word space after a non-connecting letter, and transcribed so: عادلهامن (L04), والعددالتى (L05), جذرهاوهو (L09). @obs L02: the note mark (١) stands after وهو, close before خمسة; L07: the note mark (٢) stands close before أن. The full stops after الله (L04) and المال (L07) are set close.
