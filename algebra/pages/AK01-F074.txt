@obs The letters that label the figure carry a short wavy line above them (here a line above), over one of their letters; the machine reading misreads most of them, as on the earlier frames; the ط after نقطة in L03 is printed without it and is transcribed without it. The ب of the labels is printed without its dot (ٮ), as on frames 036-073, and transcribed so; ه in its knotted initial form. The letters of a label are set as separate sorts, a narrow space apart (ٮ and ك in L02, 30 px; ا and د in L07, 37 px, the line over the ا only), and are transcribed together as one label, as on the earlier frames.
@obs The figure, drawn, not set in type, below L11: a square with ا at the upper right corner, ٮ at the upper left, a hooked ح at the lower right and د at the lower left; ط at the middle of the upper side, ز at the middle of the left side, ه at the middle of the right side, and at the middle of the lower side a letter shaped like ح without a dot; ك at the centre, where the line from ط to the lower side crosses the line from ه to ز; the lines from ط to ز, from ز to the middle of the lower side, from there to ه and from ه to ط form the inner square. The ب of the figure has no dot.
