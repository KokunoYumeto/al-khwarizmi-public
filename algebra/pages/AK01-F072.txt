% Frame 072, printed p. 56: the chapter on mensuration continued: the circumference by three and a seventh is a
% convention among people, not a necessity; the geometers have two other sayings: the root of ten times the square of
% the diameter, and, for the astronomers, the diameter times 62832 divided by 20000; and the diameter is the
% circumference divided by three and a seventh. The area of a circle is half the diameter times half the
% circumference, as for every figure with equal sides and angles; or the square of the diameter less its seventh and
% half its seventh. A segment of a circle is a half, less than a half or more than a half, by its sagitta; the
% diameter from the chord and the sagitta. Below the rule three footnotes of the editors.
