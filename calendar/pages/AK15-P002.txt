@obs L01 «مقالة» in a large type with a long swash; L05 in a larger type. Between L04 and L05 a rule with an ornament in its middle, and below it, a little to the left of the middle, a small printed mark like a tooth with a curl (about 30 px wide); not transcribed. L11: «١٣٦٦ هـ» over a rule and «١٩٤٧ م» under it, with «سنة» in a smaller type at the right end of the rule; L12, at the lower right of the page: the print run «٥٠٠» over a rule and «١٣٥٦ ف» (the year of the Fasli era) under it.
@obs L12: «تعدا. الطبع»: the second د of تعداد (so printed in full on PDF 1, L14) is missing, and a small dot (6 px) stands in its place, at the height of the baseline; transcribed as printed. L03: «تاليف», without hamza; L04: «الخوارزمى», with the dot of the خ, the final ى without dots.
@obs The edition was printed with a separate pagination and a title page of its own dated 1366/1947 and bound with other treatises in collections dated 1367/1948 (PDF 1); de Blois, n. 2, describes two such collections, of nine and of eleven treatises, with al-Khwarizmi's first in both.
