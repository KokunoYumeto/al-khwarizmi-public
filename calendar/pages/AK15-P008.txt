@obs Checked against the four gates of the standard calendar (calendar/checks/gates.py), with the hours counted as on p. 5 (PDF 6). L01-L02 with p. 6 (PDF 7) L18-L19, a leap year: a molad from Sunday 20h 491p to the limit of Monday gives Monday with the year complete (385 days). L02-L04, a common year: before Sunday 9h 204p, Monday, deficient (353 days); L04-L07: after it, to the limit of Monday, Monday, complete (355 days), see AK15-CN020. L07-L10, a common year after a leap year: a molad after 589 parts of the fourth hour of the day of Monday (Monday 15h 589p, see AK15-CN021) gives Tuesday with the year regular (354 days), the rule later called BeTUTaKPaT: with a molad from Monday 15h 589p the molad of the leap year before lies 5 days 21 hours 589 parts earlier, on Tuesday at 18h or later, so that its first of Tishri was Thursday, and a first of Tishri on Monday would leave it 382 days.
@obs Checked: the solar year of L11-L12, 365 days and 5 3791/4104 hours, is the nineteen-year cycle divided by 19: 6939d 16h 595p (p. 5, PDF 6, L09-L10, with AK15-CN011) / 19 = 365d 5h 997 12/19 parts, and 997 12/19 parts = 18955/20520 hour = 3791/4104 hour, as de Blois states for this section.
