@obs The description of the islands of the outer western sea goes on from p. 85 (frame 091), whose last words, «خمس جزائر صغار* صفّ واحد كلّ واحدة مقدارها ربع», L01 continues with «جزء وربع جزء مابين طول ٮد {z} (١٣٠٧) الى ٮح {z} (١٣٠٨) وأوسطها على عرض سٮ ك», five small islands in one row, each of the size of a quarter of a degree and a quarter of a degree, between the longitude ٮد {z} and ٮح {z}, and their middles on the latitude سٮ ك: the two ends of the row carry the running numbers (١٣٠٧) and (١٣٠٨). «مابين» is printed as one word; «وأوسطها» has a hamza over the alif.
@obs L02-L05: three small islands, each «جزيرة صغيرة مقدار ثلث جزء», a small island of the size of a third of a degree, with the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٣٠٩)-(١٣١١); each entry ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs L05-L16: «جزيرة اوليا فيها مدن اوّلها عند طول ٮح ل والعرض نا ل», the island اوليا, on which are towns, the first of them at the longitude ٮح ل and the latitude 51° 30′; then its coast from point to point, «وتمرّ … وتصير الى …»: «على صورة القوارة» (L06, L16), along the shape of the qawwāra; «مصبّ نهر» (L06-L07, L07, L12), the mouth of a river; «تمرّ بمدينة اماعس وبمصبّ نهر» (L08), past the town of اماعس and the mouth of a river; «تمرّ بمصبّ نهر ثمّ تصير الى» (L14), past the mouth of a river, then it comes to; «موضع مسيل نهرين» (L16), a place where two rivers flow out; the island goes on on p. 87. Points 1313 and 1327 (L06, L16) are given by their latitude only, «تمرّ بعرض». The running numbers run (١٣١٢)-(١٣٢٧) and the numbers of Suhrāb [٨٨٢]-[٨٩٧], without a gap.
