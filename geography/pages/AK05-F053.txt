@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 46-47: each of its lines continues the same line of p. 46 (frame 052). L01 gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction of the last mountain of the second clime, entry 659/660; L02, «عرضه ل *», ends the heading of p. 46, L02, the third clime and its latitude, 30; L03, «الإقليم الثالث *», ends «* الجبال الّتى فى», the mountains in the third clime; L04-L16 give the boundaries, the colours and the directions of the mountains of p. 46, L04-L16, entries 661/662-685/686. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads. The headings stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their beginnings on p. 46; transcribed as headings.
