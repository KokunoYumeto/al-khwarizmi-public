@obs The places in which the boundaries of the countries are written end on this page. L01 begins with the number of Suhrāb, [٥٤١], of the last country of p. 104 (frame 110, L11, entry 1592). Then the countries, each with the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}: «بلاد عرمانيا وهى ارض الصقالبة» (L01), Germania, which is the land of the Slavs; «بلاد سڡمرقى وهى الجزيرة المفتوحة الى البرّ» (L02), سڡمرقى, which is the peninsula open to the land; «بلاد غابة ارقونيوس» (L03), the land of the forest of ارقونيوس; «بلاد سرماطيه وهى ارض برجان» (L04), Sarmatia, which is the land of the Burjān; «بلاد الٮتز» (L05); «جزيرة طوقيا وهى متّصلة بالتيز» (L06), the island طوقيا, which is joined to التيز; «بلاد سرماطيه وهى ارض اللان» (L07), Sarmatia, which is the land of the Alans; «بلاد اسقونيا وهى ارض الترك» (L08-L09), the land of the Turks; «بلاد اسقوثيا وهى ارض التغزغز» (L09-L10), Scythia, which is the land of the Toghuzghuz; «بلاد سيرقى وهى ارض سينستان» (L11); and «بلاد ماجوج عند طول …» (L12), Magog, without «وسطها». Each entry ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk; the last, after [٥٥٠] and the note mark u, closes the section.
@obs The running numbers run (١٥٩٣)-(١٦٠٣) without a gap. The numbers of Suhrāb stand after each running number: [٥٤٢]-[٥٤٥] after (١٥٩٣)-(١٥٩٦), then [٥٥١] and [٥٥٢] after (١٥٩٧) and (١٥٩٨), then [٥٤٦]-[٥٥٠] after (١٥٩٩)-(١٦٠٣): Suhrāb has the island طوقيا and the second Sarmatia after the other countries of the page.
@obs L10: «وسط⟨ها⟩», the ها between angle brackets, followed by the note mark o: the manuscript lacks it, and the editor supplies it (note o). Transcribed with the brackets.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «البرّ» (L02) with a shadda over the ر; «متّصلة» (L06) with a shadda over the ت.
@obs Names: «عرمانيا», «الصقالبة», «غابة», «ارقونيوس», «سرماطيه», «برجان», «طوقيا», «بالتيز», «اللان», «اسقونيا», «الترك», «اسقوثيا», «التغزغز», «سينستان» and «ماجوج» are printed with all their dots. L02: «سڡمرقى»: after the س a raised loop without dots, then a small loop on the line, the م, the ر and «قى», whose ق has its two dots. L11: «سيرقى», the ي with its two dots below joined at the top to the head of the ر. L05: «الٮتز»: after the ل a small tooth without a dot, then a taller tooth with two dots above, joined at the top to the head of the ز, which has its dot; in «بالتيز» of L06 the two dots of the ت stand over the small tooth and the two dots of the ي below the taller one. Transcribed as printed.
@unres L05: the two dots over the taller tooth may be the dots of the ت displaced from the small tooth, with those of a ي not printed: التيز, the name of L06. Transcribed الٮتز.
@obs Numerals: «نب» (L01, L05, the degrees of the latitude), with the dot of the ن above and that of the ب below, 52; «قد» (L09) and «قعا» (L12), with the two dots of the ق, 104 and 171; «نز» (L09, the degrees of the latitude), with the dot of the ن and that of the ز, 57; «ٮج» (L07, the degrees of the latitude), a tooth without its dot joined to a ج with its dot, which read with a ي gives 13 and read with a ن 53. Without dots, transcribed so: a tooth in «ٮح» (L04, L06, L12, the degrees of the latitude; L07, the degrees of the longitude), «ٮط» (L08, L10) and «ٮد» (L11), the degrees of the latitude, which read as a ن give 58, 59 and 54; the loop of ف or ق in «ڡمح» (L10) and «ڡس» (L11), the degrees of the longitude, which read with ق give 148 and 160.
@obs L06, the degrees of the longitude, are printed with the notched form ٮر of frame 041 (L06): a short tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two forming a notch, 3 px deep, at the top, where the isolated ر of the numerals is a single rounded stroke (frame 072, L06); in these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ٮر, which read as نز gives 57 and read as يز 17. The editor's note h gives the variant reading ر ه.
@unres L06: the notched form may also be read as a single ر whose head is split, ر, 7. Transcribed ٮر.
@obs The ح of the numerals, «ٮح» (L04, L06, L07, L12), «عح» (L08) and «ڡمح» (L10), shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3, which L07 prints with its dot in «ٮج». Transcribed as printed.
