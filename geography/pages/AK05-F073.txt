@obs The running text of the outer western sea goes on from p. 66 (frame 072): the coast from point to point, «يمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}; after each point stand the editor's running number in parentheses, (٩٨٤)-(١٠٠٢), and the number of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٥٦٩]-[٥٨٦]; point 988 (L04) has none.
@obs L03-L06: after the shape of a qawwāra the coast reaches Tangier, «وذلك عند طنجة», and leaves two thirds of a degree of this place, «ثمّ يترك مقدار ثلثى جزء من هٰذا الموضع», on the line of the clime whose latitude is 36 (L04); this place is joined to the sea of Tangier, and on it is the image of two idols of copper, which carry in their hands another idol by its legs; it is said that these are the landmarks of Hercules, «اعلام هرقل», beyond which nobody has passed (L05-L06). L09: «وهناك مصبّ نهر اس», there is the mouth of the river اس.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ» and «ويمرّ», with a shadda over the ر; «ثمّ», «انّ», «خطّ», «مصبّ» and «متّصل», with a shadda; «الّذى» and «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل; «هٰذا» and «وهٰذا», with a dagger alif over the ه (L04, L06); «جزء», with a hamza on the line (L04); «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif (L04); «بأيديهما», with a hamza over the alif (L06); «آخر», with a madda (L05); «صنماً», with the tanwin of the accusative over its alif (L05). L06: «⟨الموضع⟩», between angle brackets, followed by the note mark f: the editor supplies the word, which the manuscript lacks (note f).
@obs Note marks: L03, a after the latitude; L05, b after «عليه» and c after «صنماً»; L06, d after «هرقل» and e after «احد»; L09, g after the longitude «د ل»; L12, h after the second latitude; L14, i after the longitude; L15, k after the first latitude and l after the second longitude.
@obs L07, the degrees of the first longitude: a small solid teardrop, 13 by 16 px, larger than the dots of the font (9 by 9 px) and much smaller than the sign for zero, with the form of the round ه of frame 065 (L15); transcribed ه, 5.
@obs L12, the degrees of the first latitude: a small round loop, 16 by 18 px, on the line, 5 px to the right of an alif, with the joining stroke between them not printed; the latitude «ما ٮه» of the same line prints the initial م joined to its alif, and the loop has its size. Transcribed ما, 41, between the latitudes 39° 40′ (L11) and «ما ٮه», 41 degrees (L12).
@unres L12: the loop before the alif in the first latitude may also be read as a round ه with a separate alif: ه ا. Transcribed ما.
@obs L14: the minutes of the longitude are printed «⟨م⟩ه»: an initial م between angle brackets, then the final ه of «مه», 45, followed by the note mark i: the editor supplies the م from S where the manuscript has a gap (note i). Transcribed with the brackets in the cell.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: لٮ (L02, the degrees of the latitude), a ل joined to a final ب without its dot, 32; مٮ (L13, the degrees of the first latitude), 42; ٮه (L07, the minutes of the second longitude; L11, the minutes of the first latitude; L12, the minutes of the second latitude), a tooth without its dot joined to a final ه.
@obs In these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed: ر (L03, L07, L15, the degrees of the longitude), لر (L08, L09, the degrees of the latitude), 37. L07 prints لز, with the dot, in the degrees of the second latitude.
@obs The ح of the numerals, ح (L01, L02, the degrees of the longitude), لح (L09, L10) and مح (L14, both latitudes), shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3, which in مح would give 43 and 43° 15′, between the latitudes 42 (L13) and 44 (L15). Transcribed as printed.
@obs The isolated ى, 10, has no dots (L01, L07, L08, L10). The isolated ك, 20, is printed with the small inner stroke of the letter (L07, L15).
