@obs The title page is set in Roman capitals, with three lines in black letter (Fraktur), transcribed in Roman type: «Herausgegeben» (L05), «Mit fünf Tafeln in Lichtdruck» (L11) and «Otto Harrassowitz · Leipzig» (L13). The transliterated names keep the diacritics of the print: the dots below Ṣ, Ḍ and Ḥ, the macrons, the haček of Ǧ and the breve below Ḫ of «AL-ḪUWĀRIZMĪ», the half ring of «ǦAʿFAR». The shelfmark of the manuscript, «COD. 4247», stands between two thin slanting strokes, transcribed as slashes; its figures are old-style. A rule separates the date, «MCMXXVI», 1926, from the publisher's line.
@obs L10: the editor's name is printed «MŹIK», with an acute accent over the Z, where the series title page prints «MŽIK», with a haček (frame 212, L04), the form of the name Mžik; see the critical note. The reprint's copy of this page has the same accent (frame 214).
@obs The volume is the third of the series «Bibliothek arabischer Historiker und Geographen» (frame 212). Its back, frame 209, carries the imprint.
