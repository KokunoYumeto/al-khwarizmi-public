@obs The table goes on from frame 018 without a heading: the cities of the third clime, entries 148-164. For entries 149-158 the table has a further pair of columns, ruled like the others, between the names and the longitudes: it holds a first longitude, degrees and minutes, then the word «اخرى» stands between the columns, and the longitude columns hold the second; the latitude follows as usual. Entries 148 and 159-164 have one longitude, and their names stand over the further columns; transcribed so, the name spanning them. Entries 148-158 have no numbers of Suhrāb; entries 159-164 have [١٢٤]-[١٢٩]. Entries 148-158 and 160 and 163 are printed without the word «مدينة»; entry 161 is the word «مدينة» alone.
