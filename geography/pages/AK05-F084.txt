@obs The running text goes on from p. 77 (frame 083), whose last point, «يمرّ الى طول قيو م والعرض ٮه ك», L01 numbers (١١٨٥) [٧٧١]: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١١٨٥)-(١٢٠٧), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٧٧١]-[٧٩٣], without a gap. L05: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 20ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, at its end, between «والعرض» and the latitude «ٮر مه» at the beginning of L06; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs The places of the coast: L01-L02: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة قطرا ومصبّ نهر ڡرٮدس», below the town of قطرا and the mouth of the river ڡرٮدس; L03: «ويمرّ بمدينة موزيرس ومصبّ نهر ڡٮدس», past the town of Muziris and the mouth of the river ڡٮدس; L05: «مماسّ* لمدينة قاطلا», touching the town of قاطلا; L09: «ويمرّ بمدينة اميرس», past the town of اميرس; L13: «ويمرّ الى اسفل مدينة عند طول», below a town whose name is not given; L14: «وهٰذا الموضع عند الدردور», and this place is at al-Durdūr, the whirlpool. L12, L15, L16: «يمرّ على صورة القوارة», along the shape of the qawwāra; points 1202 and 1207 (L12, L16) are given by their latitude only, «يمرّ بعرض», and point 1205 (L15) by its longitude only, «يمرّ بطول».
@obs L05: «مماسّ*»: an asterisk with six solid arms stands after the word, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10). Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ», «ويمرّ», «ومصبّ» and «مماسّ» with a shadda; «بأسفل» with a hamza over the alif; «وهٰذا» with a dagger alif above the ه. «موزيرس» and «اميرس» have the two dots of the ي.
@obs L02: «ڡرٮدس»: an upright loop without dots, 15 by 18 px, joined at its foot by a short stroke to a ر, whose head is a small rise at the joint and whose tail descends 22 px below the line, as deep as the ر of «نهر» before it; the و of «ويصير» in the same line descends 11 px. Then a tooth without a dot, 17 px high, joined to a final د, whose arm rises 28 px, and a final س. Transcribed ڡرٮدس.
@unres L02: the loop with the descending stroke after «نهر» may also be read as a و: وٮدس. Transcribed ڡرٮدس.
@obs L03: «ڡٮدس»: an upright loop without dots, 13 by 16 px, joined to a low rise, 10 px wide and 5 to 7 px above the joining stroke, then a final د, whose arm rises 27 px, and a final س. The rise stands above the joining stroke with no part below it, as the tooth of «ڡٮح» (L02); transcribed as a low tooth without a dot.
@unres L03: the low rise between the loop and the د may also be read as a medial م: ڡمدس. Transcribed ڡٮدس.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: a tooth without its dot in the degrees of the latitude, ٮح (L01, L04, L06), ٮد (L02), ٮو (L03, L05), ٮر (L06) and ٮط (L07, twice; L08), which read with a ي give 18, 14, 16, 17 and 19; ں (L07, the minutes of the longitude), the round bowl of a final ن without its dot, 35 px wide, 50; and the loop of ف or ق without dots in the degrees of the longitude: «ڡٮح» (L02), the loop and a short tooth without a dot that rises above the joining stroke, with no part below it, where the medial م of these numerals is a diamond that stands out above and below the stroke (frame 013, L13, «ڡمح») or is set under the ق (frame 082, L16, «قمط»), before the head of the ح, 118 or 158 read with ق; «ڡط» (L03), 109 or 89; «ڡح» (L09), 108 or 88. With their dots: «قيو» (L01), with the dots of the ق and of the ي, 116; «قب» (L10), with the dot of the ب, 102; «فز» (L14, L15) and «فو» (L15), with the dot of the ف, 87 and 86, and «فز» with the dot of the ز.
