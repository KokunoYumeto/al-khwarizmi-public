@obs The table goes on from frame 039 without a heading: the cities of the seventh clime, entries 488-499. Entries 493 and 499 have no number of Suhrāb; the others have [٤٥٠]-[٤٥٤] (entries 488-492), [٤٥٥]-[٤٥٨] (entries 494-497) and [٤٦٣] (entry 498). Entries 489, 493 and 499 begin with the word «مدينة»; entry 498 has it in «وهى المدينة الكبيرة». The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 9ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside entry 497 (L10), as on frames 028, 030 and 032.
@obs L01: «اٮورٮوں» is printed without dots: after the alif, a short tooth, 15 px high, joined to a و; a ر; a second short tooth joined to a و; and a final ن without its dot. Each tooth stands at the right of the loop of its و, which the word-initial و of «وهى» (L11) has not. Transcribed اٮورٮوں; the forms of the editor's note a have ڡ, ث and ب in the places of the teeth.
@obs L02: «ثورٮا»: the ث with its three dots; after the ر, a low tooth without a dot joined to the final alif. «حدّ», with a shadda over the د.
@obs L04: «انقزه»: the ن with its dot, the ق with its two dots, and a third dot, 10 by 11 px, above the stroke that joins the ق to the descending letter: the dot of a ز. Transcribed انقزه; the form of the editor's note c has ر.
@obs L07: «ڡلميش»: the initial loop without dots; the ي with its two dots and the ش with its three. L09: «دسقاٮا»: the ق with its two dots; the tooth before the final alif without a dot.
