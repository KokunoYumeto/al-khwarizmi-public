@obs The description of the island with a town of p. 91 (frame 097, L15) goes on: its coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets. L01: «تمرّ على صورة الطيلسان تمرّ بعرض لح {z} (١٤٠٩)», along the shape of the ṭaylasān at the latitude لح {z} (point 1409, given by its latitude only); then points 1410 and 1411; L03-L04: «مصبّ نهر وتمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول مه ن والعرض لد {z}», the mouth of a river; and it passes to the place from which we began, which is at the longitude 45° 50′ and the latitude 34° 0′, without a running number; the editor's note e identifies it with point 1408, whose longitude is printed «مه ں» there, without the dot of the ن (frame 097, L15).
@obs L04-L05: «خمس جزائر صغار* مقدار سدس جزء سدس جزء فيما بين طول مز ں (١٤١٢) الى طول ن ن (١٤١٣) وهى كلّها على عرض لا ں», five small islands of the size of a sixth of a degree by a sixth of a degree, between the longitude مز ں and the longitude 50° 50′, all of them on the latitude لا ں.
@obs L05-L09: «جزيرة فيها مدينة اوّلها عند طول مط ك والعرض له {z}», an island without a name, on which is a town; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 49° 20′ and the latitude 35° 0′ (point 1414); then «وتمرّ على صورة الطيلسان تمرّ بعرض لد م» (L06), along the shape of the ṭaylasān at the latitude 34° 40′ (point 1415, given by its latitude only); points 1416-1418; and «تمرّ الى طول مط ك والعرض له {z} وهو الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأ⟨نا⟩» (L09), to the longitude 49° 20′ and the latitude 35° 0′, which is the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note g identifies it with point 1414.
@obs L09-L11: «عشر جزائر صغار* مقدار عشر جزء فى عشر جزء وموضعها فى مقدار جزء ونصف فى جزء ونصف وسط هٰذه الجزائر عند طول ن ك والعرض لر ك (١٤١٩)», ten small islands, each of the size of a tenth of a degree by a tenth of a degree, whose place is within one and a half degrees by one and a half degrees; the middle of these islands at the longitude 50° 20′ and the latitude لر ك.
@obs L11-L16: «جزيرة كيوس مقدار جزء ونصف فى جزء» (L11-L12), the island كيوس, of one and a half degrees by one degree, point 1420; «جزيرة حرنيس مقدار جزء وربع فى ⟨نصف جزء⟩» (L13), the island حرنيس, of one and a quarter degrees by half a degree, point 1421; and three islands of a quarter of a degree, «جزيرة مقدار ربع جزء» (L14-L16), points 1422-1424. «كيوس» and «حرنيس» are printed with all their dots; the ح of «حرنيس» has none.
@obs The running numbers run (١٤٠٩)-(١٤٢٤) without a gap; the numbers of Suhrāb, [٩٤٤]-[٩٥٣], stand after (١٤٠٩)-(١٤١١), (١٤١٤)-(١٤١٨), (١٤٢٠) and (١٤٢١), without a gap; points 1412, 1413, 1419 and 1422-1424 have none.
