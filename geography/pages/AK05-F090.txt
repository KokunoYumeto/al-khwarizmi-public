@obs L01-L02: the headings of the new part of the work, in the large type, each between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks: «* صفة الجزائر الّتى فى البحار *», the description of the islands in the seas, and «* الجزائر الّتى فى بحر المغرب لخارج *», the islands in the outer western sea. «الجزائر» has a hamza on the tooth before the ر, «الّتى» a shadda over the ل; «البحار» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ح and the alif, which is not transcribed. L02: «لخارج» is printed without the alif of the article: after the ب of «المغرب» and a word space, the ل joins the خ directly, as in «لطيلسان» on p. 74 (frame 080, L12). Transcribed as printed.
@obs L03-L15: twelve islands, each with its size in degrees, «مقدارها جزآن فى جزء ونصف», its size is two degrees by one and a half, and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}. Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk. The editor's running numbers run (١٢٨٣)-(١٢٩٤); the numbers of Suhrāb [٨٦٩]-[٨٧٣] stand after (١٢٨٣)-(١٢٨٧) and [٨٧٤] after (١٢٨٩); islands 1288 and 1290-1294 have none.
@obs The islands: L03: «جزيرة ڡطوا», two degrees by one and a half; L04: «جزيرة ڡلرازيا», «جزء ونصف فى جزء وربع», one and a half by one and a quarter; L05: «جزيرة هارا»; L06: «جزيرة كساڡارسيا»; L08: «جزيرة داعوطلا», each one and a half by one, «مقدار» without the pronoun; L09 and L11: «جزيرة مقدار جزء», an island of one degree, without a name; L10: «جزيرة رفاٮوطس مدوّرة مقدار جزء ونصف», a round island of one and a half degrees; L12-L15: four islands without a name, each «مقدار جزء ونصف فى مثله», one and a half degrees by as much again.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «جزآن» with a madda over the alif; «جزء» with a hamza on the line; «مدوّرة» with a shadda over the و; «فى» ends in a ى without dots.
@obs Letters without dots in the names, transcribed as printed: L03: «ڡطوا», an upright loop without dots joined to the ط; L04: «ڡلرازيا», the loop at the beginning joined to the ل, without a dot; L06: «كساڡارسيا», the loop before the second alif without a dot; L10: «رفاٮوطس», the ف with its dot, the tooth before the و without one.
@obs L06: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 22ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between the asterisk after [٨٧١] and «جزيرة»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs Angle brackets, transcribed as printed: L09: «⟨طول⟩», followed by the note mark e: the editor supplies the word, which the manuscript lacks (note e). L12: «⟨طول د ك والعرض يح م⟩» and L13: «⟨جزيرة مقدار جزء ونصف فى مثله وسطها عند طول⟩», each followed by the note mark k: these words stand in the margin of the manuscript, written by a later hand (note k); without them the text passes from «وسطها عند» in L12 to the longitude «ز ل» in L13.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: «ٮح» (L06, L08, the degrees of the latitude), a tooth without its dot, which read with a ي gives 18, where L12 prints «يح» with the two dots of the ي; «ٮه» (L09, the degrees of the latitude), a tooth without its dot joined to a final ه, which read with a ي gives 15. With their dots: «يا» (L05), the two dots of the ي merged into one bar; «يب» (L07), with the dots of the ي and of the ب, 12; «يو» (L10), 16; «يز» (L11), 17; «ج» (L06, L09), 3; «ز» (L03, L13), 7; «كج» (L15), 23.
@obs The ح of the numerals, ح (L03, L08, L10), ٮح (L06, L08) and يح (L12), shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3, which L06, L09 and L15 print with its dot. Transcribed as printed. The isolated ى, 10, has no dots (L08). The isolated ك, 20, is printed with the small inner stroke of the letter.
