@obs L01-L03 end the sea of Tangier and the seas joined to it: the running text goes on from p. 73 (frame 079), whose last word, «ويمرّ», L01 continues with «الى طول»; the points (١١١٨)-(١١٢٠), with the numbers of Suhrāb [٧٠٤]-[٧٠٦], pass «مماسّ* لجبل لوس», touching the mountain لوس (L01), and the sea is «متّصل* ببحر* المغربىّ الخارج وموضع الأصنام الثلٰثة الّتى ذكرناها آنفاً», joined to the outer western sea and the place of the three idols that we mentioned before (frame 073, L05-L06). L03 ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk. «الأصنام» has a hamza over the alif; «الثلٰثة» a dagger alif above the ل; «الّتى» a shadda over the ل; «آنفاً» a madda over the first alif and the tanwin of the accusative over the last.
@obs L04-L06: the heading of the next section, in the large type, between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks: «* بحر القلزم وبحر* الأخضر وبحر السند وبحر الهند وبحر الصين وبحر البصرة بعضها متّصل ببعض وهو البحر الكبير *», the sea of al-Qulzum, the Green Sea, the sea of Sind, the sea of India, the sea of China and the sea of Basra, joined to one another, which are the Great Sea. «الأخضر» has a hamza over the alif and «متّصل» a shadda over the ت. In this large type the ب of «بحر» and «وبحر» is set above the ح as a short oblique stroke, with its dot below the ح, as in the heading of p. 69 (frame 075).
@obs L01, L07: «مماسّ*»; L03: «متّصل*» and «ببحر*»; L04: «وبحر*»: an asterisk with six solid arms stands after the word, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10).
@obs L07-L15: the course of the new sea from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١١٢١)-(١١٣٢), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٧٠٧]-[٧١٨].
