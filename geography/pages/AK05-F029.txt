@obs The table goes on from frame 028 without a heading: the last cities of the fourth clime, entries 323-332. Then the heading of the fifth clime and of its cities, set between small open stars, and its first cities, entries 333-335. Numbers of Suhrāb stand at entries 323, 324 ([٢٤٦], [٢٤٧]), 327-330 ([٢٤٨], [٢٥١], [٢٥٢], [٢٥٠]) and 333-335 ([٢٧٩]-[٢٨١]); entries 325, 326, 331 and 332 have none. Entries 330 and 333-335 have the word «مدينة»; entry 334 is the word «مدينة» alone.
@obs L03: the name of entry 325 is printed as six dots between angle brackets and its latitude as five dots between angle brackets that stretch across both columns of the latitude, each with the note mark b: the editor's sign for what he could not supply (frame 010, note a); his note b says that the manuscript has a gap. Transcribed as printed, the latitude as one cell spanning the two columns. A speck stands above the dots of the name; it is not transcribed.
@obs L04: entry 326, Bardasīr, has no coordinates: the columns of the numerals are empty, and no rule is printed between entries 325 and 326. The editor's note c says that the manuscript gives no longitude and latitude.
@obs L06: «ڡاقلا»: the first letter is a loop without dots that runs at its foot into the foot of the alif, as the ف of «فارس» (frame 028, L10-L12), where a word-initial و stands apart from the alif and its tail descends below the line (frame 027, L16, «واسط»); transcribed ڡا. L07: «ڡسطلما», the loop without dots joined to the س.
@obs L08: an asterisk stands after اسكندريّة, «مدينة اسكندريّة* الشرقيّة», raised like the editor's note marks: his sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (introduction, frame 184, p. XXIX), here an indefinite noun with a definite attribute, as on frame 014 (L09); transcribed as printed. The minutes of the longitude of this entry are printed as a zero sign between angle brackets, «⟨ō⟩», with the note mark d: the editor supplies them.
@obs Signs in the names, transcribed as printed: L02: «المحمّديّة», with a shadda over the second م and over the ي; L08: «اسكندريّة» and «الشرقيّة», with a shadda over the ي. L10: «سردانيه» ends in a ه without the dots of a ة; «فى» has no dots on the ى.
@obs L11-L12: the latitude of the fifth clime, «ما ō», 41° 0', is printed with a line above the numeral and the zero sign; transcribed with {ov:…}. In both headings the alif of الإقليم carries a hamza below; L12: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل.
