@obs The table of the mountains goes on across the opening pp. 40-41: each line of p. 40 is continued by the same line of p. 41 (frame 047), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. The page has no heading. The running numbers go on from entry 560/561 (frame 044) and run (٥٦٢ ٥٦٣)-(٥٩٤ ٥٩٥); every mountain has two numbers of Suhrāb, which run [١٠٦١]-[١٠٩٦] with two exceptions to their order: L02 has [١٠٦٥ ١٠٦٦] and L03 [١٠٦٣ ١٠٦٤], and L08 has four (below).
@obs L08: the bracket of the numbers of Suhrāb holds four numbers in two tiers within one pair of square brackets, «١٠٧٥ ١٠٧٦» above «١٠٨٧ ١٠٨٨»; transcribed «[١٠٧٥ ١٠٧٦ ١٠٨٧ ١٠٨٨]», the upper pair first, as on frame 012 (L01). The name «باليس» stands between angle brackets, followed by the note mark b: the editor supplies it (note b).
@obs L09, L11, L12: the name is the word «جبل» alone. L10: «جبل* الأسود», the Black Mountain: an asterisk stands after «جبل», raised like the editor's note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (introduction, frame 184, p. XXIX); «الأسود» with a hamza over the alif.
@obs L01: «ثلا», the three dots of the ث printed as one blot, 18 by 18 px and 198 px of ink, where a single dot of the page has some 60 to 80. L02: «لوراطيس», the ي with its two dots. L04: «غادبطن», with the dots of the غ, the ب and the ن.
@obs L03: «معرس الأسفل», the lower Maʿras, the ع without a dot and «الأسفل» with a hamza over the alif; then «جبل غانه», the غ and the ن with their dots, followed by the note mark a.
@obs L05: «الڡس»: after «ال», a loop with a counter, 14 by 17 px, without dots, joined on the line to a س: the loop of ف or ق, where the م of this font is a small filled loop (L15, L17). Transcribed الڡس.
@obs L06: «مارى» ends in a ى without dots. «أوّله», with a mark over the alif 15 by 13 px, the size and form of the hamza of «الأسفل» (L03, 15 by 14 px), where the madda over the alif of «وآخره» is a flat stroke 28 by 10 px (frame 044, L10); transcribed as a hamza. A shadda stands over the و. Frame 044 prints the same word without the hamza (L10, L12).
@obs L13: «جبل اهرد فى جزيرة سرنديب يقال له جبل الرهون», the mountain اهرد on the island of Sarandīb, which is called the mountain of al-Ruhūn: «اهرد» without dots; «فى» with the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى; «جزيرة» and «سرنديب» with all their dots.
@obs L14: «اورنا», the ن with its dot. L15: «مٮٮدروس»: after «جبل», a small filled loop, 17 by 20 px, the initial م; two low teeth without dots, 4 and 7 px high; a د, whose upright is 26 px; then «روس». The editor's note g gives the form of entries (1716), (1718) and (1784), «منيدروس», with the dots of the ن and the ي.
@obs L16: «هوسس»: after the و, six teeth before the bowl of the final س, the first 15 px high, as the first stroke of a س after a letter that does not join (frame 043, L07), and the others 8 to 12 px; transcribed هوسس. L17: «مصوں», the initial م a small filled loop; the final ن without its dot. L16, L17: «فى جزيرة الجوهر», on the island of the jewels.
@obs Specks, not transcribed: L04, two specks, 4 by 4 and 3 by 4 px, at mid-height between the ٥ and the ٦ of the first running number, much smaller than the zero of the numbers of Suhrāb, a dot 13 by 13 px; and a speck, 6 by 8 px, at the lower right of the ٩ of the second.
@obs The editor's footnotes of the opening run across both pages: they begin at the foot of p. 41 (frame 047) with notes a, b and c and the beginning of note d, and continue here with the end of note d, a correction of the manuscript in a numeral of p. 41, and notes e, f and g. The texts of notes a and b, whose marks stand on this page (L03, L08), are printed on p. 41.
