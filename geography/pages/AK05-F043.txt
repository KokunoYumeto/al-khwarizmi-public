@obs L06: «ارٮسه»: after «ار», a group 82 px wide: a tooth 15 px high without a dot, three low teeth of 5, 7 and 8 px and a final ه, 17 px wide. An initial س of this page has three teeth, the first of 8 to 10 px (L04, L05, L08); the tall stroke is therefore read as a tooth before the س. @unres L06: where a س begins a group after a letter that does not join, its first stroke can be as tall as 13-14 px (L07, «طاس», and the «اس» of «راسٮٮاس», L01), so that the four strokes after «ار» may also be read as a س followed by a tooth, ارسٮه. Transcribed ارٮسه.
