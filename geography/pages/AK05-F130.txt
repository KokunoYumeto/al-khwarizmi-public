@obs The spring in the mountain ٮويرٮورا goes on from p. 123 (frame 129), whose last words, «والثانية عند طول», L01 continues with «له ل والعرض ل م (١٨٧٥) *», the second of the two small lakes, at the longitude 35° 30′ and the latitude 30° 40′.
@obs L01-L02: «عين اوّلها عند طول لو {z} والعرض ل م (١٨٧٦) ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول له ك والعرض ل⟨ٮ⟩ {z} (١٨٧٧) *», a spring whose beginning is at the longitude 36° 0′ and the latitude 30° 40′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 35° 20′ and the latitude ل⟨ٮ⟩ ō. L02: the ل is printed as an upright, 43 px high, whose foot turns slightly to the left at the line before the opening bracket, where the alif of «والعرض» before it ends straight; the editor supplies the flat bowl of a ب, without its dot, between the brackets, followed by the note mark a, all under the line over the numerals: ل⟨ٮ⟩, 32.
@unres L02: the upright before the bracket may also be read as an alif: ا⟨ٮ⟩, which gives no numeral. Transcribed ل⟨ٮ⟩.
@obs L03-L06: an island with three springs. «عين فى جزيرة اوّلها عند طول مه ل والعرض له م (١٨٧٨) ومص⟨بّها فى البحر عند⟩ طول مه ن والعرض لو مه (١٨٧٩) *», a spring on an island whose beginning is at the longitude 45° 30′ and the latitude 35° 40′, and its mouth ⟨in the sea at⟩ the longitude 45° 50′ and the latitude 36° 45′; «عين فى هٰذه الجزيرة ايضاً اوّلها عند طول م⟨ه مه⟩ والعرض له ك (١٨٨٠) مصبّها فى البحر غند طول مو ك والعرض لو {z} (١٨٨١) *», a spring also on this island, whose beginning is at the longitude ⟨45° 45′⟩ and the latitude 35° 20′, its mouth in the sea at the longitude 46° 20′ and the latitude 36° 0′; «وعين فى هٰذه الجزيرة ايضاً اوّلها عند طول مه مه والعرض له {z} (١٨٨٢) ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول مو ل والعرض له ك (١٨٨٣) *», and a spring also on this island, at the longitude 45° 45′ and the latitude 35° 0′, its mouth in the sea at the longitude 46° 30′ and the latitude 35° 20′.
