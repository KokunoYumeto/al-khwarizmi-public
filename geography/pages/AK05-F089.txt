@obs The running text goes on from p. 82 (frame 088), whose last words, «يمرّ الى طول», L01 continues with the longitude «ڡعو مه»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٢٦٩)-(١٢٨٢), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٨٢٥]-[٨٣٨], without a gap.
@obs The places of the coast: L02: «هناك مصبّ نهر ويحدث فى هٰذا الموضع جزيرة يقال لها القلعة المضيئة», there is the mouth of a river, and at this place there arises an island called the Shining Castle; L04: «ويمرّ فيماسّ جبل جزيرة الياقوت والجواهر», and it passes and touches the mountain of the island of the Ruby and the Jewels; L05: «ويمرّ متّصل* بالجبل المحيط بجزيرة الجواهر مع الجبل حتّى يصير الى», and it passes joined to the mountain that surrounds the island of the Jewels, along the mountain, until it comes to; L11: «مصبّ نهر», the mouth of a river, and «على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān; L13-L14: «فيحدث هناك جزيرة يقال لها جزيرة الفضّة», and there arises an island called the island of Silver; L14: «ويمرّ بمصبّ نهر سواس ويصير الى موضعه الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول قسد {z} والعرض ى {z} خلف الاستواء», and it passes the mouth of the river سواس and comes to its place from which we began, which is at the longitude 164° 0′ and the latitude 0° 10′ beyond the equator: the first point of the Dark Sea, on p. 82 (frame 088, L02), and point 1147, as the editor's note i says; this last point has no running number. L15 ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk: the end of the section. Point 1280 (L11) is given by its latitude only, «يمرّ بعرض».
@obs The latitudes from point 1275 on are said to be «خلف خطّ الاستواء» (L06-L07, L09) or «خلف الاستواء» (L11-L13, L15), beyond the equator. L07-L08, L08 and L10: «⟨خلف خطّ الاستواء⟩», between angle brackets and followed by the note marks e (twice) and g: the editor supplies the words, which the manuscript lacks (notes e and g). «الاستواء» ends in an alif and a small hamza on the line.
@obs L05: «متّصل*»: an asterisk with six solid arms stands after it, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10).
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ», «ويمرّ», «فيماسّ», «مصبّ», «بمصبّ», «متّصل», «حتّى», «خطّ» and «الفضّة» with a shadda; «هٰذا» with a dagger alif above the ه; «الّذى» with a shadda over the ل; «ابتدأنا» with a hamza over the alif; «فى» and «حتّى» end in a ى without dots.
