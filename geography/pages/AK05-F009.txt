@obs L01: the basmala is written in calligraphy and printed from a block; hamza and shadda are transcribed where they are written, its other vowel signs are not, as on the title pages (frames 005 and 007). The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 2ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the left margin beside it.
@obs L02-L03: the head of the table, in a smaller type. Its columns, from the right: «(العدد)», the editor's running number in parentheses, and «[العدد كما تجده فى كتاب عجائب الأقاليم]», the number of the same entry in Suhrāb's ʿAjāʾib al-aqālīm in square brackets, both printed vertically, the second in two lines within square brackets; «اسماء المدن»; «الطول» and «العرض», each over two columns headed «درج» and «دقائق», degrees and minutes, printed vertically. On this page the column of the square brackets is empty. A rule stands above each pair of numeral cells of every entry, and above the cells of entries 1-6 also below them.
@obs L04: the heading of the section, «اسماء المدن الّتى خلف خطّ الاستواء», stands between two small open stars, transcribed as asterisks.
@obs The numerals of the table are written with letters, by their numerical values (abjad), the hundreds first: سه, 65; قنح, 158. The editor keeps the defective writing of the manuscript and corrects it only where dots stand in the wrong place or a number is impossible (introduction, frame 192, p. XXI). The loop of ف or ق printed without dots is transcribed ڡ (L06, L07, L10, L11, L12), and the final ب printed without its dot ٮ (L06, ڡكٮ); where the print dots the ق, it is transcribed ق (L08, قنح; L09, قسا). In the latitudes of L08 and L12 the print has ر; the editor states that in these numerals ر stands for 7 and not for 200 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed.
@obs The sign for zero is a small circle with a stroke above it, the form which, the editor says, the Strasbourg manuscript alone uses for the zero (introduction, frame 192, p. XXI); it is written {z} in the page files and set as a small circle with a bar in the PDF.
@obs L05: the longitude of entry 1, سه, and its minutes, the zero sign, are printed with a short line below them, under the س and the ه and under the circle of the zero sign; transcribed with the markup {u:…}. The pages of the introduction read so far do not say what the line marks.
@obs L06: «اعا», as printed: an alif, an ع joined to a final alif, without the tooth or the dot of a ن (the join rises 4 px above the baseline); the editor's note b refers to the name «اعنا» at entry (1496).
@obs L09: «قطيغوراا», as printed, with two alifs after the ر: the first stands 2 px from the ر, the second 14 px after the first; the editor's note e gives the name as «قطيغورا», with one alif, at entry (1726/1). The note mark f stands after the degrees of the longitude, قسا.
@obs L10: a small irregular mark of ink, 7 px wide and 14 px high, stands above the left end of the longitude ڡسد; it is not shaped like the dots of the font, and a dot there would make an impossible number; it is not transcribed.
@obs L12: the note mark i stands after the minutes of the latitude. In the footnotes the note i, the last line of the page, is printed without its mark; the editor's corrigenda (p. 163, frame 169) supply it.
@obs The editor's notes refer to other entries of the book that name the same places: افاطا at (1142), written there «راقاطا»; اعا at (1496), «اعنا»; القمر at (1496); برقايا at (1498), «برقانا»; قطيغوراا at (1726/1); مودير at [105], an entry of Suhrāb; سوسه at (67) and (1726/1); and the minutes of the latitude of entry 8 at (1726/1).
@obs Below the notes, at the left, the signature «1*» of the printed sheet; the page number «— ٣ —» stands at the foot, centred.
@variant L06: for القمر the manuscript reads القفر, as it does also at entry (1496).
@variant L09: for the name قطيغوراا the manuscript gives the variant reading ڡطعورا, with the loop of the ف or ق undotted; at entry (1726/1) the name is written قطيغورا, with the same variant.
@variant L09: the degrees of the longitude, قسا, are unclear in the manuscript.
