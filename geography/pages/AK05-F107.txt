@obs The islands of the sea of al-Baṣra end on p. 100 (frame 106). L01: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred: «* جزائر بحر جرجان وطبرستان *», the islands of the sea of Jurjān and Ṭabaristān, with a small open star with six points at each end; «جزائر» has a hamza on the tooth.
@obs L02-L06: four islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}: «جزيرة مقدار جزءين فى جزء ونصف» (L02), of two degrees by one and a half, the words «جزء ونصف» standing between two note marks a; «جزيرة مقدار جزءين فى جزء» (L03, L04), of two degrees by one; and «جزيرة مقدار جزءين فى ثلثى جزء» (L05), of two degrees by two thirds of a degree. Running numbers (١٥٤٤)-(١٥٤٧); the numbers of Suhrāb [١٠٢٢]-[١٠٢٤] stand after (١٥٤٤), (١٥٤٥) and (١٥٤٧); point 1546 has none. L06 is a short line, closed by a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk, like the stars between the islands.
@obs L07: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred: «* المواضع الّتى تكتب فيها حدود البلدان *», the places in which the boundaries of the countries are written, with a small open star with six points at each end; «الّتى» has a shadda over the ل.
@obs L08-L12: the countries, each with the longitude and latitude of the middle of its writing, «وسط الكتاب عند طول … والعرض …»: «بلاد نيفل الداخلة» (L08), the inner land of نيفل, at the latitude 5° 30′ beyond the equator; «غانه قوم يسمّون اغرمطين» (L09), غانه, a people called اغرمطين; «بلاد الدملى الّتى لا يكون فيها المطر» (L10), the land of الدملى, in which there is no rain; «بلاد ليبوانى الداخلة وسط كتابها» (L11), the inner land of ليبوانى, the middle of its writing; and «بلاد بمملكة البربر» (L12), the land in the kingdom of the Berbers, whose latitude follows on p. 102. Each entry ends with a small open star with six points. The running numbers run (١٥٤٨)-(١٥٥١), and the numbers of Suhrāb, after [١٠٢٤] of point 1547, are [٤٩٧]-[٥٠٠], after (١٥٤٨)-(١٥٥١), without a gap.
