@obs The table of the mountains goes on across the opening pp. 60-61: each line of p. 60 is continued by the same line of p. 61 (frame 067), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. L01-L03 are the last three entries of the sixth clime. The headings run across the opening: L04, «* الإقليم السابع», is continued on p. 61 by «عرضه مح *», the seventh clime and its latitude, 48; L05, «* الجبال الّتى فى», by «الإقليم السابع *», the mountains in the seventh clime; L13, «* خلف الإقليم السابع», by «الى عرض ثلثة وستّين *», beyond the seventh clime to the latitude of sixty-three; and L14, «* الجبال الّتى وراء», by «الإقليم السابع *», the mountains beyond the seventh clime. They are set in the large type, each beginning with a small open star, transcribed as an asterisk, and stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their ends on p. 61; transcribed as headings. L04, L13: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif; L05, L14: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل; L05: «فى», with the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى; L13: «خلف», with the dots of the خ and the ف; L14: «الجبال», printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ب and the alif, which is not transcribed, and «وراء», with a hamza after the alif.
@obs The running numbers go on from entry 869/870 (frame 064) and run (٨٧١ ٨٧٢)-(٨٩١ ٨٩٢). The numbers of Suhrāb are [١٣٥٧ ١٣٥٨]-[١٣٧٥ ١٣٧٦] (L01-L12) and [١٣٨١ ١٣٨٢] (L15); [١٣٧٧]-[١٣٨٠] do not appear on the page. The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 15ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside entry 877/878 (L06).
@obs L03: «السدّ بين جبلين ياجوج وماجوج», the dam between two mountains, Gog and Magog, with a shadda over the د of «السدّ»: the only entry of the page that does not begin with «جبل». L10, L11: the word «جبل» alone. L12: «سامولا فى بلاد ياجوج», Sāmūlā in the land of Gog, with the dots of «بلاد» and «ياجوج». L15: «مالى فى جزيرة», Mālī on an island; «مالى» ends in a ى without dots, with a speck, 3 by 5 px, below it. «فى» (L07, L12, L15) has the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى.
@obs L01: «قاسيا», the ق with its two dots and the ي with its two dots below; the note mark a follows.
@obs Loops without dots in the names. The medial ع of this type is wider than high (frame 054, L11, «المعدن»: 18 by 11 px), where the medial loop of ف or ق is narrower and taller (frame 064, L17, «اسفطافا», with the dot of the ف). L02: «اسعودر»: after the alif, the three teeth of a س (12, 9 and 8 px), a loop 17 px wide and 14 px high, then و, د and ر; transcribed ع. L07: «الڡس» and «ماڡلوٮ»: upright loops, 12 by 17 and 11 by 14 px, with a counter; transcribed ڡ; the final ب of «ماڡلوٮ» has no dot. L08: an upright loop, 12 by 14 px; transcribed ڡ. L09: a loop 18 px wide and 12 px high; transcribed ع.
@unres L02: the loop may also be read as the loop of ف or ق: اسڡودر. Transcribed اسعودر.
@unres L09: the loop may also be read as the loop of ف or ق: لٮٮڡوس. Transcribed لٮٮعوس.
@obs L06: «ٮورسس»: a tooth without a dot, 18 px high, joined to a و; a ر, rising 20 px above the line like the isolated ر of L02, its head split for 7 px into two strokes; and six teeth before the bowl of the final س. The notched ٮر, a tooth joined to a ر (frame 041, L06), rises only 12 px. Transcribed ٮورسس.
@obs L08: «سسٮٮڡا»: after «جبل», eight upright strokes before the loop, the first 10 px high and the others 5 to 8 px, then the alif; six of them are taken as the teeth of two س, as in the six teeth of «ٮورسس» (L06), whose first two strokes stand as close together (7 and 8 px) as the first two here. A speck of 9 px, much smaller than the dots of the font, stands above the joint of the loop.
@unres L08: the eight strokes before the loop are of nearly the same height, so the grouping into letters does not show: the name may also be read ٮسسٮڡا or ٮٮسسڡا. Transcribed سسٮٮڡا.
@obs L09: «لٮٮعوس»: after the ل, two teeth without dots, 6 and 7 px high and 16 px apart, the second 4 px from the loop with a dip to 3 px between them; then the loop, a و and a final س with three teeth. Transcribed as counted.
