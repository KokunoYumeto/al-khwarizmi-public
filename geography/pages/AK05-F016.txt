@obs L11: the second word of the name is printed «امق»: an alif, then an initial م joined to a final ق with its two dots, without a tooth between them; transcribed as printed. A short horizontal stroke crosses the left parenthesis of the running number «(١٠٨)», a flaw of the print.
@obs L14: an asterisk stands after واغلة, «اوموره واغلة* بين البحرين», raised like the editor's note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (introduction, frame 184, p. @obs L06: below the tooth of the degrees of the latitude, ٮو, stands a small dark speck, 5 px across, where the dots of the font are 10-11 px; it is not transcribed as a dot.
