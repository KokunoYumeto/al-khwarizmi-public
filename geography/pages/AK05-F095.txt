@obs The description of the island سقيديا goes on from p. 88 (frame 094), whose last words, «وتمرّ على صورة القوارة تمرّ بعرض س ل», are followed in L01 by their running number, (١٣٦٤), and the number of Suhrāb, [٩١٩]. L01-L02: «وتصير الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول مب ل والعرض ٮط م», and it comes to the place from which we began, which is at the longitude 42° 30′ and the latitude ٮط م: this last point has no running number; the editor's note a identifies it with point 1361, the first point of the island (frame 094, L14), whose latitude is printed «نط م» with the dot of the ن.
@obs L02-L05: «جزيرة امراتوس الّتى فيها الرجال اوّلها عند طول مط م والعرض سد مه», the island امراتوس, on which are the men; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 49° 40′ and the latitude 64° 45′ (point 1365); then «تمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», points 1366-1368, and «تمرّ الى الموضع الّذي منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول مط م والعرض سد مه», back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note f identifies it with point 1365.
@obs L06-L09: «جزيرة امرانوس الّتى فيها النساء اوّلها عند طول ن ل والعرض سا ى», the island امرانوس, on which are the women; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 50° 30′ and the latitude 61° 10′ (point 1369); then points 1370-1372, the last «على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān (L08); and «ثمّ تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول ن ل والعرض سا ى», then back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note g identifies it with point 1369. «امرانوس» has the dot of the ن, where «امراتوس» (L02) has the two dots of the ت.
@obs The running numbers run (١٣٦٤)-(١٣٧٥) without a gap; the numbers of Suhrāb, [٩١٩]-[٩٢٧], stand after (١٣٦٤)-(١٣٧٢), without a gap; islands 1373-1375 have none. Each of the two islands, and the island سقيديا, ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs L10-L11: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred on two lines: «* الجزائر الّتى فى بحر طنجة ومرطاس / وإفريقيّة وبرقة والشام *», the islands in the sea of Tangier, of مرطاس, of Ifrīqiya, of Barqa and of Syria. «ومرطاس» ends in an isolated س, a taller first tooth, two smaller teeth and the bowl, without dots; the note mark h follows it. «الّتى» has a shadda over the ل; «وإفريقيّة» a hamza below the alif and a shadda over the ي; «والشام» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ش and the alif, which is not transcribed; a small open star with six points stands at the beginning of the first line and at the end of the second.
@obs L12-L14: the first islands of the section, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …»: «جزيرة مدوّرة مقدار جزء» (L12), a round island of one degree; «جزيرة مقدار جزء ونصف فى جزء» (L12-L13), of one and a half degrees by one degree; «جزيرة مدوّرة مقدار جزء وربع» (L13-L14), a round island of one and a quarter degrees; each entry ends with a small open star with six points. L14 ends with «اربع», four, continued on p. 90.
