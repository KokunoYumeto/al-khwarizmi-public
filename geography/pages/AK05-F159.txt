@obs The river اسطولس goes on from p. 152 (frame 158), whose last words, «* نهر اسطولس من جبل سرطاميڡا», L01-L02 continue: «اوّله عند طول مج ه والعرض ٮٮ ه (٢٣٢٣) مصبّه فى البحر عند طول مز {z} والعرض نح ل (٢٣٢٤) *», its beginning is at the longitude 43° 5′ and the latitude ٮٮ ه, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 47° 0′ and the latitude 58° 30′. «مصبّه» stands without و. The mountain سرطاميڡا is entries 901-902 of the table of the mountains (frame 068, L05), whose boundaries are at 41° 45′ and 49° 30′ and at 44° 0′ and 51° 45′ (frame 069, L05).
@obs L02-L03: «نهر الڡسلس اوّله عند طول مح مه والعرض ن ى (٢٣٢٥) مصبّه فى البحر عند طول نا ل والعرض نا ن (٢٣٢٦) *», the river الڡسلس, whose beginning is at the longitude 48° 45′ and the latitude 50° 10′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 51° 30′ and the latitude 51° 50′.
@obs L02: «الڡسلس»: after «ال», an upright loop without dots, 14 px wide and 19 rows high, with a white counter, the form of the loop of ف or ق (frame 158, L10); the medial ع of «والعرض» in the same line is a solid body without a counter, 6 rows lower. Then the three teeth of a س, a ل and a final س.
@obs L04-L05: «نهر من جبل اوّله عند طول مر ه والعرض نه ك (٢٣٢٧) مصبّه فى البحر عند طول مط {z} والعرض نح ل (٢٣٢٨) *», a river from a mountain, whose beginning is at the longitude مر ه and the latitude 55° 20′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 49° 0′ and the latitude 58° 30′. The mountain is not named; (٢٣٢٧) and (٢٣٢٨) have no numbers of Suhrāb.
@obs L05-L07: «عين فى جزيرة ڡذٮا اوّلها عند طول مج ك والعرض نط ں (٢٣٢٩) ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول مو {z} والعرض ٮط م (٢٣٣٠) *», a spring on the island ڡذٮا, whose beginning is at the longitude 43° 20′ and the latitude 59° 50′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 46° 0′ and the latitude ٮط م. L07 holds only the number of Suhrāb and the asterisk, at the right.
@obs L05: «ڡذٮا»: an initial loop, 15 px wide, filled solid, without a dot of its own; a ذ with its dot; a tooth without a dot joined to the alif. The dot above the word stands over the ذ, not over the loop. The island is «سقيديا» of the outer western sea (frame 094, L14), whose coast begins at the longitude 42° 30′ and the latitude 59° 40′, where the editor's note l gives the form of this entry (2329), ڡذٮا.
@obs L08-L10: «عين تصير الى بطيحة راستاس اوّلها عند طول نط م والعرض ٮو ك (٢٣٣١) ومصبّها فى البطيحة عند طول نا {z} والعرض ند ى (٢٣٣٢) * ومقدار البطيحة نصف جزء ومركزها عند طول ٮا ل والعرض ٮح ں (٢٣٣٣) *», a spring that goes to the marsh راستاس, whose beginning is at the longitude 59° 40′ and the latitude ٮو ك, and its mouth in the marsh at the longitude 51° 0′ and the latitude 54° 10′; and the size of the marsh is half a degree, and its centre at the longitude ٮا ل and the latitude ٮح ں. «راستاس»: ر, ا, the three teeth of a س, the ت with its two dots, ا and the final س.
@obs L10-L12: «ويقع ايضاً الى هٰذه البطيحة عين اوّلها عند طول ٮب ك والعرض نه ه (٢٣٣٤) ومصبّها فى البطيحة عند طول نا ل والعرض ٮد ى (٢٣٣٥) *», and into this marsh falls also a spring whose beginning is at the longitude ٮب ك and the latitude 55° 5′, and its mouth in the marsh at the longitude 51° 30′ and the latitude ٮد ى.
@obs L12-L14: «يخرج من هٰذه البطيحة نهر يصبّ فى البحر ⟨اوّله⟩ عند طول ٮا ل والعرض نح ل (٢٣٣٦) ومصبّه ⟨فى⟩ البحر عند طول نا {z} والعرض ٮح م (٢٣٣٧) *», out of this marsh comes a river that flows into the sea; ⟨its beginning⟩ is at the longitude ٮا ل and the latitude 58° 30′, and its mouth ⟨in⟩ the sea at the longitude 51° 0′ and the latitude ٮح م.
@obs L15-L16: «يخرج من نهر هٰذه العين نهر اوّله عند طول نا ى والعرض نز ل (٢٣٣٨) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول نح {z} والعرض نط {z} (٢٣٣٩) *», out of the river of this spring comes a river whose beginning is at the longitude 51° 10′ and the latitude 57° 30′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 58° 0′ and the latitude 59° 0′.
