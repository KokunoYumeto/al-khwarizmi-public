@obs L02: the coordinates of entry 305, Nahāwand, stand within one pair of angle brackets, «⟨عد ō لو ō⟩», the opening bracket before the degrees of the longitude and the closing one after the minutes of the latitude, followed by the note mark a: the editor supplies the whole of them.
