@obs The Nile goes on from p. 107 (frame 113), whose last words, «فيمرّ النيل فيقطع خطّ الإقليم», L01 continues with «الأوّل عند طول ٮج {z} وعرض ٮو ك (١٦٣١)», and the Nile crosses the line of the first clime at the longitude ٮج {z} and the latitude ٮو ك; «ويمرّ حتّى يجوز خطّ اقليم* الأوّل بجزء ونصف على سمته (١٦٣٢)» (L01-L02), and it passes until it goes beyond the line of the first clime by one and a half degrees in its direction: point 1632, which has no coordinates. Then the course from point to point, «ثمّ يعدل الى طول … والعرض …», then it turns to the longitude … and the latitude …, points 1633-1637, with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets.
@obs L06-L09: «ثمّ يمرّ فيصير الى مدينة ملوى عند طول نا ل والعرض ٮط ك (١٦٣٨)», then it passes and comes to the town of Malwā at the longitude 51° 30′; «ثمّ يمرّ فيصير الى مدينة اسوان مماسّ* لها عند طول ٮه ل والعرض كب ل (١٦٣٩)», then to the town of Aswān, touching it, at the longitude ٮه ل and the latitude 22° 30′; «ثمّ يعدل ⟨الى طول⟩ ٮح {z} وعرض كد {z} (١٦٤٠) مماسّ* لجبل اليوڡا», then it turns to the longitude ٮح {z} and the latitude 24° 0′, touching the mountain اليوڡا; «ثمّ يعدل الى طول ٮه ل والعرض كد ك (١٦٤١)», then to the longitude ٮه ل and the latitude 24° 20′.
@obs L09-L11: «ثمّ يمرّ فيقع فى الإقليم الثالث يماسّ فى بعض المواضع جبل المقطّم والمدن عليه كما كتبنا من طولها وعرضها ثمّ يمرّ الى مصر مماسّ* لها عند طول ند ل والعرض كط يه (١٦٤٢)», then it passes and falls into the third clime, touching in some places the mountain al-Muqaṭṭam and the towns on it, as we have written of their longitude and latitude; then it passes to Miṣr, touching it, at the longitude 54° 30′ and the latitude 29° 15′.
@obs L11-L14: «ثمّ يفترق من مصر الى البحر فى خلجان سبعة *», then it divides from Miṣr to the sea into seven channels; «الأوّل منها يمرّ الى الإسكندريّة عند طول نا ل (١٦٤٣)», the first of them passes to Alexandria at the longitude 51° 30′; «ويخرج من هٰذا الخليج خليجان الأوّل منهما عند طول نا م (١٦٤٤) والآخر عند طول ٮح {z} (١٦٤٥)», and from this channel come two channels, the first of them at the longitude 51° 40′ and the other at the longitude ٮح {z}; «ومصبّ الخليج», and the mouth of the channel, continued on p. 109.
@obs The running numbers run (١٦٣١)-(١٦٤٥) and the numbers of Suhrāb [١٥٢٧]-[١٥٤١], each after its running number, without a gap; (١٦٤٣) and (١٦٤٤) stand at the beginning of L13 and L14. @obs Numerals: «ٮج» (L01), a tooth without its dot joined to a ج with the dot in its bowl, which read as a ن gives 53; «ٮو ك» (L01), a tooth without its dot joined to a و, which read with a ي gives 16° 20′; «نب {z}» (L02), with the dot of the ن and of the ب; «نا» (L03, L06, L12, L13), «ن» (L04, twice) and «ند» (L11), with the dot of the ن; «يز ل» (L04), with the two dots of the ي and the dot of the ز, 17° 30′; «كب ل» (L07), with the dot of the ب; «كط يه» (L11), with the two dots of the ي, 29° 15′. Without their dots, transcribed so: «ٮح» (L03, L05, the degrees of the latitude; L08, L14, the degrees of the longitude), «ٮط» (L05, L06, the degrees of the latitude), a tooth without its dot, which read with a ي give 18 and 19.
@obs L05, the degrees of the longitude, «ٮٮ ل»: a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, 62 px wide, with a tall riser and a small bump at the right end of the bowl, where the isolated ب is a bowl of about 50 px with a riser at each end (frame 113, L03, with its dot); read with a ن, 52° 30′, near the longitudes 50° 20′ (L04) and 51° 30′ (L06).
@obs L07 and L09, the degrees of the longitude, «ٮه»: a short upright without a dot, joined at its foot to the loop of a final ه, the same form as «نه» of frame 113 (L08), whose upright has the dot of the ن above it, where the isolated ه, 5, of these numerals is a small round ring (frame 112, L02); transcribed ٮه, which read with a ن gives 55° 30′, beside the longitude 54° 30′ of Miṣr (L11) and the longitude of Aswān in the table of the cities, «ٮو {z}», 56° 0′ read with a ن (frame 014, L14).
