@obs The islands of the sea of al-Qulzum go on from p. 93 (frame 099), whose last words, «جزيرة مقدار», L01 continues with «نصف جزء»: single islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٤٤٠)-(١٤٤٨): of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L01, L06, L08); of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L01, L03); of a third of a degree, «ثلث جزء» (L02, L07); of one degree, «جزء» (L04); and of two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L05). Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs L10-L11: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred on two lines: «* ⟨الجزائر الّتى فى⟩ البحر الأخضر / والسند والهند والصين *», the islands in the Green Sea and of Sind, India and China. The words «الجزائر الّتى فى» stand between angle brackets, followed by the note mark d: the manuscript has a gap there, and the editor supplies them (note d). The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 26ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type at the beginning of L10, before the star; transcribed as a margin note before the line. «الأخضر» has a hamza over the alif and «الّتى» a shadda over the ل; «والصين» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ي and the ن, which is not transcribed.
@obs L12-L16: the islands of the Green Sea: single islands of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L12, L15), half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L13), and two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L14), running numbers (١٤٤٩)-(١٤٥٢); then «جزيرة اوّلها عند طول عد ل والعرض ح ك (١٤٥٣)» (L15-L16), an island without a name and without a size, the first point of whose coast is at the longitude 74° 30′ and the latitude ح ك; then «تمرّ الى طول عر ō والعرض ٮا ō», continued on p. 95.
@obs L05: «⟨وسطها⟩», between angle brackets, followed by the note mark b: the manuscript lacks the word, and the editor supplies it (note b). Transcribed with the brackets.
@obs The running numbers run (١٤٤٠)-(١٤٥٣) without a gap. The numbers of Suhrāb, [٩٦٤]-[٩٦٩], stand after (١٤٤٢)-(١٤٤٥), (١٤٤٧) and (١٤٤٨), and [٩٧١]-[٩٧٣] after (١٤٥٠), (١٤٥١) and (١٤٥٣); [٩٧٠] stands after (١٤٣٩) on p. 93 (frame 099, L16). Points 1440, 1441, 1446, 1449 and 1452 have none.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «اوّلها» (L15) with a shadda over the و; «تمرّ» (L16) with a shadda; «جزء» with a hamza on the line after the ز; «ثلثى» ends in a ى without dots.
@obs Numerals: «ير» (L01, the degrees of the latitude), the ي with its two dots below joined to a ر without a dot, which with ر read as ز gives 17° 40′; «يح» (L02, L06, L07, the degrees of the latitude), with the two dots of the ي; «نح» (L02), «ن» (L02, L06, the minutes), with the dot of the ن; «سز» (L13), with the dot of the ز, 67. Letters without their dots, transcribed so: «سٮ» (L04, L07, L09, the degrees of the longitude), the flat final bowl of the ب without its dot, 62; «ں» (L05, the minutes of the latitude), the bowl of a final ن without its dot, 50; «ٮا» (L16, the degrees of the second latitude), a tooth without its dot joined to an alif, which read with a ي gives 11.
