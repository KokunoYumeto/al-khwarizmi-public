@obs The leaf, on the grey mottled paper of the reprint title page (frame 005), reproduces by offset the German title page of 1926 (frame 210), line for line; it is the reprint's title leaf at the end of the volume, which opens from the right. Transcribed as frame 210. The title page is set in Roman capitals, with three lines in black letter (Fraktur), transcribed in Roman type: «Herausgegeben» (L05), «Mit fünf Tafeln in Lichtdruck» (L11) and «Otto Harrassowitz · Leipzig» (L13). The transliterated names keep the diacritics of the print: the dots below Ṣ, Ḍ and Ḥ, the macrons, the haček of Ǧ and the breve below Ḫ of «AL-ḪUWĀRIZMĪ», the half ring of «ǦAʿFAR». The shelfmark of the manuscript, «COD. 4247», stands between two thin slanting strokes, transcribed as slashes; its figures are old-style. A rule separates the date, «MCMXXVI», 1926, from the publisher's line.
