@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 40-41: each of its lines continues the same line of p. 40 (frame 046), L01-L17, entries 562/563-594/595. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads.
@obs The colours are «اصفر», yellow (L01, L14, L15, L16); «وردىّ», rose (L02, L07); «لازورد», lapis blue (L03, L04, L17); «حديدىّ», iron-coloured (L05); «زيتىّ», olive (L06); «مبيّض», whitish (L08); «ازرق», blue (L09); «اسود», black (L10); «لكّىّ», lac red (L11); and «احمر», red (L12, L13). The directions are «مغربىّ», western (L01, L03, L08, L10, L16), «جنوب», south (L02, L04, L07), and «شمال», north (L05, L06, L09, L11, L13, L14, L15, L17); L12 prints «مغرٮىّ», the ب without its dot, where L01 and the others print it with the dot below.
@obs Signs in the colours and directions, transcribed as printed: «وردىّ», «حديدىّ», «زيتىّ» and «مغربىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it; «مبيّض», with a shadda over the ي; «لكّىّ», with a shadda over the ك and another over the final ى. «زيتىّ» has the dot of the ز, the two dots of the ي below and the two of the ت above; «مبيّض» the dots of the ب, the ي and the ض.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: ٮا (L01, the degrees of the first latitude; L11, the degrees of the last latitude), a tooth joined to an alif, 11, as on frame 045 (L11); ٮٮ (L03, L07, L08, L10, the degrees of the last latitude), a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, 61 to 69 px wide, with a tall riser and a small bump at the right end of the bowl, which read with a ي give 12; ٮ (L13, the degrees of the last latitude; L05, the degrees of the first latitude), the isolated ب without its dot, a bowl of 49 to 50 px with a riser at each end, 2; سٮ (L05, the degrees of the first longitude), 62; ٮح (L06, the degrees of both latitudes; L07, the degrees of the first latitude), 18; ٮط (L12, L14, the degrees of the first latitude), 19; ٮو (L15, L17, the degrees of the last latitude), 16; the loop of ف or ق without dots in the degrees of the longitude: ڡمٮ (L15, the first), with a م and a final ب without its dot, 142; ڡمر (L15, the last), 147; ڡعه (L16, both; L17, the first), 175; ڡعر (L17, the last), 177; ں (L15, the minutes of the last longitude), the bowl of a final ن without its dot, 50, round and deep like the ن of the minutes of the last latitude of the same row, where the bowl of the ب is long and flat (L13).
