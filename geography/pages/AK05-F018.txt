@obs The table goes on from frame 017 without a heading: the cities of the third clime, entries 129-147. Entries 129-131 and 143-147 are printed without the word «مدينة»; entries 143-147 have no numbers of Suhrāb. The numbers of Suhrāb run [١٠٨]-[١١٦] in entries 129-137, then [١١٨], [١١٩], [١١٧] in entries 138-140 and [١٢١], [١٢٢] in entries 141-142; transcribed as printed. The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 4ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside entry 147 (L19).
@obs L01: the letter before the final ى of «اسطمى» is a small loop with a counter, raised a little above the line and joined below to the bowl of the ى, the build of the م in «صلمى» (frame 014, L01); the medial ق of «شرقه» (L02) stands higher above the line. Transcribed م.
@obs L02: «على شرقه الجبل», as printed: the ق with its two dots and a final ه. L04: «حامّة», with a shadda over the م. L06: the first letter of «ڡاسا», the initial loop of ف or ق joined to the alif, is printed without dots. L08: «طاووسن», with two و. L07: «ٮرباريا»: the first letter is a tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, where the isolated ر of the font is a plain curved stroke (frame 021, L08, «عارٮرا»); transcribed ٮر. The ب after it has its dot.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: ٮٮ (L09, the degrees of the longitude), a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, with two risers at the right end of the bowl where the isolated ب has one; ٮد (L11), ٮه (L13-L15) and ٮو (L16), the tooth without its dot, in the degrees of the longitude; ں (L13, L14), the bowl of a final ن without its dot, in the minutes of the latitude. With their dots: كز (L02, the longitude; L09 and L18, the latitude), ند (L10), نج (L12), ن (L08; L16, the minutes of the longitude), نو (L17) and نز (L18), and يه (L11, the minutes of the longitude).
@obs L10, L15, L16: the degrees of the latitude are printed كر, with ر, and L19: the degrees of the longitude, ٮر, a tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, where the isolated ر of the font is a plain curved stroke (frame 021, L09); in these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p.
