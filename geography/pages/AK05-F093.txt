@obs The description of the island اوليا goes on from p. 86 (frame 092), whose last word, «وتصير», L01 continues with «الى طول»: its coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets. The places of the coast: «وتمرّ بمصبّ نهر» (L02, L06), past the mouth of a river; «وتمرّ بمصبّ نهرين» (L03), of two rivers; L05-L06: «ثمّ تمرّ الى مدينة الوى الكبيرة عند طول», then it passes to the great town of الوى, at the longitude; L08: «وتمرّ على صورة الطيلسان الى مصبّ نهر», along the shape of the ṭaylasān to the mouth of a river; L09: «وتمرّ على صورة القوارة تمرّ بعرض», along the shape of the qawwāra, at the latitude (point 1339, given by its latitude only); L10-L11: «مصبّ نهر تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول يج ل والعرض نا ل», the mouth of a river; it passes to the place from which we began, which is at the longitude 13° 30′ and the latitude 51° 30′. This last point has no running number; the editor's note d identifies it with point 1312, the first point of the island (frame 092, L05), whose longitude is printed «ٮح ل», a tooth without a dot and a ح, where here «يج» has the two dots of the ي and the dot of the ج.
@obs L11-L14: three small islands, each «جزيرة مقدار …», of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L12), a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L12), and a third of a degree, «ثلث جزء» (L13), with the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٣٤١)-(١٣٤٣), without numbers of Suhrāb; each entry ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk. L14-L16: «جزيرة ٮولى فيها مدينة اوّلها عند طول كو ك والعرض سج {z}», the island ٮولى, on which is a town; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 26° 20′ and the latitude 63° 0′; then «تمرّ على صورة القوارة بعرض سب {z}» (L15), along the shape of the qawwāra at the latitude 62° 0′, and the coast goes on to p. 88. «ٮولى» begins with a tooth without a dot and ends in a ى without dots; the editor's note g gives the forms ثولى and اثلى of other entries.
@obs The running numbers run (١٣٢٨)-(١٣٤٦), and the numbers of Suhrāb [٨٩٨]-[٩١٠] and [٩١١]-[٩١٣], without a gap. L07: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 23ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between [٩٠٦] and «وتصير»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «تمرّ», «وتمرّ», «بمصبّ», «مصبّ» and «ثمّ» with a shadda; «الّذى» with a shadda over the ل; «ابتدأنا» with a hamza over the alif; «اوّلها» with a shadda over the و; «الوى», «الّذى» and «ٮولى» end in a ى without dots.
@obs L13, L14: the degrees of the latitude are printed with the notched form ٮر of frame 041 (L06), a short tooth joined to a ر, whose heads form a notch at the top over the tail of the ر, where the isolated ز of the numerals is a single rounded stroke (frame 089, L08; frame 090, L03, L13); and one dot above the notch. L13: the tooth, at the right, rises 4 px above the head of the ر, and the dot, 10 by 11 px, stands over the tooth; transcribed نر, which with ر read as ز gives 57. L14: the dot stands over the left side of the notch, nearer the head of the ر than the tooth; transcribed ٮز, which read with ن gives 57. The latitudes of the neighbouring islands are 58° 10′ (L12) and 63° 0′ (L15). L09 prints «نز» with both dots, the ن's over the tooth and the ز's over the head.
