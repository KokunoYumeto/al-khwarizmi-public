@obs The table of the mountains beyond the seventh clime goes on across the opening pp. 64-65, without a heading: each line of p. 64 is continued by the same line of p. 65 (frame 071), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. The running numbers go on from entry 927/928 (frame 068) and run (٩٢٩ ٩٣٠)-(٩٦٥ ٩٦٦); the numbers of Suhrāb run [١٤١٩ ١٤٢٠]-[١٤٥٥ ١٤٥٦] without a gap. The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 16ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside entry 963/964 (L18).
@obs L01, L03, L11: the word «جبل» alone. L04: «جبل يجىء من اوّل هٰذا الجبل», a mountain that comes from the beginning of this mountain: «يجىء» with the dots of the ي and of the ج, a ى without dots and a hamza on the line after it; «اوّل» with a shadda over the و; «هٰذا» with a dagger alif over the ه. L09: «جبل متّصل بهٰذا الجبل», a mountain joined to this mountain, as on p. 62 (frame 068, L14). L06: «جبل الان», the mountain of the Alans. L16: «جبل ياجوج المحيط اوّله من الصغد», the mountain of Gog, the surrounding one, whose beginning is from al-Sughd, «اوّله» with a shadda over the و. L17: «جبل ماجوج وهو مظلم موصول بالجبل المحيط», the mountain of Magog, which is dark, joined to the surrounding mountain. L18: «جبل حسدس فى بلاد ياجوج» and L19: «جبل دورالس فى بلاد ياجوج», mountains in the land of Gog; «فى» has the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى.
@obs L10: «انازاٮا»: the tooth before the first alif has the dot of a ن, and the tooth before the final alif has none. L13: «لناتيتى», with the dots of the ن, of the two ت and of the ي, ends in a ى without dots. L02: «ارسانا», L05: «اسقاسيا», L08: «سونيا», L12: «اورقيون», L14: «اسمريا» and L15: «ارانيا», with all their dots.
@obs L07: «طفورا»: after the ط, an upright loop, 13 px wide and 15 px high, with a white counter and a dot above it; transcribed ف. In the form of entry (2239) that the editor's note f gives, the letter after the ط is lower and wider, 17 by 14 px, with a dot above: the medial غ.
@obs Marks not transcribed: L08, a short raised stroke, 23 by 8 px, between the two numbers of Suhrāb; L09, a dot of 5 by 5 px at mid-height between the two running numbers, smaller than the dots of the font.
@obs The editor's footnotes of the opening begin at the foot of p. 65 (frame 071) with notes a-c and the beginning of note d, and continue here in three lines with the end of note d and notes e-h. The texts of notes a and c, whose marks stand on this page (L02, L05), are printed on p. 65; the marks of notes d, e, g and h stand on p. 65 (frame 071, L05, L06, L08, L16). In note c, a tick of 5 by 4 px stands above the loop of the second form; it is smaller than a dot and is not taken as one.
@obs The page number «— ٦٤ —» stands at the foot, centred.
@variant L02: at entry (2401) the name is written ارساٮا, the tooth before the final alif without the dot of the ن (note a, printed at the foot of p. 65).
@variant L05: at entry (2227) the name is written اسقاسا, and at entries (2229) and (2231) اسڡاسا, with a loop without dots (note c, printed at the foot of p. 65).
@variant L07: at entry (2239) the name is written طغورا (note f).
