@obs The running text goes on from p. 72 (frame 078), whose last latitude, «لو ى», is that of point 1099: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٠٩٩)-(١١١٧), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٦٨٥]-[٧٠٣], without a gap. L12: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 18ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «بأسفل» and «مدينة»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs The places of the coast: L03: «مماسّ* لمدينة», touching the town, whose name is not given; L06: «الٔى اسفل مدينة ٮٮادر», to below the town of ٮٮادر; L07: «مصبّ نهر ويرجع», the mouth of a river, and it returns; L10: «يمرّ على ص⟨ورة⟩ القوارة», along the shape of the qawwāra; L11: «ويضرب الإقليم الخامس ويمرّ الى اسفل رومية الكبيرة وينتهى الى طول», it extends into the fifth clime, passes below Rome the Great and ends at the longitude; L12: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة اسطانا», below the town of اسطانا; L13-L14: «مماسّ* لجبل اروس», touching the mountain اروس; L14-L15: «متّصل* ⟨ب⟩جبل هيكل الزهرة», joined to the mountain of the temple of Venus; L15: «متّصل* بجبل اٮدواقرا», joined to the mountain اٮدواقرا. The shapes of the coast are «القوارة» (L02, L08, L10) and «الطيلسان» (L04, L06).
@obs L03, L13: «مماسّ*», and L14, L15: «متّصل*»: an asterisk stands after the word, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10). «مماسّ» has a shadda over the س, «متّصل» over the ت.
@obs L06, L15: «الٔى»: a hamza stands above the ل, 15 by 16 px and 18 px to the left of the head of the alif, where the other «الى» of the page have none; transcribed with the hamza above the ل, as printed.
@obs L03, L04, L05 (twice), L06, L10: the latitude is introduced by «العرض», without the و of «والعرض», which the other points of the page have. Transcribed as printed.
@obs Angle brackets, transcribed as printed: L08: «⟨ويمرّ⟩», followed by the note mark n, and L14: «⟨ب⟩», followed by the note mark t and joined to «جبل»: the editor supplies the words, which the manuscript lacks (notes n and t); L10: «ص⟨ورة⟩», followed by the note mark p: the editor supplies the end of the word where the manuscript has a gap (note p).
