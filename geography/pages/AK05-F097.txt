@obs The description of the island ڡلڡوسس goes on from p. 90 (frame 096), whose last words, «جزيرة ڡلڡوسس فيها مدينة», L01 continues with «اوّلها عند طول لح مه والعرض له ل (١٣٩٣)», the first point of its coast, at the longitude لح مه and the latitude 35° 30′; then «تمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», points 1394-1396, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}; L03-L04: «ثمّ تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا عند طول لح مه والعرض له ل», then it passes to the place from which we began, at the longitude لح مه and the latitude 35° 30′, without «وهو» before «عند» and without a running number; the editor's note e identifies it with point 1393.
@obs L04-L14: single islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٣٩٧)-(١٤٠٧): of two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L04, L10); of one and a quarter degrees, «جزء وربع» (L05, L06); of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L07); of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L08, L09, and L10-L11, where the words stand between the brackets); and of two degrees by one degree, «جزءين فى جزء» (L11-L14), the islands «قرينوس» (L11), «شامس» (L12) and «قوقارا» (L14). L15: «جزيرة فيها مدينة اوّلها عند طول مه ں والعرض لد {z}», an island without a name, on which is a town; the first point of its coast is at the longitude مه ں and the latitude 34° 0′, and the coast goes on to p. 92. Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
