@obs The table goes on from frame 036 without a heading: the cities of the sixth clime, entries 445-458. All have numbers of Suhrāb: [٣٩١]-[٣٩٩] (entries 445-453), at entry 454 two numbers in one pair of square brackets, «٣٨١» above «٤٠٣», transcribed «[٣٨١ ٤٠٣]», as on frame 031, and [٤١٥]-[٤١٨] (entries 455-458); [٣٨١] is the number that does not appear on frame 036. No entry has the word «مدينة».
@obs L03: «دواٮسس»: after the alif, a tooth 14 px above the line without a dot, then two groups of three low teeth, 3 to 6 px, the second running into the final bowl. The tooth is taller than the teeth of س, and the teeth that follow it are lower than those of an initial س, which are 10, 9 and 6 px in «اسقوقو» (L02) and «ماسوريا» (L14); transcribed دواٮسس. The note mark b stands lower than the other marks, at the height of the letters.
@obs L05: «اوور»: after the alif, two و, the first with a short tail that ends at the line, the second with the long tail of the letter, then the ر; each و has the closed loop of the letter, which a د has not (L01, L03). Transcribed اوور.
@obs L10: «ايطنيا»: a small round dot, 5 by 5 px, stands just above the top of the alif, at its left; it has not the form of a hamza, which in this font is some 15 by 14 px (frame 028, L08, «الأهواز»). Not transcribed.
@obs L13: «سٮسمانى»: an initial س, with teeth of 13, 10 and 8 px; a tooth 18 px above the line without a dot; a second س with low teeth; م, ا, a ن with its dot and a final ى without dots. Transcribed so.
