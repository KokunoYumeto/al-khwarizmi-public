@obs L08, L09: «ڡرعامس» and «ڡورنا» begin with a loop raised above the line and joined by a stroke on the line to the next letter: the loop of ف or ق without dots, as «ڡرقا» on frame 023 (L01); in «ڡرعامس» the next letter is a ر, with the small head of the letter (9 px), in «ڡورنا» a و, with its loop (14 px), followed by a separate ر. An isolated و does not join the next letter. Transcribed ڡر and ڡو.
@obs L17: «هنزيط»: after the initial ه, a tooth with a dot above it, then a second upright with a dot above it that runs at its foot into the descending tail of a ز, then the ي with its two dots below and the ط; the two dots stand side by side, each above one of the uprights (centred 3 and 2 px from them). Transcribed هنزيط. The editor's note d gives the readings of the manuscript.
@obs L12: the degrees of the longitude are printed «ن⟨ه⟩»: an initial ن, a tooth with its dot above, standing on the line outside the angle brackets, and the ه supplied by the editor between them (note c: the manuscript is unclear), followed by the note mark c; transcribed so.
