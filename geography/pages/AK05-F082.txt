L12: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 19ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «والعرض» and the latitude «ا ى»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs L01-L02: point 1152 has the latitude «{z} {z}», 0° 0′, and lies «الى خطّ الاستوى*», on the equator. L02-L03: a remark between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks: «* ثمّ يصير العرض الّذى نذكر⟨ه⟩ الآن على مالم نزل نذكره من اوّل الكتاب *», then the latitude that we give now becomes as we have given it from the beginning of the book, that is, north of the equator again (compare the remark of p. 75, frame 081, L10-L11). «مالم» is printed as one word. L02: «الاستوى*»: an asterisk with six solid arms, the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language (frame 046, L10).
@obs The places of the coast: L03: «فيمرّ من هناك على صورة الطيلسان», from there it passes along the shape of the ṭaylasān; L08: «يمرّ بمدينة اسفتر وهى مدينة الرفأ», past the town of اسفتر, which is the town of al-Rafaʾ; L10: «بمدينة فرداثرا ومصبّ نهر دوايس», by the town of فرداثرا and the mouth of the river دوايس; L11: «ويمرّ بمصبّ نهر سالس», past the mouth of the river سالس; L13: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة سمردن», below the town of سمردن; L14: «بمصبّ نهر حوساں», at the mouth of the river حوساں.
