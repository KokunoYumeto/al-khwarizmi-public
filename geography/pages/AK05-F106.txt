@obs The islands in the Green Sea go on from p. 99 (frame 105), whose last word, «جزيرة», L01 continues with «الفضّة اوّلها من طول قند ك والعرض ه م خلف الاستواء (١٥٣٠)», the island of silver, the first point of whose coast is at the longitude قند ك and the latitude 5° 40′ beyond the equator; here «من طول» stands where the other islands have «عند طول». Then «تمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», points 1531-1533, each beyond the equator, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}; and L04-L05 «تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه بدأ⟨نا⟩ وهو عند طول ڡٮد ك والعرض ه م خلف الاستواء *», back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note b identifies it with point 1530. Here the verb is «بدأ⟨نا⟩», where the other islands have «ابتدأنا».
@obs L06: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred: «* الجزائر الّتى فى بحر البصرة *», the islands in the sea of al-Baṣra, with a small open star with six points at each end. «الجزائر» has a hamza on the tooth and «الّتى» a shadda over the ل.
@obs L07-L15: the islands of the sea of al-Baṣra, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …»: islands of one degree, «جزيرة مقدار جزء» (L07, L07-L08, L08-L09, L09-L10, L12, L13), points 1534-1537, 1539 and 1540; an island of two thirds of a degree, «⟨ثلثى جزء⟩» (L10-L11), point 1538; and «ثلٰث جزائر مقدار جزء جزء فيما بين طول ڡد {z} (١٥٤١) الى طول ڡر ل (١٥٤٢) وسط كلّها عند طول ڡه مه والعرض كو {z} (١٥٤٣)» (L14-L15), three islands of one degree by one degree, between the longitude ڡد {z} and the longitude ڡر ل, the middle of all of them at the longitude ڡه مه and the latitude 26° 0′. Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs The running numbers run (١٥٣٠)-(١٥٤٣) without a gap; the numbers of Suhrāb, [١٠١٢]-[١٠٢١], stand after (١٥٣٠)-(١٥٣٣) and (١٥٣٥)-(١٥٤٠); points 1534 and 1541-1543 have none. L11: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 28ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «العرض» and the numerals «كد ك»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs Angle brackets, each followed by a note mark: «بدأ⟨نا⟩» (L04), the نا that the manuscript lacks (note a); «⟨والعرض⟩» (L10), supplied in a gap of the manuscript (note d); and «⟨ثلثى جزء وسطها عند طول ڡا {z} و⟩العرض» (L10-L11): the bracket opens before «ثلثى» at the end of L10 and closes after the و of «والعرض» in L11: the manuscript has a gap there with the remains of letters, and the editor supplies the words (note f); within the brackets the note mark e follows the longitude. Transcribed with the brackets.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «الفضّة» (L01) with a shadda over the ض; «اوّلها» (L01) with a shadda over the و; «تمرّ» and «كلّها» (L15) with a shadda; «الّذى» (L04) with a shadda over the ل; «بدأ» (L04) with a hamza over the alif; «ثلٰث» (L14) with a dagger alif above the ل, printed thin, 4 by 8 px, where that of frame 104 (L11) is 7 by 13 px; «جزء» and «الاستواء» with a hamza; «جزائر» with a hamza on the tooth; «الّذى», «فى» and «ثلثى» end in a ى without dots.
@obs The longitudes of the island of silver: L01, «قند», the ق with its two dots, a short tooth with the dot of the ن above it, and a د, 154; L02 and L05, the same form without dots: the loop of ف or ق, a short tooth low in the joining stroke, and a د, transcribed «ڡٮد»; it is longer than «ڡد» of L14 by the tooth, 60 and 61 px against 45 px, as «قند» of L01 is 65 px. Read with ق and ن, «ڡٮد» gives 154, and read with ق and ي, 114. The other two points of the island, «ڡٮط ل» (L02, L03): between the loop and the ط, a short tooth without a dot that rises above the joining stroke, with no part below it, where the medial م of these numerals is a diamond that stands out above and below the stroke (frame 013, L13, «ڡمح») or is set under the ق (frame 082, L16, «قمط»); they measure 63 and 61 px from the stem of the ط to the end of the loop, where the compact «قط» of frame 102 (L06) measures 48 px. Read with ق and ن they give 159° 30′, so that the coast of the island runs from 154° to 159° 30′. The editor's notes do not comment on these longitudes.
@unres L02, L05: the joining stroke between the loop and the د may also be a stretched join without a tooth: ڡد, 104 read with ق. Transcribed ڡٮد.
