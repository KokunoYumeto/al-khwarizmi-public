@obs The places in which the boundaries of the countries are written go on from p. 102 (frame 108), whose last words, «بلاد هراة وسطها عند طول ڡو {z} والعرض له ن», L01 continues with their running number, (١٥٦٧), and the number of Suhrāb, [٥١٦]. Then the countries, each with the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}: «بلاد الأندلس» (L01), al-Andalus; «بلاد اڡرس» (L02); «بلاد لوقيه» (L03); «بلاد ڡٮلٮقٮه» (L03-L04); «بلاد الموصل» (L04-L05), al-Mawṣil; «بلاد لسطانا» (L05-L06); «بلاد اسڡانيه» (L06-L07); «بلاد تيرسنا» (L07-L08); «بلاد افليقيه» (L08); «بلاد ماقدونيه» (L09), Macedonia; «بلاد موسيا الفوقانيّة» (L10), Upper Moesia; «بلاد موسيا السفليّة» (L11), Lower Moesia; and «بلاد برقه» (L12). Each country ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk; the page ends with the star after [٥٢٩].
@obs The running numbers run (١٥٦٧)-(١٥٨٠) and the numbers of Suhrāb [٥١٦]-[٥٢٩], each after its running number, without a gap; [٥١٧], [٥٢٥]-[٥٢٨] stand at the beginning of their lines.
@obs Names printed without dots, transcribed so: «اڡرس» (L02), an alif, the loop of ف or ق without dots joined by a short stroke to a ر, and a س; «اسڡانيه» (L06), the loop without dots, where the ن has its dot and the ي its two dots below. L03: «ڡٮلٮقٮه»: a loop without dots, a short tooth, a ل, a very low second tooth, 4 to 6 px above the joining stroke, a ق with its two dots, a low third tooth and a final ه; the teeth have no dots. With their dots: «الأندلس» (L01), with a hamza over the alif; «لوقيه» (L03), «تيرسنا» (L07), «افليقيه» (L08), «ماقدونيه» (L09), «موسيا» (L10, L11) and «برقه» (L12). «الفوقانيّة» (L10) and «السفليّة» (L11) have a shadda over the ي.
@unres L03: the low rise between the ل and the ق may also be the joining stroke without a tooth: ڡٮلقٮه. Transcribed ڡٮلٮقٮه.
@obs Numerals printed without dots, transcribed so: «ٮد» (L03, the degrees of the longitude), a tooth without its dot joined to a د, 54 read with a ن, 14 read with a ي; «مٮ» (L09, the degrees of the longitude), the flat final bowl of a ب without its dot, 42, where L07 and L12 print «مب» with the dot. With their dots: «لز» (L01, L04), «نز» (L04), with a dot over the tooth and another over the head of the ز, 57; «ن» (L05, L08, the minutes), with the dot of the ن; «مج» (L09), with the dot in the bowl of the ج, 43.
@obs In these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed: «مر» (L12, the degrees of the longitude), 47, a ر with a single rounded head joined to the م.
@obs The ح of the numerals, «لح» (L02, L03, L05), «سح» (L05), «ح» (L06, twice) and «مح» (L07), shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3, which L09 prints with its dot in «مج». Transcribed as printed. The isolated ى, 10 (L01), has no dots. The isolated ك, 20, is printed with the small inner stroke of the letter (L06, L07).
@obs Note marks: L01, a after the latitude; L02, b after «اڡرس», c after the longitude and d after the latitude; L03, e after «لوقيه»; L04, f after the longitude; L05, g after the latitude; L08, h after the longitude; L09, i after «ماقدونيه» and k after the longitude; L10, l after the latitude; L12, m after «برقه».
