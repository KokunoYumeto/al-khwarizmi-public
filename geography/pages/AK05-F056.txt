@obs The table of the mountains goes on across the opening pp. 50-51: each line of p. 50 is continued by the same line of p. 51 (frame 057), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. L01-L03 give the last three mountains of the third clime, entries 721/722-725/726. The headings run across the opening: L04, «* الإقليم الرابع», is continued on p. 51 by «عرضه لو *», the fourth clime and its latitude, 36; and L05, «* الجبال الّتى فى», by «الإقليم الرابع *», the mountains in the fourth clime. They are set in the large type, each beginning with a small open star, transcribed as an asterisk, and stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their ends on p. 51; transcribed as headings. L04: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif; L05: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل, and «فى», with the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى.
@obs The running numbers go on from entry 719/720 (frame 054) and run (٧٢١ ٧٢٢)-(٧٤٧ ٧٤٨). The numbers of Suhrāb are [١٢١٥ ١٢١٦] and [١٢١٧ ١٢١٨] (L01, L02); [١١٩١ ١١٩٢] and [١١٩٣ ١١٩٤] (L03, L06), the two pairs that do not appear on frame 054; and [١٢١٩ ١٢٢٠]-[١٢٣٧ ١٢٣٨] (L07-L16). The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 13ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside the heading of the fourth clime (L04).
@obs L02: «سي⟨م⟩تين»: an initial س and a ي with its two dots below; then a medial م between angle brackets: the editor supplies it where the manuscript is damaged (note c); then a ت with its two dots, a ي with its two dots and a final ن with its dot, followed by the note mark c.
@obs L03: «نهر سمس»: «نهر», the ن with its dot; then «سمس», the teeth of a س, a small filled loop on the line, the م, and a final س. The space after the ر of «نهر» is 17 px wide, where within a word a ر stands at most 3 px from the next letter (frame 052, L06, «مرسول»; frame 054, L14, «ارٮٮطا»); transcribed as two words.
@obs L06: «دٮلور»: after the د, a short tooth without a dot, 16 px high, joined to a ل, a و and a ر; the editor's note g gives the form of entry (1853), «دٮلوں», with the same tooth and a final ن without its dot.
@obs L09: «اڡلاثر»: after the alif, a closed loop without dots joined to the lām-alif: the loop of ف or ق, where an ع after a letter that does not join is an open hook (frame 054, L04, «فرعى»); then the ث, its three dots set as a triangle, and a ر.
@obs L12: «ٮروں»: a tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, as on frames 015, 021 and 041; then a و and a final ن without its dot.
@obs L07: «الشمس الثانى», the second mountain of the sun, «الثانى» with the three dots of the ث and a final ى without dots; L08: «اطلس الصغير», the Lesser Atlas; L10, L11, L12: «فى جزيرة», on an island; L10: «بالوا», the ب with its dot; L11: «حسا», the ح without a dot; L13: «الثلج بدمشق», the mountain of snow at Damascus, with the dots of the ث, the ج, the ب, the ش and the ق; L14: «لبنان»; L15: «اللكّام», with a shadda over the ك; L16: «سنير», the ن with its dot above and the ي with its two below.
@obs L16: a thin slanting stroke, 7 by 4 px, stands above the ن and the ي of «سنير», too thin for a vowel sign of the font; it is not transcribed.
@obs The editor's footnotes of the opening begin at the foot of p. 51 (frame 057) with notes a-d and the beginning of note e, and continue here in two lines with the end of note e and notes f, g, h and i. The texts of notes a and c, whose marks stand on this page (L01, L02), are printed on p. 51; the marks of notes e, f and i stand on p. 51.
