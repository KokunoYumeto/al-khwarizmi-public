@obs The page begins the part of the work on the seas, in running text. L01, «* البحار الّتى على كرة الأرض المعمورة وجزائرها *», the seas on the sphere of the inhabited earth and their islands, and L02, «* البحر المغربىّ الخارج والشمالىّ الخارج *», the outer western sea and the outer northern sea, are headings in the large type, each between two small open stars, transcribed as asterisks. L01: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل; «الأرض», with a hamza over the alif; «وجزائرها», with a hamza on a tooth without dots. L02: «المغربىّ» and «والشمالىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it; the second «الخارج» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, after the خ, which is not transcribed.
@obs L03-L14: the course of the coast from point to point, «يمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», it passes to the longitude … and the latitude …, with the degrees and the minutes written with letters (abjad) as in the tables, each pair under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}. After each point stand the editor's running number in parentheses, (٩٦٧)-(٩٨٣), which goes on from the table of the mountains, and the number of the same point in Suhrāb's ʿAjāʾib al-aqālīm in square brackets: [٥٠٣]-[٥٠٩], then [٥٦٠]-[٥٦٨]; point 974 (L08) has none.
@obs The coast passes «صورة الطيلسان», the shape of a ṭaylasān (L04), «صورة قوارة», the shape of a qawwāra (L05), and «صورة الشابورة», the shape of a shābūra, and back «مثل الشابورة», like the shābūra (L09, L10); and the mouths of two rivers, «وهناك مصبّ نهر روذس», there is the mouth of the river روذس (L09-L10), with a shadda over the ب of «مصبّ», and «وهناك مصب نهر حوسدس» (L11-L12), without it. «اوّله» (L03) has a shadda over the و, «يمرّ» and «ويمرّ» over the ر, and «ثمّ» over the م; «روذس» has the dot of the ذ.
@obs L03: the latitudes of the first two points are printed «{z} ى», the sign for zero in the place of the degrees and ى, 10, in that of the minutes.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: ٮٮ, a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, 65 and 68 px wide (L06, the degrees of both latitudes), where the isolated ب is a bowl of about 50 px with a riser at each end, which read with a ي give 12, between the latitudes before and after, 8 and 16; a tooth without its dot in the degrees of the latitude, ٮو (L07, L08), ٮح (L09) and ٮط (L10), which read with a ي give 16, 18 and 19; ں, the round bowl of a final ن without its dot, 50 (L10, the minutes of the longitude); ٮه (L11, the minutes of the longitude), a tooth without its dot joined to a final ه. The isolated ى, 10, has no dots (L03, L08, L09, L12).
