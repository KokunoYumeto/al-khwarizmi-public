@obs The river قيدس goes on from p. 116 (frame 122), whose last words, «نهر قيدس من عين مع طرف الجبل ٮٮطعں», L01 continues with «اوّلها عند طول ڡيح ل والعرض ك {z} (١٧٦٢)», the beginning of its spring, at the longitude ڡيح ل and the latitude 20° 0′; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ڡٮا ل والعرض ٮد ل (١٧٦٣) *» (L01-L02), and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude ڡٮا ل and the latitude ٮد ل.
@obs L02-L04: «نهر ڡرٮدس من جبل ٮٮطعں اوّله عند طول ڡك ل والعرض ⟨ك {z}⟩ (١٧٦٤)», the river ڡرٮدس, from the mountain ٮٮطعں; its beginning is at the longitude ڡك ل and the latitude ⟨20° 0′⟩; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ڡٮد ل والعرض ٮه ك (١٧٦٥) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude ڡٮد ل and the latitude ٮه ك. The coast of the Great Sea passes the mouth of a river ڡرٮدس at entry (1187) (frame 084, L02).
@obs L04-L06: «نهر سالس من عي⟨ن فى الجب⟩ل ٮٮطعں اوّله عند طول ڡك ك والعرض ٮط م (١٧٦٦)», the river سالس, from a spring in the mountain ٮٮطعں; its beginning is at the longitude ڡك ك and the latitude ٮط م; «ومصبّه ⟨فى البحر عند⟩ طول و⟨ٮط {z} والعرض⟩ ٮد مه (١٧٦٧) *», and its mouth ⟨in the sea⟩ is at the longitude printed «و⟨ٮط {z}» and the latitude ٮد مه.
@obs L06: «و⟨ٮط {z} والعرض⟩»: the line over the numerals begins over a و printed before the opening bracket and reaches over «ٮط {z}» within the brackets; the closing bracket follows «والعرض», before the note mark g. Transcribed with the line over the و, the bracket and the numerals.
@obs The mountain ٮٮطعں of L02 and L04 is entries 655-656 of the table of the mountains (frame 050, L17), printed «جبل نبطعں», where the editor's note f gives ٮٮطعى, without dots, at entries (1762), (1764) and (1766), corrected to ٮٮطعں in the editor's corrigenda (p. 163, frame 169); note b restores the latitude of L03 after these entries (655)-(656). L02: a tooth without a dot, 14 px high, and a low rise of 3 to 6 px before the ط; L04: a tooth without a dot, 18 px high, and a flat stroke of 17 px before the ط; in both the ط, the medial ع and the final bowl without a dot of p. 116 (frame 122, L17). Transcribed ٮٮطعں, with the low second tooth of «نبطعں» in the table.
@unres L04: the flat stroke between the tooth and the ط of «ٮٮطعں» may be a joining stroke without a second tooth: ٮطعں. Transcribed ٮٮطعں.
@obs L06-L08: «نهر حاٮرس يخرج من جبل اداسرون اوّله عند ⟨طول ڡكر ى⟩ والعرض كا ى (١٧٦٨)», the river حاٮرس comes out of the mountain اداسرون; its beginning is at ⟨the longitude ڡكر ى⟩ and the latitude 21° 10′; «يمرّ بمدينة حاٮرن ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ڡكح ل والعرض ٮه م (١٧٦٩) *», it passes the town حاٮرن, and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude ڡكح ل and the latitude ٮه م.
