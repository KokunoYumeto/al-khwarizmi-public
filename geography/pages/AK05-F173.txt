@fig AK05-F173-FIG1 40 340 2130 3170
@obs The plate reproduces in halftone the map on fol. 21ᵃ of the manuscript, which the editor's list of plates (p. 162, frame 168) names the map of the forms of the coast and of the terms used for them. The map is printed sideways on the page; the caption «TAFEL 2» stands in the margin and reads with the map.
@obs The map shows a sea drawn with wavy parallel lines, whose coast turns in and out in bays and capes; the forms of the coast are labelled in writing with the terms for them. The editor's list names «تسنيم» as a term of the map that the text does not use, and «شابورة» (p. 162, frame 168). The text names the forms in its descriptions of the coasts, for example «على صورة الطيلسان» (frame 104, L02, L07) and «على صورة القوارة» (frame 085, L12).
@obs The plate is reproduced as an image (the crop of the frame and its derivative); its handwritten labels are not transcribed letter by letter.
