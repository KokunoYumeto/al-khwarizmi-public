The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 6ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type in the left margin beside entry 266 (L17).
@obs L06, L09: «قبرس» is printed with the dot of the ب, where on frame 024 (L18) the same name shows no dot.
@obs Vowel signs and other marks in the names, transcribed as printed: L03: «المقدّس», with a shadda over the د; «إيلِيَاء», with a hamza below the first alif, a kasra below the ل, a fatha above the second ي and a final hamza on the line after the alif. L05: «قيساريّة», L08: «طبريّة» and L18: «اللَاذقيّة», with a shadda over the ي; in L18 also a short slanting stroke, a fatha, above the لا. L07: «عكّا», with a shadda over the ك. L08: «الأردنّ», with a hamza above the alif and a shadda over the ن. L12, L16: «صيداء», with the hamza on the line after the alif.
@obs L01: the two dots of the ة of «الرملة» are printed touching the top of the letter. L12: a thin stroke, 4 px wide and 11 px high, stands above the ب of «البحر»; it is smaller than the vowel signs of the font and is not transcribed.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: ٮو (L02, L03) and ٮح (L07, L09), the tooth without its dot, in the degrees of the longitude; ٮط (L11-L13, L16), the tooth without its dot before a ط; لٮ (L03, L04, L08), the final ب without its dot, in the degrees of the latitude; ٮو (L15, the minutes of the latitude). With their dots: نه (L01), نو (L04), نز (L05, L06, L10), ن (L04 and L09, minutes), لب (L01), لج (L12), يه (L15).
@obs L01: the dot of the ب of the latitude «لب» is printed small, 4 px across, below the middle of the bowl; a round dot of the size of the font's dots stands on the line to the left of the bowl, between the columns of the degrees and the minutes, and is not transcribed. L15: a dark speck, 4 by 5 px, stands to the left of the و of the minutes «ٮو» at the height of a dot above the tooth; it is not transcribed as a dot.
@obs L08: the degrees of the longitude, ٮر: a tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, where the isolated ر of the font is a plain curved stroke (frame 021, L09); the neighbouring entries have نز, 57, and ٮح, 58. In these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed.
@obs The minutes of the latitude in L05 and of the longitude in L11 are «ٮه», a tooth without its dot joined to a final ه; transcribed ٮه.
