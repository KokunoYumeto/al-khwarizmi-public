@obs The table goes on from frame 026 without a heading: the cities of the fourth clime, entries 287-303. Only entry 288 has the word «مدينة». Entries 288-290 and 297 have no numbers of Suhrāb; the others have [١٩٤] (entry 287), [٢٢٦], [٢٢٨], [٣٠١], [٣٠٢], [٢٢٧], [٢٥٦] (entries 291-296) and [٢٦٠], [٢٢٩], [٢٦٦], [٢٣٠], [٢٦٧], [٢٧٦] (entries 298-303).
@obs L03-L04: entries 289 and 290, al-Nāʾūsa and Ālūsa, have no coordinates: the columns of the numerals are empty, and no rules are printed in them from below entry 288 to above entry 291. The editor's notes c and d say that the manuscript gives no longitude and latitude for them.
@obs Signs in the names, transcribed as printed: L03: «الناؤوسة», with a hamza above the first و; L04: «آلوسة», with a madda over the alif; L06: «الأنبار» and L10: «رأس», with a hamza above the alif; L07: «حرّان» and L15: «سرّ», with a shadda over the ر; L11: «قرقيسياء», with the hamza on the line after the alif; L15: «رأى», with a hamza above the alif and a final ى without dots; L17: «المدائن», with a hamza above the tooth.
@obs L15: «سرّ من رأى»: the tail of the ر of سرّ ends to the right of the م, without reaching under it, 20 px from it at the nearest; inside a word on this page ر stands 12 to 17 px from the next letter (L07, L11), and before a word space 28 px or more (frame 018, L15-L16); in doubt the space is written.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: ں (L11, the minutes of the longitude), the bowl of a final ن without its dot; لٮ (L16, the degrees of the latitude), the final ب without its dot. With their dots: سز (L01), the ز with its dot; لب (L06); لج (L17); ن (L12, the minutes of the longitude; L13, the minutes of the latitude); يه (L05).
@obs L12: the degrees of the longitude, سر: in these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII), as in سز of L01, printed with the dot; transcribed ر as printed.
@obs The degrees printed with a final ح, سح (L05, L10), عح (L14, L17) and لح (L05, L14), show no dot in the bowl: the same form is a ج without its dot, 3, which the print gives with its dot in لج (L17). Transcribed as printed.
