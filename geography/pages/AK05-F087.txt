@obs The running text goes on from p. 80 (frame 086), whose last words, «يمرّ الى طول», L01 continues with the longitude «ص م»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٢٤٠)-(١٢٦٠), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٨٤٨]-[٨٦٨], without a gap.
@obs The places of the coast: L12: «ويمرّ بمصبّ نهرين», past the mouth of two rivers; L15-L16: «ويمرّ فيماسّ الجبل الّذى ابتدأنا منه وهو جبل نون يماسّه عند طول عد م والعرض مج ن», it passes and touches the mountain from which we began, which is the mountain نون; it touches it at the longitude 74° 40′ and the latitude 43° 50′. The editor identifies this last point, which has no running number, with point 1231 (frame 086, L09), whose latitude is «مح ه», 48° 5′ (notes r and s); there the mountain is called «جبل يون», and the editor's note i on p. 58 (frame 064) records both names. L16 ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk: the end of the section.
@obs Points 1244 and 1246 (L03-L04, L05) are given by their longitude only, «يمرّ بطول», and point 1247 (L05) by «ويصير الى طول ڡح ل» without a latitude; points 1250 and 1259 (L07, L14) by their latitude only, «يمرّ بعرض». The shapes of the coast are «الطيلسان» (L02, L05, L08, L10) and «القوارة» (L03, L07, L13-L14).
