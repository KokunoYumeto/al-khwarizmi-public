@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 62-63: each of its lines continues the same line of p. 62 (frame 068), L01-L18, entries 893/894-927/928. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads.
@obs The colours are «وردىّ», rose (L01, L04, L18); «اصفر مشبع», deep yellow (L02, L05, L09, L10, L15); «لازورد», lapis blue (L03); «اصفر», yellow (L06, L11, L13, L17); «احمر مشبع», deep red (L07); «احمر كمد», dull red (L08, L16); «اصفر تبنىّ», straw-yellow (L12); and «اصفر كدر», murky yellow (L14). The directions are «شمال», north (L02-L05, L07, L11-L14, L17, L18), «مغربىّ», western (L01, L06, L08, L09, L16), and «جنوب», south (L10, L15). «وردىّ», «تبنىّ» and «مغربىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it.
@obs L01: the note mark a stands after the colour. L13: the mark h stands after the minutes of the last latitude, «مه», and the mark i after the colour. L14: the mark k stands after the minutes of the first latitude, «مه».
@obs L08: the degrees of the last longitude are printed «⟨م⟩و»: the م between angle brackets, the note mark e, then the و. L11: the degrees of the first longitude, «⟨نز⟩», with the dots of the ن and of the ز, between angle brackets, followed by the note mark f. The editor supplies them from S where the manuscript is unclear (notes e and f). Transcribed with the brackets in the cells.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: a tooth without its dot in ٮو (L04, L11, the degrees of the first latitude; L16, the degrees of the last latitude), ٮد (L07, the degrees of the first latitude), ٮح (L08, L11, the degrees of the last latitude; L09, both longitudes; L10, the first longitude; L11, the last longitude) and ٮط (L12, the degrees of the last latitude), which read as a ن would give 56, 54, 58 and 59; مٮ (L04, the degrees of the last longitude), 42; سٮ (L12, the degrees of the last longitude), 62; and ٮٮ (L09, L10, the degrees of the first latitude), a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, which read with a ن give 52, as نب with the dots (L01, L02, L17). With their dots: نب (L01, L02, L17), with the dots of the ن and of the ب, 52; ند (L04, L16), 54; نا (L05, L08), 51; نو (L07, L10), 56; نز (L11, L15), 57; نح (L15), 58; عز (L16, L17), 77; قز (L14), 107; فح (L18), 88; ن, 50 (L01, L06, L07, L12, L13, L15, L17).

@obs L09, L10: the degrees of the first latitude are a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots: in L09 a flat bowl of 55 px and a tall riser standing apart at its right end, 63 px together; in L10 a bowl with the tooth and the riser at its right end, 64 px, where the isolated ب is a bowl of about 50 px with a riser at each end (frame 047, L05).
