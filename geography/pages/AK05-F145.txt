@obs The river from a town on the sea goes on from p. 138 (frame 144), whose last words, «نهر يخرج من مدينة على البحر اوّله», a river that comes out of a town on the sea, whose beginning, L01 continues: «عند طول ما له والعرض مح {z} (٢١٠٨) ومصبّه فى مدينة عند طول مح {z} والعرض مب ى (٢١٠٩) *», is at the longitude 41° 35′ and the latitude مح ō, and its mouth is in a town, at the longitude مح ō and the latitude 42° 10′. The editor marks «مدينة» with sic (note a).
@obs L02-L05: «نهر يخرج من جبل اوّله عند طول مح مه والعرض مب ن (٢١١٠) يمرّ فيما بين مدينتين لا اسماء لهما ويصبّ فى البحر فيما بين مدينة لا اسم عليها فى الصورة ومدينة لرسو عند طول مه ى والعرض م {z} (٢١١١) *», a river that comes out of a mountain, whose beginning is at the longitude مح مه and the latitude 42° 50′; it passes between two towns that have no names and flows into the sea between a town that has no name on it in the map and the town لرسو, at the longitude 45° 10′ and the latitude 40° 0′. The town is entry 354 of the table of the cities, «لرسو على البحر» (frame 031, L04), at the longitude 45° 40′ and the latitude 40° 0′.
@obs L05-L07: «نهر يخرج من جبل يمرّ فيما بين مدينة لا اسم لها فى الصورة ومدينة اسلينو اوّله عند طول مد ى والعرض مح ى (٢١١٢) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول مه ل والعرض ما {z} (٢١١٣) *», a river that comes out of a mountain and passes between a town that has no name in the map and the town اسلينو; its beginning is at the longitude 44° 10′ and the latitude مح ى, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 45° 30′ and the latitude 41° 0′. The town is entry 444 of the table of the cities, «اسلببو» (frame 036, L16), at the longitude 45° 10′ and the latitude 42° 10′, where the editor's note g gives the form اسلينو of this entry (2112); here the ي has its two dots below and the ن its dot above.
@obs The towns without names are entries 440 and 442 of the table of the cities, the word «مدينة» alone (frame 036, L12, L14); entry 443 is «مدينة سلوانا» (L15). The editor's notes b and d concern them.
@obs L07-L09: «نهر يخرج من جبل اوّله عند طول مد ل والعرض مح ن (٢١١٤) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول مه {z} والعرض مب ك (٢١١٥) *», a river that comes out of a mountain, whose beginning is at the longitude 44° 30′ and the latitude مح ن, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 45° 0′ and the latitude 42° 20′.
@obs L09-L10: «نهر يخرج من جبل ٮلمون اوّله عند طول ٮد ل والعرض مٮ {z} (٢١١٦) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ٮه م والعرض له م (٢١١٧) *», a river that comes out of the mountain ٮلمون, whose beginning is at the longitude ٮد ل and the latitude مٮ ō, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude ٮه م and the latitude 35° 40′. The mountain is entries 851-852 of the table of the mountains, «جبل كلمون» (frame 064, L08), whose first boundary is at the longitude 58° 50′ and the latitude 42° 40′ and its last at the longitude ٮه ن and the latitude 41° 0′ (frame 065, L08); the name is printed here, as on frame 135 (L01), with a tooth without a dot before the ل. The latitude of the mouth, 35° 40′, stands well south of the other points of the page, 40° to 48° 50′; the editor's notes do not comment on it.
@obs L11: «عين لا يجرى منها نهر عند طول ٮح م والعرض مد م (٢١١٨) مقدارها سدس جزء *», a spring from which no river runs, at the longitude ٮح م and the latitude 44° 40′; its extent is a sixth of a degree.
@obs L12-L14: «نهر طور اوّله عين عند طول سح {z} والعرض مد م (٢١١٩) يمرّ فيما بين البحر ومدينة جرزان ويمرّ فيما بين مدينة برذعة ومدينة باسيا ويصبّ فى البحر عند طول عد م والعرض مٮ يه (٢١٢٠) *», the river طور, whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 68° 0′ and the latitude 44° 40′; it passes between the sea and the town جرزان, passes between the town برذعة and the town باسيا, and flows into the sea at the longitude 74° 40′ and the latitude مٮ يه. The towns are entries of the table of the cities (frame 038): 461, «جزان» (L03), at the longitude 71° 0′ and the latitude 44° 0′; 465, «برذعة» (L07), at the longitude 78° 0′ and the latitude مح ō; and 466, «رفسا مع الجبل وباب الخزر» (L08), at the longitude 73° 50′ and the latitude 45° 35′; the editor's notes there give the forms of this entry (2120): جزران, ٮردعه and باسيا.
@obs L13: «جرزان»: the ج with its dot, the joined ر without a dot, a ز with its dot, 7 by 8 px, over the separate letter, an alif and the final ن with its dot; the editor's note b on frame 038 gives the form of this entry (2120) as جزران, with the dot over the letter joined to the ج. «برذعة»: the ب with its dot, the ذ with its dot and the ة with its two.
@obs L15-L16: «نهر الفرات اوّله عين فى جبل اوردخس عند طول سح ل والعرض ما ⟨ك⟩ (٢١٢١) ⟨يمرّ الى طرف⟩ جبل مٮڡنبا عند طول س ك والعرض مب ك», the river Euphrates, whose beginning is a spring in the mountain اوردخس, at the longitude 68° 30′ and the latitude 41° ⟨20′⟩; ⟨it passes to the edge of⟩ the mountain مٮڡنبا at the longitude 60° 20′ and the latitude 42° 20′, continued on p. 140.
