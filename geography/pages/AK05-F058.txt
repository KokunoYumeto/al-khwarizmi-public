@obs The table of the mountains goes on across the opening pp. 52-53: each row of p. 52 is continued by a row of p. 53 (frame 059), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. The descriptions of entries 753/754 and 757/758 take two printed lines each, L03-L04 and L06-L07; the second line has no numbers and is transcribed as a row with empty number cells. Their boundaries, colours and directions stand on p. 53 at the height of the second line (L04, L07), and p. 53 has no line at the height of the first (L03, L06). The headings L15-L16 run across the opening: L15, «* الإقليم الخامس», is continued on p. 53 by «عرضه ما *», the fifth clime and its latitude, 41; and L16, «* الجبال الّتى فى», by «الإقليم الخامس *», the mountains in the fifth clime. They are set in the large type, each beginning with a small open star, transcribed as an asterisk, and stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their ends on p. 53; transcribed as headings. L15: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif; L16: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل, and «فى», with the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى.
@obs The running numbers go on from entry 747/748 (frame 056) and run (٧٤٩ ٧٥٠)-(٧٧٣ ٧٧٤); the numbers of Suhrāb run [١٢٣٩ ١٢٤٠]-[١٢٦٣ ١٢٦٤] without a gap. The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 14ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside entry 765/766 (L11).
