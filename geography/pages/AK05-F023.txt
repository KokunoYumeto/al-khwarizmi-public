@obs The table goes on from frame 022 without a heading: the cities of the fourth clime, entries 214-230. Numbers of Suhrāb stand at entries 214-218 ([١٩٧]-[٢٠١]), 225 ([٢٠٢]), 227 ([٢٠٤]) and 230 ([٢٠٥]); [٢٠٣] does not appear, and the editor's note g proposes it for entry 228. Only entry 220 has the word «مدينة». The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 5ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the left margin beside entry 225 (L13).
@obs L07-L08: entry 220, the city of Tūnis, has two pairs of coordinates, printed on two lines and joined on the right by a brace; the running number and the name stand between the two lines. Transcribed as two rows, the second without number and name; the minutes of the second longitude carry the editor's note mark b.
@obs L09-L12: entries 221-224, Tāhart, Tāqdamt, Targha and Kutāma, have no coordinates: the columns of the numerals are empty, and no rules are printed in them from below the second line of entry 220 to above entry 225.
@obs L01, L18: «ڡرقا» and «ڡرنبوس» begin with a loop raised above the line and joined by a stroke on the line to a ر: the loop of ف or ق without dots, of the size and height of the ق of «قرب» (L01), which has its two dots. An isolated و stands on the line and goes straight into its tail, without a joining stroke and without the head of a ر; transcribed ڡر.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the names, transcribed so: «ٮرقه» (L13), the tooth before the ر; «ماوں» (L04), the final ن; «قورٮں» (L16), a tooth and the final ن after the ر. L02: «اثانا»: the three dots of the ث are printed as one mark; the ن has its dot. L09, L10: the two dots of the first ت of «تاهرت» and «تاقدمت» are printed touching the top of the following alif.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: لٮ, the final ب without its dot, in the degrees of the latitude (L02, L03, L05, L06) and of the longitude (L07); مٮ (L05, the degrees of the longitude). With their dots: مب (L14, the degrees of the longitude); يه (L02, L03) and ن (L01, L04), in the minutes of the latitude.
@obs L01: the degrees of the longitude, لر: in these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed. L18: in the degrees of the longitude, مو, the م is printed apart from the و.
