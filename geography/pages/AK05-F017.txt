@obs The table goes on from frame 016 without a heading: the last cities of the second clime, entries 115-126. Then the heading of the third clime and of its cities, set between small open stars, and the first two cities of the third clime, entries 127-128. Entry 125 has no number of Suhrāb; the numbers run [٩٤]-[١٠٤] in entries 115-126 and [١٠٦], [١٠٧] in entries 127-128, and [١٠٥] does not appear on the page.
@obs L13: the heading reads «الإقليم الثالث وعرضه ل كٮ», with و before عرضه, where the headings of the first and second climes have عرضه alone (frames 010 and 014). The latitude of the third clime, «ل كٮ», is printed with a line above the two numerals, transcribed with {ov:…}; its minutes are a long oblique stroke over a flat bowl, 120 px wide in the type of the heading, the ك joined to the final ب without its dot, 22 (frame 014, L11): 30° 22′. This is the latitude that Ptolemy gives for the parallel of the longest day of 14 hours (Almagest II 6), as the 16° 27′ of the first clime (frame 010, L01) is that of 13 hours. In L13 and L14 the alif of الإقليم carries a hamza below; L14: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل. A speck, 4 px across, stands on the line between الثالث and the closing star of L14; it is not transcribed.
@obs L02: the first letter of «ڡاطالا», the initial loop of ف or ق joined to the alif, is printed without dots; note b gives قاطلا from another entry. L04: «ارٮى», the tooth before the final ى without its dot; a speck stands on the line after the word. L09: «اطراٮس»: after the alif a tall tooth without its dot, then a final س with three small teeth and its bowl; transcribed so (the editor's note e at L08 compares this entry).
@obs L01: a small oblique mark, 5 px wide and 6 px high, stands above «امرس» between the م and the س; it is smaller than the vowel signs of the font and is not transcribed.
@obs L09, L10: the degrees of the longitude, «ڡمٮ»: the loop of ف or ق without dots, a medial م set below it, and the flat bowl of a final ب without its dot, with the small tooth with which the final ب joins; read with the values ق م ب, 142, beside the longitude of L08, قم, 140. L04: the degrees of the longitude, «قٮٮ»: the ق with its two dots, then a tooth and the final ب, both without dots, the build of قنٮ on frame 013 (L14).
