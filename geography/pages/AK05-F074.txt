@obs The running text of the outer western and northern sea goes on from p. 67 (frame 073): the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٠٠٣)-(١٠٢٧), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٥٨٧]-[٦١٠]; point 1014 (L08) has none. L01: «وهناك مصبّ نهر», there is the mouth of a river, without its name; L05-L06: «على صورة القوارة», along the shape of the qawwāra; L13: «ويرجع الى», it returns to; L16: «على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān. «يمرّ», «ويمرّ», «ثمّ» and «مصبّ» are printed with a shadda.
@obs L05: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 17ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «والعرض» and the latitude «مو كه», not in the margin; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs L04: the word «طول» is printed between angle brackets, «⟨طول⟩», followed by the note mark b: the editor supplies it, which the manuscript lacks (note b). L07: the minutes of the longitude are the sign for zero between angle brackets, followed by the note mark e: the editor supplies them from S where the manuscript has a gap (note e).
@obs Note marks: L03, a after the longitude; L04, b after «⟨طول⟩» and c after the latitude; L06, d after the second longitude; L07, e after the longitude and f after the second latitude; L08, g after the first longitude; L11, h after the first longitude; L12, i after the first latitude.
