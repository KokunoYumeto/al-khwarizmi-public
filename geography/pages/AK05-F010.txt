@obs L01-L02: the two headings stand between small open stars, transcribed as asterisks. In L01 the latitude of the clime, «يو كر», is printed with a line above the two numerals, the line with which the edition marks numerals written with letters in the running text; transcribed with {ov:…}. The ر of كر stands for ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII): 16° 27'.
@obs From this page the second column carries, in square brackets, the numbers of the same entries in Suhrāb's ʿAjāʾib al-aqālīm: [١]-[٣] at entries 9-11 and [٤] at entry 18; the other entries have none.
@obs The angle brackets ⟨ ⟩: the editor's note a says that he marks between them what he supplies where the manuscript is damaged, has a gap or omits something, and with dots between them what he could not supply; the words of entry 9 are supplied from Suhrāb, whose book he cites as S. Transcribed as printed: ⟨مدينة ٮارس⟩ (L03), ⟨ٮد⟩ (L05, the degrees of the latitude), ⟨مدينة من⟩ (L08).
@obs L03: «⟨مدينة ٮارس⟩ا», as printed: after the closing bracket and the note mark a stands a single alif, outside the brackets, the letter that the manuscript keeps of the damaged words; the first letter of ٮارس, a tooth joined to the alif, is printed without a dot.
@obs Letters printed without their dots, transcribed so: the degrees of the longitude ٮٮ (L04) and of the latitude ٮٮ (L06), each a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, with two risers at the right end of the bowl, a tall and a short one, where the isolated ب has one (frame 013, L15); ڡكد (L07), the loop of ف or ق without dots; ٮد (L05) and ٮارس (L03), the tooth without its dot; ى, the final form without dots (L03, the degrees of the longitude, 10). The ق of قعٮ (L08) has its two dots, printed small; the print of this cell is blurred, and the shape between the ق and the final ٮ is that of a medial ع.
@obs L09-L11: for the three ports of China, entries 15-17, the print sets a single zero sign in the middle of each pair of columns, under the rules of the longitude and of the latitude; transcribed as a zero sign spanning the two columns.
@obs L05: the editor's note c says that the manuscript is damaged at دنقلة but the reading is certain; note d, that the degrees of the latitude are damaged and are supplied from S.
