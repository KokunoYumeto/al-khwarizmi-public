@obs L01-L03: «نهر يخرج من جبل اوّله عند طول عا مه والعرض ما ل (٢١٣٠) يمرّ الى موضع طوله عا ن والعرض مح {z} (٢١٣١) يمرّ فيما بين برذعة ومدينة نشوى ويصبّ فى البحر عند طول عد ن والعرض ما مه (٢١٣٢) *», a river that comes out of a mountain, whose beginning is at the longitude 71° 45′ and the latitude 41° 30′; it passes to a place whose longitude is 71° 50′ and the latitude مح ō, passes between Barda'a and the town Nashwā, and flows into the sea at the longitude 74° 50′ and the latitude 41° 45′. The towns are entries 465, «برذعة», at the longitude عح ō and the latitude مح ō, and 463, «نشوى», at the longitude 74° 25′ and the latitude 41° 35′, of the table of the cities (frame 038, L07, L05).
@obs L04: «* الإقليم السابع وما فيه من العيون والأنهار *», the seventh clime and the springs and rivers in it: the heading, in larger type, between two open stars.
@obs L05-L06: «نهر اوّله عين عند طول ك م والعرض مز ٮه (٢١٣٣) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ٮح {z} والعرض مز ل (٢١٣٤) *», a river whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 20° 40′ and the latitude 47° ٮه, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude ٮح ō and the latitude 47° 30′.
@obs L06-L08: «نهر حرٮٯ اوّله عين عند طول كا ل والعرض مه ك (٢١٣٥) يمرّ الى طول كا ى والعرض مح {z} (٢١٣٦) ثمّ يصبّ فى البحر عند طول ٮط {z} والعرض مح ل (٢١٣٧) *», the river حرٮٯ, whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 21° 30′ and the latitude 45° 20′; it passes to the longitude 21° 10′ and the latitude 48° 0′, then flows into the sea at the longitude ٮط ō and the latitude 48° 30′.
@obs L08-L11: «⟨نهر⟩ اسماٮوس اوّله ⟨فى⟩ جبل ٮورسس عند طول كه {z} والعرض مر {z} (٢١٣٨) ⟨يمرّ بمدينة⟩ درڡطٯ ويصبّ فى البحر عند طول كا {z} والعرض نا ن (٢١٣٩) *», ⟨the river⟩ اسماٮوس, whose beginning is ⟨in⟩ the mountain ٮورسس at the longitude 25° 0′ and the latitude مر ō; ⟨it passes the town⟩ درڡطٯ and flows into the sea at the longitude 21° 0′ and the latitude 51° 50′. The mountain is entries 877-878 of the table of the mountains, «جبل ٮورسس» (frame 066, L06); the town is entry 512 of the table of the cities, «درڡطٯ» (frame 042, L02), at the longitude 28° 30′ and the latitude 49° 20′.
@obs L12-L14: «نهر رنوس اوّله عين عند طول كح ل والعرض مز {z} (٢١٤٠) بمدينة السٮس وبمدينة ٮورما ويصبّ فى البحر عند طول كط {z} والعرض نز {z} (٢١٤١) *», the river رنوس, whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 28° 30′ and the latitude 47° 0′; by the town السٮس and by the town ٮورما, and it flows into the sea at the longitude 29° 0′ and the latitude 57° 0′; «ويصبّ الى هٰذا النهر عين اوّلها عند طول كو م والعرض نا ل», and into this river flows a spring whose beginning is at the longitude 26° 40′ and the latitude 51° 30′, continued on p. 142. The towns are entries 518, «السٮس», at the longitude 29° 30′ and the latitude 54° 5′, and 516, «ٮوديا», of the table of the cities (frame 042, L08, L06); the editor's notes there record that this entry (2141) writes the first name the same way, with the variant reading السس, and the second ٮورما, with the variant reading ٮودما, as notes f and g of this page.
@obs L06 and L10: «حرٮٯ» and «درڡطٯ» end in the final ق joined to the letter before it, without its dots: two upright horns, 20 to 22 px high, joined in a band below their tips and open below it, the right one standing on the joining stroke, the left one running down into a round bowl. The ق with its two dots has this form in «امق» (frame 016, L11). The final ن after a tooth is printed otherwise: the tooth and the right side of the ن rise apart and meet only on the line, as in «عين» (L05). In «حرٮٯ» a tooth without a dot, 16 px high, stands before the ق.
