@obs The river from the mountain سرجيس goes on from p. 115 (frame 121), whose last words, «نهر يخرج من جبل سرجيس اوّله عند طول», L01 continues with «لط ك والعرض كا {z} (١٧٤٤)», the longitude 39° 20′ and the latitude 21° 0′ of its beginning; «يمرّ بجبل رحوما فيقطعه بنصفين ويصبّ فى البحر عند مدينة ماون عند طول لط م والعرض لٮ {z} (١٧٤٥) *» (L01-L02), it passes the mountain رحوما, which it cuts into two halves, and flows into the sea at the town ماون, at the longitude 39° 40′ and the latitude لٮ ō. The mountain رحوما is entries 687-688 of the table of the mountains of the third clime (frame 054, L01), printed «حوٮا», where the editor's note a gives the form of this entry (1745), رحوما.
@obs L02-L04: «نهر يخرج ايضاً من جبل سرحس مصبّه فى هٰذا النهر اوّله عند طول ما مه والعرض كا {z} (١٧٤٦)», a river comes out also from the mountain سرحس, with its mouth in this river; its beginning is at the longitude 41° 45′ and the latitude 21° 0′; «ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول م {z} والعرض كد {z} (١٧٤٧) *», and its mouth in the river is at the longitude 40° 0′ and the latitude 24° 0′. The mountain is entries 615-616 of the table of the mountains of the second clime (frame 048, L13), «جبل حرحس», where the editor's note f gives the name سرجيس at entry (1744), the beginning of the river before, and سرحس at this entry (1746).
@obs L05-L07: «عين عند طول ما ل والعرض ٮو ل (١٧٤٨)», a spring at the longitude 41° 30′ and the latitude ٮو ل; «يخرج منها نهر الى بحيرة نوبا عند طول مد {z} والعرض ٮد م (١٧٤٩)», from it a river goes out to the lake نوبا, at the longitude 44° 0′ and the latitude ٮد م; «والبحيرة مقدار نصف جزء *», and the lake is half a degree in size.
@obs L07-L08: «عين اوّلها عند طول سط م والعرض ٮح ل (١٧٥٠)», a spring whose beginning is at the longitude 69° 40′ and the latitude ٮح ل; «تمرّ بٮاله ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول سح ى والعرض ك {z} (١٧٥١) *», it passes ٮاله, and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 68° 10′ and the latitude 20° 0′. The town is entry 100 of the table of the cities (frame 016, L03), «ماله», where the editor's note b gives the form of this entry (1751), ٮاله, with a tooth without dots for the م.
@obs L07: «بٮاله»: the ب, with its dot, rises 14 px above the joining stroke; between it and the alif the stroke runs 38 px and rises twice, 8 and 9 px wide and 5 px high, where «نوبا» (L06) joins its ب to the alif in 3 px. Transcribed with a tooth without a dot, the form that the editor's note on entry 100 gives for this entry.
@unres L07: the low rises between the ب and the alif may also be a lengthened joining stroke without a tooth: باله. Transcribed بٮاله.
@obs L08-L10: «نهر لادو يخرج من عين اوّلها عند طول عد {z} والعرض ٮح ك (١٧٥٢)», the river لادو comes out of a spring whose beginning is at the longitude 74° 0′ and the latitude ٮح ك; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول عح ك والعرض كٮ ك (١٧٥٣) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 78° 20′ and the latitude كٮ ك.
@obs L10-L11: «نهر من جبل اوّله عند طول عط مه والعرض ك ل (١٧٥٤)», a river from a mountain whose name is not given; its beginning is at the longitude 79° 45′ and the latitude 20° 30′; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ڡ {z} والعرض ٮح {z} (١٧٥٥) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude ڡ ō and the latitude ٮح ō. Points 1754 and 1755 have no numbers of Suhrāb.
@obs L12-L13: «نهر من جبل كرمان اوّله عند طول صد ل والعرض كح م (١٧٥٦)», a river from the mountain of Kirmān; its beginning is at the longitude 94° 30′ and the latitude 28° 40′; «ومصبّه فى البحر مع مدينة النيرون عند طول صٮ ك والعرض كح م (١٧٥٧) *», and its mouth is in the sea beside the town al-Nīrūn, at the longitude صٮ ك and the latitude 28° 40′. The mountain is entries 647-648 of the table of the mountains (frame 050, L13), «جبل كرمان»; the town is entry 112 of the table of the cities (frame 016, L15), «النيرون على البحر», al-Nīrūn on the sea, at the longitude صٮ ك and the latitude كح ل, where the editor's note e says that the manuscript writes the name without any dots, there and at this entry (1757).
@obs L14-L15: «نهر من جبل اوّله عند طول قٮ {z} والعرض كٮ {z} (١٧٥٨)», a river from a mountain whose name is not given; its beginning is at the longitude قٮ ō and the latitude كٮ ō; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ق {z} والعرض ك يه (١٧٥٩) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 100° 0′ and the latitude 20° 15′.
@obs L15-L17: «ونهر من هٰذا الجبل ايضاً اوّله عند طول قب {z} والعرض كا ٮه (١٧٦٠)», and a river also from this mountain; its beginning is at the longitude 102° 0′ and the latitude كا ٮه; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ق ل والعرض ك ل (١٧٦١) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 100° 30′ and the latitude 20° 30′; then «نهر قيدس من عين مع طرف الجبل ٮٮطعں», the river قيدس, from a spring beside the end of the mountain ٮٮطعں, continued on p. 117.
@obs L17: «ٮٮطعں»: two teeth without dots, the ط, the medial ع and a final bowl without a dot, 39 px wide and 24 px deep below the line, whose right side rises 4 px above the line and descends from the ع, where the final ى of «فى» (L01) and «الى» (L05) first turns to the right before its bowl. The mountain is entries 655-656 of the table of the mountains (frame 050, L17), printed «جبل نبطعں» with the same final bowl, where the editor's note f gives ٮٮطعى, without dots, at entries (1762), (1764) and (1766), a form that the editor's corrigenda (p. 163, frame 169) correct to ٮٮطعں, the form of this line.
@obs L13: «النيرون» has all its dots: the dot of the ن over the first tooth, the two dots of the ي under the second, and the dot of the final ن. L17: «قيدس», with the two dots of the ق and the two dots of the ي.
