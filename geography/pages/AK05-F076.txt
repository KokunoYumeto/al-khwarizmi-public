@obs The running text goes on from p. 69 (frame 075), whose last words, «ويمرّ الى», L01 continues with «طول»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٠٣٩)-(١٠٥٦), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٦٢٣]-[٦٤٠], without a gap. L10: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 17ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, at its beginning; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs The places of the coast: L02-L04: «وينزل مع مدينة اسفارٮوس ومع اسفلها», it goes down along the town of اسفارٮوس and along its lower part, and «يمرّ الى مدينة اقالينا يمرّ بأسفلها», it passes to the town of اقالينا and passes below it; L07: «يمرّ بيافا وأرسوف وقيساريّة وطرابلس واللاذقيّة», it passes Jaffa, Arsūf, Caesarea, Tripoli and Laodicea; L08: «ويرجع الى», it returns to; L10-L11: «متّصل* بمدينة افاسس», joined to the town of Ephesus; L12: «يمرّ بأسفل مدينة ٮطرون ويصير الى», it passes below the town of ٮطرون and comes to; L13: «مماسّ* لمدينة نيقوموذيا», touching the town of Nicomedia; L14: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة حلقيدون», below the town of Chalcedon; L15-L16: «موضع مسيل ثلٰثة انهار», a place where three rivers flow out, and «ويمرّ بمدينة هرقله ومدينة اورٮون ومدينة سيناٯ», the towns of Heraclea, اورٮون and سيناٯ; «ويمرّ هنالك بمصبّ», and there it passes the mouth of, continued on p. 71. The shapes of the coast are «القوارة» (L06, L10, L15) and «الطيلسان» (L11, L13).
@obs L10: «متّصل*» and L13: «مماسّ*»: an asterisk stands after the word, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10). «مماسّ» has a shadda over the س; in L13 the note mark l follows the asterisk.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ», «ويمرّ», «ثمّ», «متّصل» and «بمصبّ» with a shadda; «بأسفلها», «بأسفل» and «وأرسوف» with a hamza over the alif; «وقيساريّة» and «واللاذقيّة» with a shadda over the ي; «ثلٰثة» (L16) with a dagger alif, standing high above the three dots of the first ث. «هرقله» (L16) ends in ه.
@obs Teeth without dots in the names, transcribed so: L03: «اسفارٮوس»: after the ر, a tooth rising 18 px, joined to the و, without a dot; the form of note a has the same tooth. L12: «ٮطرون»: the tooth joined to the ط has no dot. L16: «اورٮون»: after the ر, a tooth without a dot, whose join to the following و is printed broken; the form of note n has the same tooth after «اثور».
@obs L16: «سيناٯ»: the last letter has the form of the isolated ق, a loop above a deep bowl, like the numeral ق, 100, of frame 071 (L02), and no dots; the isolated ى of the text has an S-shaped head (L10, the minutes of the latitude). Transcribed with the dotless qāf, ٯ.
@unres L09: the notched form may also be read as a single ر whose head is split, ر, 7. Transcribed ٮر.
@unres L16: the last letter of «سيناٯ» may also be read as a ى printed without the turn of its head: سيناى. Transcribed سيناٯ.
@obs Numerals: L01, L03: «لب», with the dot of the ب, 32. L10, L13: «نا», 51: the dot of the ن touches the right side of the alif. In these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII); transcribed ر as printed: «لر» (L10, the degrees of the latitude), 37, and «مر» (L14, the degrees of the latitude), 47. L09, the degrees of the longitude: the notched form ٮر of frame 041 (L06), a short tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two forming a notch at the top, where the isolated ر is a single rounded stroke; transcribed ٮر, which read as نز gives 57° 5′, between the longitudes 61° 0′ (L08) and 51° 40′ (L09). The ح of «لح» (L05) and «نح» (L15) shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3. L09: the line over the longitude «ٮر ه» is 72 px long, shorter than the others, about 110-140 px.
