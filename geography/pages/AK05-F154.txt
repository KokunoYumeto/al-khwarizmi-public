@obs The springs and rivers that fall into the great river go on from p. 147 (frame 153), whose last point, «ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول قز ك والعرض ن ل (٢٢٣٢)», L01 completes with its number of Suhrāb, [١٥٩١], followed by an asterisk.
@obs L01-L05: «ويقع ايضاً الى النهر الكبير ثلٰثة انهار من جبل مخرج النهر الأوّل ⟨عند طول⟩ ڡح {z} والعرض مح ل (٢٢٣٣) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول ڡج ى والعرض ٮا ل (٢٢٣٤) والثانى اوّله عند طول ڡج ل والعرض مر ل (٢٢٣٥) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول قد ل والعرض ن م (٢٢٣٦) والثالث اوّله عند طول قد مه والعرض مح ه (٢٢٣٧) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول ڡو ه والعرض ن ك (٢٢٣٨) *», into the great river fall also three rivers from a mountain: the source of the first river is ⟨at the longitude⟩ ڡح ō and the latitude 48° 30′, and its mouth in the river at the longitude ڡج ى and the latitude ٮا ل; the second begins at the longitude ڡج ل and the latitude مر ل, and its mouth in the river is at the longitude 104° 30′ and the latitude 50° 40′; the third begins at the longitude 104° 45′ and the latitude 48° 5′, and its mouth in the river is at the longitude ڡو ه and the latitude 50° 20′. The mountain is not named.
@obs L05-L11: «ويقع ايضاً الى النهر الكبير ثلٰثة انهار من جبل طغورا الأوّل منها مخرجه عند طول قيب {z} والعرض نح ى (٢٢٣٩) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول قط ك والعرض ن ه (٢٢٤٠) ومخرج الثانى عند طول قيد ه والعرض نب ه (٢٢٤١) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول قيب ى والعرض مط ل (٢٢٤٢) ومخرج الثالث عند طول قيه ل والعرض ٮا ل (٢٢٤٣) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول قيد ه والعرض مح ل (٢٢٤٤) *», into the great river fall also three rivers from the mountain طغورا: the first of them has its source at the longitude 112° 0′ and the latitude 58° 10′, and its mouth in the river at the longitude 109° 20′ and the latitude 50° 5′; the source of the second is at the longitude 114° 5′ and the latitude 52° 5′, and its mouth in the river at the longitude 112° 10′ and the latitude 49° 30′; the source of the third is at the longitude 115° 30′ and the latitude ٮا ل, and its mouth in the river at the longitude 114° 5′ and the latitude 48° 30′.
@obs The mountain «طغورا» (L06) is entries 941-942 of the table of the mountains, «جبل طفورا» (frame 070, L07), whose editor's note f gives the form of this entry (2239), طغورا; its boundaries are at the longitude 109° 20′ and the latitude 55° 45′ and at the longitude قٮه م and the latitude 51° 20′ (frame 071, L07). Here the غ has its dot.
@obs L11-L13: «ويقع الى هٰذا النهر الكبير عين اوّلها عند طول ڡط م والعرض مد ل (٢٢٤٥) مصبّها فى النهر عند طول قيح ه والعرض مو ه (٢٢٤٦) *», into this great river falls a spring whose beginning is at the longitude ڡط م and the latitude 44° 30′; its mouth in the river is at the longitude 118° 5′ and the latitude 46° 5′. «مصبّها» stands without و.
@obs L13-L15: «ويقع الى النهر الكبير ايضاً عين اوّلها عند طول قكد ل والعرض مط ه (٢٢٤٧) ومصبّها فى النهر عند طول قكج {z} والعرض مو ك (٢٢٤٨) *», into the great river falls also a spring whose beginning is at the longitude 124° 30′ and the latitude 49° 5′, and its mouth in the river at the longitude 123° 0′ and the latitude 46° 20′. L15 holds only the running number, the number of Suhrāb and the asterisk, at the right.
@obs L16: «نهر بوخردس اوّله عين عند طول ڡمه ل والعرض مر {z} (٢٢٤٩) يمرّ الى», the river بوخردس, whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 145° 30′ and the latitude 47° 0′; it passes to …, continued on p. 149. «بوخردس» has the dot of the ب and the dot of the خ.

@obs The loop of ف or ق without dots in the degrees of the longitude, transcribed so: «ڡح {z}» (L02), read قح 108° 0′ or فح 88; «ڡج ى» (L02) and «ڡج ل» (L03), read قج 103° 10′ and 103° 30′ or فج 83; «ڡو ه» (L05), read قو 106° 5′ or فو 86; «ڡط م» (L11), read قط 109° 40′ or فط 89; «ڡمه ل» (L16), the loop before م, a tens letter, which only ق can precede, 145° 30′; the variant of note g, «ڡعه ل», has the loop before ع, likewise ق, 175° 30′. @obs «ٮا ل» (L03, L10): a tooth without its dot joined to an alif: read with a ن, 51° 30′; read with a ي, 11. At L10, 51° 30′ is the latitude of the end of the mountain طغورا, 51° 20′ in the table; at L03, the great river passes 51° 0′ at the longitude 100° 30′ (frame 153, L05). In L03 faint specks of 6 to 8 px stand to the right of the tooth, not above it; the dot of the font is some 9 px across and solid.
@obs «مر ل» (L03) and «مر {z}» (L16): the letter joined to the م is the joined ر, the dotless ز: its head is a small hook 8 px wide and 7 to 8 rows high, without a counter, where the و of «مو ه» (L12) has a round head 14 px wide and 15 rows high. In these numerals the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII): 47° 30′ and 47° 0′.
