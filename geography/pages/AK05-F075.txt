@obs L01-L04 end the outer western and northern sea: after the point of latitude 72 (L01) the coast passes to the latitude «عح», returns from there to the longitude of one degree, «ويرجع من هناك الى طول جزء واحد» (L02), and returns along the longitude «ا {z}», 1° 0′, from the latitude «عح» to the latitude «{z} ى», 0° 10′, on the meridian of one degree, «على سمت طول جزء واحد», and so back to the place with whose mention it began, «فيرجع الى الموضع الّذى فيه ابتدأنا بذكره» (L03-L04): the first point of p. 66 (frame 072, L03), longitude 1° 0′ and latitude 0° 10′ (note a). L04 ends with a small open star, transcribed as an asterisk. «جزء» has a hamza on the line; «الّذى» a shadda over the ل; «ابتدأنا» a hamza over the alif.
@obs L05-L07: the heading of the next section, in the large type, between two small open stars, transcribed as asterisks: «* بحر طنجة وبحر مرطاسه وبحر أفريقيّة وبحر برقة وبحر مصر والشام وبحر ٮرقه ولزقه كلّها متّصلة بعضها ببعض *», the sea of Tangier, the sea of مرطاسه, the sea of Ifrīqiya, the sea of Barqa, the sea of Egypt and Syria, and the sea of ٮرقه and لزقه, all of them joined to one another. «أفريقيّة», with a hamza over the alif and a shadda over the ي; «كلّها», with a shadda over the ل; «متّصلة», with a shadda over the ت; «ببعض» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, before the ض, which is not transcribed. «مرطاسه» and «لزقه» end in ه, «برقة» in ة; «ٮرقه» begins with a tooth without a dot, joined to the ر.
@obs L08: the course of the new section begins «الابتداء من بحر طنجة من عند الأصنام النحاس», the beginning is from the sea of Tangier, from the copper idols (frame 073, L05-L06), at the longitude «ز ل», with the dot of the ز, and the latitude «له م»; this first point has neither a running number nor a number of Suhrāb (the editor's note g gives it as point 987). The word «⟨يمرّ⟩», between angle brackets and followed by the note mark h, is supplied by the editor, which the manuscript lacks (note h). L12: «وهناك مصبّ نهر خليفان», there is the mouth of the river Khalīfān; L13: «على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān, and «يمرّ بعرض», it passes at the latitude; L14: «يمرّ الى مدينة ثوقا ⟨ومدينة⟩ ذياررطس», it passes to the town of ثوقا and the town of Diarrhytus, with «⟨ومدينة⟩» supplied by the editor (note k). The running numbers run (١٠٢٨)-(١٠٣٨), and the numbers of Suhrāb [٦١١]-[٦١٣] and [٦١٥]-[٦٢٢].
