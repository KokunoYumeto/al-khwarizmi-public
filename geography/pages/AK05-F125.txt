@obs L01: the heading of the new section, in the large type, between two small open stars, transcribed as asterisks: «* ⟨الإق⟩ليم الثالث وما فيه من العيون و⟨الأنهار⟩ *», the third clime and the springs and the rivers in it; «الإقليم» with a hamza below the alif, «الأنهار» with a hamza over the alif. The editor supplies «الإق» and «الأنهار» (note a): an angle bracket opens before «الإق»; the ق is printed in its final form, not joined to the ل, and is followed by the note mark a, 18 by 20 px, of the size of the note mark after «⟨الأنهار⟩», 16 by 18 px, but without a closing bracket (critical note AK05-CN011). «الأنهار» stands between two brackets after «و», with the note mark a after the closing one.
@obs L02-L03: «⟨عين اوّلها⟩ عند طول ى م والعرض كط ⟨{z}⟩ (١٧٩٣) ⟨ومصبّها فى البحر⟩ عند طول ح ك والعرض كح ٮه (١٧٩٤) *», ⟨a spring whose beginning⟩ is at the longitude 10° 40′ and the latitude 29° ⟨0′⟩, ⟨and its mouth in the sea⟩ is at the longitude 8° 20′ and the latitude كح ٮه.
@obs L03-L05: «عين اوّلها عند طول ⟨يا يه⟩ ⟨وال⟩عرض كح {z} (١٧٩٥)», a spring whose beginning is at the longitude ⟨11° 15′⟩ and the latitude 28° 0′; «ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول ح {z} والعرض كز ل (١٧٩٦) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 8° 0′ and the latitude 27° 30′.
@obs L05-L06: «عين ⟨اوّلها⟩ عند طول يا يه والعرض كر ك (١٧٩٧)», a spring ⟨whose beginning⟩ is at the longitude 11° 15′ and the latitude كر ك; «ومصبّها فى البحر عند طول ح {z} والعرض كر {z} (١٧٩٨) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 8° 0′ and the latitude كر ō.
@obs L07-L08: «نهر حلٮڡاث اوّله عند طول ٮح {z} والعرض ل {z} (١٧٩٩)», the river حلٮڡاث; its beginning is at the longitude ٮح ō and the latitude 30° 0′; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ٮر مه والعرض لٮ {z} (١٨٠٠) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude ٮر مه and the latitude لٮ ō. The editor's note e refers to entry (1036), the mouth of a river on the coast of the sea of Tangier, «وهناك مصبّ نهر خليفان» (frame 075, L12), at the latitude لٮ ō.
@obs L07: «حلٮڡاث»: the initial ح, the ل, a low rise in the joining stroke, 6 px wide and 4 to 5 px above it, an upright loop without dots, 14 px wide and 19 px high, joined to the alif, and a final ث with its three dots. The loop has the form of the medial ف or ق without dots, where the medial ع of «والعرض» (L07) is a flat filled loop. Transcribed with a low tooth without a dot, as «ڡٮدس» of frame 084 (L03).
@unres L07: the low rise between the ل and the loop may also be the joining stroke without a tooth: حلڡاث. Transcribed حلٮڡاث.
@obs L08-L10: «نهر ساوس اوّله عين عند طول يط م والعرض ل {z} (١٨٠١)», the river ساوس, whose beginning is a spring at the longitude 19° 40′ and the latitude 30° 0′; «ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ك ك والعرض لٮ {z} (١٨٠٢) *», and its mouth in the sea is at the longitude 20° 20′ and the latitude لٮ ō.
