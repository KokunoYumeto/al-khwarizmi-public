@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 60-61: each of its lines continues the same line of p. 60 (frame 066). L01-L03 give the boundaries, colours and directions of entries 871/872-875/876; L04, «عرضه مح *», ends the heading of p. 60, L04, the seventh clime and its latitude, 48; L05, «الإقليم السابع *», ends «* الجبال الّتى فى», the mountains in the seventh clime; L06-L12 give entries 877/878-889/890; L13, «الى عرض ثلثة وستّين *», ends «* خلف الإقليم السابع», beyond the seventh clime to the latitude of sixty-three; L14, «الإقليم السابع *», ends «* الجبال الّتى وراء», the mountains beyond the seventh clime; L15 gives entry 891/892. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads. The headings stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their beginnings on p. 60; transcribed as headings.
@obs L04: «عرضه» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ض and the ه, and L14: «السابع» with one between the ب and the ع; the kashida is not transcribed. The latitude «مح» has a line above it, 94 px long, 75 px above the letters, transcribed with {ov:…}, as the latitudes of the earlier climes. L05, L14: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif. L13: «الى» ends in a ى without dots; «ثلثة» is printed without the dagger alif that frame 041 (L03) prints in «ثلٰثة»; «وستّين», with a shadda over the ت.
@obs The colours are «احمر», red (L01); «لازورد», lapis blue (L02, L08, L12), and «لازوردىّ», with a ى and a shadda (L06); «حديدىّ», iron-coloured (L03); «وردىّ», rose (L07, L09, L11, L15); and «اصفر كمد», dull yellow (L10). The directions are «شمال», north (L01, L06, L07, L10, L11), «جنوب», south (L02, L08, L09, L12, L15), and «مغربىّ», western (L03). «لازوردىّ», «حديدىّ», «وردىّ» and «مغربىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it.
@obs L03: the note mark b stands after the minutes of the last latitude, «لز», the mark c after the colour and the mark d after the direction.
