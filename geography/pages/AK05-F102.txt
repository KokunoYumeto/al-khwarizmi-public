@obs The islands in the Green Sea go on from p. 95 (frame 101), whose last words, «جزيرة مقدار جزء ونصف», L01 continues with «فى جزء وسطها عند طول ڡد م والعرض ٮٮ ى (١٤٦٧)», an island of one and a half degrees by one degree, its middle at the longitude ڡد م and the latitude ٮٮ ى. Then single islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٤٦٨)-(١٤٨١): «جزيرة مقدار جزءين وربع فى عرض جزء ونصف» (L01-L02), of two and a quarter degrees in a breadth of one and a half degrees; of two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L03, L04, L09); of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L05); «جزيرة مربّعة ثلٰثة اجزاء فى ثلٰثة اجزاء» (L05-L06), a square island of three degrees by three degrees, without «مقدار»; of one degree, «جزء» (L07); of one and a quarter degrees by one degree, «جزء وربع فى جزء» (L08); of one degree by as much again, «جزء فى مثله» (L10); of a sixth of a degree, «سدس جزء» (L11); of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L12); of one and a half degrees by one degree, «جزء ونصف فى جزء» (L13); of one and a half degrees by as much again, «جزء ونصف فى مثله» (L14); and of one and a quarter degrees by as much again, «جزء وربع فى مثله» (L15-L16).
