@obs The running text goes on from p. 71 (frame 077), whose last word, «والعرض», L01 continues with the latitude «ن م»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٠٧٨)-(١٠٩٨), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٦٦٢]-[٦٨٤], without a gap. L01: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript is printed in Latin type within the line, at its end after [٦٦٣], as «(fol, 18ᵃ)», with a comma after «fol»; transcribed as printed, as a margin note before the line (see the critical note).
@obs The places of the coast: L02: «ويرجع الى», it returns to, and «يمرّ بأسفل مدينة راسنياس», it passes below the town of راسنياس; L03: «على صورة الطيلسان بمصبّ تسعة انهار», along the shape of the ṭaylasān, at the mouth of nine rivers; L04: «يمرّ الى اسفل مدينة اسطورا عند طول», below the town of اسطورا, at the longitude; L05-L06: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة القسطنطينيّة ويصير الى», below the town of Constantinople, and comes to; L06-L07: «ويمرّ الى اسفل مدينة اود عند طول», below the town of اود; L10-L11: «يماسّ مدينة اسلينو», it touches the town of اسلينو; L14-L15: «وفيه مصبّ نهرين», and in it is the mouth of two rivers. «يمرّ», «ويمرّ», «بمصبّ» and «مصبّ» are printed with a shadda; «بأسفل» with a hamza over the alif; «القسطنطينيّة» with a shadda over the ي; «يماسّ» with the two dots of the ي and a shadda over the س.
@obs L15-L16: two points stand between angle brackets, from «⟨يمرّ» (L15) to «[٦٨٣]⟩» (L16), followed by the note mark o: the editor supplies them from S, which the manuscript lacks (note o). Their running numbers carry a stroke and a sub-number, (١٠٩٧/١) and (١٠٩٧/٢), and they have the numbers of Suhrāb [٦٨٢] and [٦٨٣].
@obs L02: the degrees of the longitude, «ٮب»: a tooth without its dot joined to a final ب with its dot below the left part of the bowl, 64 px wide, where the isolated ب is a bowl of about 50 px with a riser at each end (frame 113, L03); read with a ن, 52° 45′, beside the longitudes 51° 45′ (L01) and «ٮا {z}» (L03), which read as نا gives 51° 0′, and the town راسنياس, which the point passes, at the longitude 52° 10′ (frame 043, L01).
