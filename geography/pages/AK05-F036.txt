@obs L01: «منانبا»: after the alif of «منا», a tooth with a dot above it and a low tooth with a dot below it, ن and ب, joined to the final alif.
@obs L03: «ٮاٮٮدون»: the tooth joined to the alif has no dot; after it a tooth, 15 px above the line, and a low tooth, 5 px, joined to the د, without dots, as in the form the editor's note b gives from entry (2101); the final ن has its dot.
@obs Names printed without dots, transcribed so: L09: «ٮاذر», the tooth before the alif without its dot, the ذ with its dot; L10: «ٮرحان», the first letter a tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, where the isolated ر of the font is a plain curved stroke (frame 021, L08); L11: «اڡوان» and L13: «اڡذورس», the loop of ف or ق without dots.
