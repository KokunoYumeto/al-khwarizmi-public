@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 54-55. L01-L16 continue the same lines of p. 54 (frame 060), entries 775/776-805/806; L17, entry 807/808, stands at the height of p. 54, L18, the second line of its description, and the page has no line at the height of p. 54, L17. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads.
@obs The colours are «احمر», red (L01, L03, L06); «وردىّ», rose (L02, L05); «لكّىّ», lac red (L04); «اصفر», yellow (L07, L11-L13); «احمر رمّانىّ», pomegranate red (L08); «لازورد», lapis blue (L09, L14); «ملوّن», many-coloured (L10); «رمادىّ», ash-grey (L15); «اصفر كمد», dull yellow (L16); and «احمر مشبع», deep red (L17). The directions are «مغربىّ», western (L01, L04), «شمال», north (L02, L03, L07-L11, L13, L15, L17), «جنوب», south (L05, L06, L12, L14), and «مشرقىّ», eastern (L16).
@obs Signs in the colours and directions, transcribed as printed: «وردىّ», «رمادىّ», «مغربىّ» and «مشرقىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it; «لكّىّ» has a shadda over the ك and another over the ى, as on frame 047 (L11); «رمّانىّ», a shadda over the م and another over the ى; «ملوّن», a shadda over the و. L17: a small mark, 5 by 6 px, stands at the upper left of the alif of «احمر», much smaller than the hamza of the font (15 by 13 px, frame 046, L06); it is not transcribed.
@obs L06: the note mark d stands after the colour, «احمر». L11: the minutes of the first longitude are printed «⟨{z}⟩», the zero between angle brackets, followed by the note mark e; L12: the degrees of the first latitude «⟨لر⟩», followed by the mark f; L13: the degrees of the first longitude «س⟨ه⟩», an initial س and a ه between angle brackets, followed by the mark g, and the degrees of the first latitude «⟨لر⟩», followed by a second mark g. The editor supplies what stands between the brackets from S (notes e, f and g). Transcribed with both brackets in the cell.
