@obs The table of the mountains goes on across the opening pp. 56-57: each row of p. 56 is continued by a row of p. 57 (frame 063), which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. The description of entry 809/810 takes two printed lines, L01-L02; the second line has no numbers and is transcribed as a row with empty number cells; its boundaries, colour and direction stand on p. 57 at the height of L02, as on the openings pp. 52-55 (frames 058-061).
@obs L12-L13: the headings of the sixth clime and of its mountains, «الإقليم السادس» and «الجبال الّتى فى», are set in the large type at the inner edge of the page and continued on p. 57 by «عرضه مه *», its latitude, 45, and «الإقليم السادس *». Unlike the headings of the earlier climes (frames 044, 048, 052, 056, 058), they begin without a star; their ends on p. 57 close with one. Transcribed as headings. L12: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif; L13: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل, and «فى», with the dot of the ف and no dots on the ى.
@obs The running numbers go on from entry 807/808 (frame 060) and run (٨٠٩ ٨١٠)-(٨٣٥ ٨٣٦). The numbers of Suhrāb are [١٢٩٩ ١٣٠٠]-[١٣١١ ١٣١٢] (L01-L08), [١٣١٥ ١٣١٦] (L10), [١٣١٣ ١٣١٤] (L11) and [١٣١٧ ١٣١٨]-[١٣٢٣ ١٣٢٤] (L14-L17); entry 823/824 (L09) has none.
@obs The descriptions: L01-L02, a mountain joined to this mountain, which runs to Dunbāwand between Qazwīn and al-Rayy; L04, the mountain of Ṭabaristān, which runs between Jurjān and Nīsābūr; L06, a mountain that runs between this mountain and Samarqand; L07, L09, a mountain joined to this mountain; L16, a mountain joined to the sea, in which is the temple of Venus, «هيكل الزهرة». L03, L05, L08, L17: the word «جبل» alone.
@obs L04: «⟨في⟩»: the word stands between angle brackets, followed by the note mark c: the editor supplies it (note c). It is printed with the two dots of the final ي, where the ى of «فى» elsewhere has none (L13).
@obs L10: «عزن»: an initial ع, joined to a ز, and a final ن with its dot. A single dot, 10 by 10 px, stands over the joint of the ع and the ز, nearer to the head of the ز. Transcribed عزن.
@unres L10: the one dot over «عزن» may also belong to the ع, which would then be a غ, and the ز a ر: غرن. Transcribed عزن.
@obs L11: «اوقرقوس»: after «او», a ق with its two dots joined to a letter that descends below the line with only a slight rise at its head, 4 px, a ر; then a ق with its two dots joined to a و, whose head is a loop rising 8 px above the joining stroke; then a final س. Transcribed اوقرقوس.
@obs L14: «اوٮد»: after «او», a tooth without a dot joined to a د. L15: «ٮوٮدوغز»: a tooth without a dot, 15 px high, joined to a و; a tooth without a dot joined to a د; a و; and «غز», the غ and the ز with their dots.
@obs Signs transcribed as printed: «متّصل» (L01, L07, L09, L16), with a shadda above the two dots of the ت; «بهٰذا» (L01, L07, L09) and «هٰذا» (L06), with the dagger alif above the ه, transcribed ٰ; «يمرّ» (L01, L04, L06), with a shadda over the ر; «والرىّ» (L02), with a shadda over the final ى, which has no dots; «الى» (L01) ends in a ى without dots. With their dots: «دنباوند» (L01), the ن above and the ب below; «قزوين» (L02); «طبرستان», «جرجان» and «ونيسابور» (L04); «وسمرقند» (L06); «فيه هيكل الزهرة» (L16).
