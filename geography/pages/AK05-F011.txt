@obs The table goes on from frame 010 without a heading: the cities of the first clime, entries 19-34. The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 3ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type in the left margin beside entry 34 (L16).
@obs L06: the latitude of Ṣanʿāʾ is printed «⟨ٮد ل⟩», the degrees and the minutes within one pair of angle brackets, the whole latitude supplied by the editor (note f); it is transcribed with the opening bracket in the degrees cell and the closing one in the minutes cell.
@obs L03: the minutes of the latitude are printed «ي⟨ه⟩»: the ي stands outside the angle brackets, and only the ه is supplied (note c).
@obs L04: above the final س of الفس stands a short stroke, 16 px long, slanting down to the left, in the shape and place of a fatḥa; transcribed as a fatḥa, «الفسَ». The editor's note mark d stands after it.
@obs Letters printed without their dots, transcribed so: سماٮ (L01, the final letter); ڡٮاٮا (L15), the name of entry 30, قنانا (L12), printed without its dots, which the editor's note h identifies with entry 33; in the numerals ٮح (L01, the degrees of the longitude; L05, L10, the degrees of the latitude, where L01 prints يح with the two dots of the ي below the tooth), سٮ (L02), ٮد (L06) and عٮ (L14, L15), the tooth or the final ب without a dot; ٮ (L12, L15), the isolated ب without its dot, with one riser at the right end of the bowl; ٮٮ (L04, L08, L13, L16), a tooth joined to a final ب, both without dots, with two risers at the right end of the bowl, a tall and a short one, as يٮ and يب on frame 013 (L14, L11); ٮه (L08, the minutes of the longitude; L09, the degrees of the latitude), a tooth joined to a final ه, without its dot, the form printed with its dots in يه (L02-L04, L16). The longitude of entry 30 (L12), عب, has its dot.
@obs The degrees of the latitude of entries 20 (ح), 29 (و), 30 and 33 (ٮ) and 32 (⟨د⟩) are printed with a single letter, a number below 10, where the latitude of the first clime is 16° 27' (frame 010, L01), entries 19 and 21 have يح and يه, entries 23 and 28 ٮح, entries 22, 26, 31 and 34 ٮٮ, and entry 27 ٮه, which read with a ي gives 15; the editor's notes do not comment on them.
