@obs The springs and rivers beyond the seventh clime go on from p. 157 (frame 163), whose last point, (2390), ends with an asterisk. L01-L04: «نهران من جبل ڡرمانيا اوّل احدهما عند طول ⟨عح {z}⟩ والعرض نا {z} (٢٣٩١) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول عط ل والعرض مح ل (٢٣٩٢) ومخرج الثانى عند طول عح م والعرض ٮا ل (٢٣٩٣) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ف م والعرض ⟨مط {z}⟩ (٢٣٩٤) *», two rivers from the mountain ڡرمانيا: the beginning of one of them is at the longitude ⟨78° 0′⟩ and the latitude 51° 0′, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 79° 30′ and the latitude 48° 30′; the source of the second is at the longitude 78° 40′ and the latitude ٮا ل, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude 80° 40′ and the latitude ⟨49° 0′⟩.
@obs L01: «ڡرمانيا»: a loop without dots, 16 px wide with a white counter, on a short stem, joined at the line to the small pointed head of a ر, 6 to 7 px wide, which runs down into its tail; then م, ا, ن with its dot, ي with its two dots and a final alif. A و would have its round head over the tail, with no second head beside it. The mountain is entries 925-926 of the table of the mountains, «جبل قرمانيا» (frame 068, L17), whose boundaries are at 77° 15′ and 50° 10′ and at 79° 0′ and 52° 0′ (frame 069, L17); the editor's note n there gives the form of this entry (2391) with the same loop and ر.
@obs L04-L06: «نهر من جبل روميقا اوّله عند طول ڡح {z} والعرض نز {z} (٢٣٩٥) ومصبّه فى ⟨البحر⟩ عند طول ڡد {z} والعرض ن ك (٢٣٩٦) *», a river from the mountain روميقا: its beginning is at the longitude ڡح ō and the latitude 57° 0′, and its mouth in ⟨the sea⟩ at the longitude ڡد ō and the latitude 50° 20′. L06 holds only the latitude, the numbers and the asterisk, at the right. The mountain is entries 927-928 of the table of the mountains, «جبل روميقا» (frame 068, L18), whose first boundary is at the longitude فح ō, 88° 0′, and the latitude ٮز ō, and whose last at 98° 25′ and ٮه ō (frame 069, L18).
@obs L07-L10: «نهر من جبل زوميڡا اوّله عند طول ڡط {z} ⟨والع⟩رض ٮو مه (٢٣٩٧) ومصبّه فى البحر عند طول ڡه ل والعرض ٮا {z} (٢٣٩٨) *», a river from the mountain زوميڡا: its beginning is at the longitude ڡط ō and the latitude ٮو مه, and its mouth in the sea at the longitude ڡه ل and the latitude ٮا ō; «يقع اليه نهر من جبل ⟨رومي⟩ڡا اوّله عند طول صا {z} والعرض ٮو ه (٢٣٩٩) ومصبّه فى النهر عند طول ڡح {z} ⟨والع⟩رض ٮد {z} (٢٤٠٠) *», into it falls a river from the mountain ⟨رومي⟩ڡا: its beginning is at the longitude 91° 0′ and the latitude ٮو ه, and its mouth in the river at the longitude ڡح ō and the latitude ٮد ō. «زوميڡا» (L07), with the dot of the ز, the two dots of the ي and a loop without dots before the final alif, and «⟨رومي⟩ڡا» (L09), whose first letters the editor supplies, are the forms of the mountain روميقا at the entries (2397) and (2399) that the editor's note o on frame 068 records, the second there with a gap of six dots.
@obs L10-L12: «ويقع الى النهر الأوّل ايضاً نهر من جبل ارساٮا اوّله عند طول صر {z} والعرض ٮو {z} (٢٤٠١) ومصبّه فى النهر الأوّل عند طول فز ك والعرض ٮح {z} (٢٤٠٢) *», into the first river falls also a river from the mountain ارساٮا: its beginning is at the longitude صر ō and the latitude ٮو ō, and its mouth in the first river at the longitude 87° 20′ and the latitude ٮح ō. «ارساٮا»: the tooth before the final alif has no dot. The mountain is entries 931-932 of the table of the mountains, «جبل ارسانا» (frame 070, L02), whose boundaries are at 95° 40′ and 55° 10′ and at 100° 50′ and 59° 20′ (frame 071, L02); the editor's note a there gives this form at the entry (2401).
@obs L13: «* تمّ الكتاب *», the book is finished, centred below the text, between two small open stars, with the note mark e after «الكتاب»: the end of al-Khwārizmī's text. «تمّ» has a shadda over the م. The rest of the page is blank down to the editor's footnotes.
