@obs The table goes on from frame 033 without a heading: the last cities of the fifth clime, entries 401-411. Entries 409 and 410 have no number of Suhrāb; the others have [٣٥٢]-[٣٥٩] (entries 401-408), [٢٩١] (entry 411) and [٣٧٠] (entry 412).
