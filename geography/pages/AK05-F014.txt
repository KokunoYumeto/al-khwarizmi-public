@obs The table goes on from frame 013 without a heading: the last four cities of the first clime, entries 69-72; entry 72 has no number of Suhrāb. Then the heading of the second clime and of its cities, set between small open stars, as on frame 010; the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 3ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside the first heading (L05), where on frames 009 and 011 it stands in the left margin.
@obs L05: the latitude of the second clime, «كد ō», 24° 0', is printed with a line above the numeral and the zero sign, as the latitude of the first clime on frame 010; transcribed with {ov:…}. In both headings the alif of الإقليم carries a hamza below, printed under the ligature لا; in the first it is small, 13 px wide. L06: «الّتى», with a shadda over the ل.
@obs A small round dot, 13 px across, stands in the space between the two headings, below the end of عرضه; it belongs to neither line and is not transcribed.
@obs L09: an asterisk stands after مدينة, «حرمى مدينة* الكبيرة», raised like the editor's note marks. With it the editor marks the forms against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript, among them an indefinite noun with a definite attribute (introduction, frame 184, p. XXIX), as مدينة الكبيرة here; transcribed as printed.
@obs Letters printed without their dots, transcribed so: in the numerals the loop of ف or ق without dots, ڡعه, ڡعو, ڡعر (L01-L03); the tooth without its dots, ٮح (L07, L08, L11), ٮط (L09), ٮه (L02, the minutes of the longitude; L13, L15, the degrees of the longitude), a tooth joined to a final ه, and ٮو (L14, the degrees of the longitude), a tooth joined to a و; and the isolated ب without its dot, with one riser at the right end of its bowl, ٮ (L03). Where the print dots the numerals they are transcribed with their dots: يط (L10), يد (L12).
@obs L12: the degrees of the longitude, يد: a small dot, 7 px across and lighter than the dots of the font, stands 13 px above the tooth of the ي, whose two dots stand below it; it is not transcribed.
@obs L11, L14: the degrees of the latitude are printed «كٮ», a long oblique stroke over a flat bowl, 88 and 103 px wide, without the small inner stroke of the isolated ك: the ك joined to the final ب without its dot, 22. The isolated ك, 20, is about 35 to 38 px wide and has the small inner stroke (L08, the minutes of the latitude); the ك joined to the final ب with its dot, «كب», is 81 to 89 px wide (frame 085, L02; frame 089, L01). Frame 085 prints «كب ك», with the dot, 22° 20′, as the latitude of a point of the coast (L02), and the same group without the dot, «كٮ ك», for a later point between 24° 0′ (L05) and 21° 0′ (L08) (L06); the latitude of the third clime is «ل كٮ», 30° 22′ (frame 017, L13), as the first clime has 16° 27′ (frame 010, L01). Read so, the latitudes of Atfuwā and Suwān are 22° 45′ and 22° 30′. The isolated ه, 5 (L01, the degrees of the latitude), is a small round ring; «ٮه» (L02, L13, L15) is the tooth of a ن or a ي joined to a final ه, the form that frame 013 (L04) prints with the dot of the ن, نه.
@obs L13-L15: the degrees of the longitudes of Bilāq, Suwān and al-Qaṣr are printed «ٮه», «ٮو» and «ٮه», a tooth without its dot joined to the next letter; read with a ن they give 55° 25', 56° 0' and 55° 50', beside the longitude of the next places on the Nile, Isnā and Armant, ٮو, 56 (frame 015, L01, L02).
