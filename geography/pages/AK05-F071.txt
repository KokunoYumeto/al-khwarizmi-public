@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 64-65: each of its lines continues the same line of p. 64 (frame 070), L01-L19, entries 929/930-965/966. Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads.
@obs The colours are «لازورد مخيّر», lapis blue, mukhayyar (L01, L09); «اصفر مخيّر», yellow, mukhayyar (L02, L11, L19); «حديدىّ مخيّر», iron-coloured, mukhayyar (L03, L12); «وردىّ», rose (L04, L13); «لازورد», lapis blue (L05, L10, L15); «اصفر», yellow (L06, L14); «زيتىّ», olive-coloured (L07); «احمر مشبع», deep red (L08); «اسود مخيّر» (L16) and «اسود مخير» (L17), black, mukhayyar; and «حديدىّ», iron-coloured (L18). The directions are «شمال», north (L01-L15, L17), «مغربىّ», western (L16, L18), and «مشرقىّ», eastern (L19). «وردىّ», «حديدىّ», «زيتىّ», «مغربىّ» and «مشرقىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it; «مخيّر» has a shadda over the ي, except in L17, where it has none. L18: the shadda over the ى of «مغربىّ» is printed as a thin bar, 15 by 3 px, where L16 has the full sign, 15 by 10 px.
@obs L02: the note mark b stands after the minutes of the first longitude, «م»; L05: the mark d after the minutes of the first longitude, «كه»; L08: the mark g after the minutes of the first longitude, «يه».
