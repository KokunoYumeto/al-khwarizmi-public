@obs L01: the heading of the new section, «* البحر المظلم *», the Dark Sea, in the large type, between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks; the page has no other heading.
@obs L02: «حدّه من حدّ بحر الصين اوّله عند طول قسد {z} والعرض ى {z} خلف خطّ الاستواء», its boundary is from the boundary of the sea of China; its beginning is at the longitude 164° 0′ and the latitude 0° 10′ beyond the equator. This first point has no running number; the editor's note a identifies it with point 1147 (frame 081, L13-L14). «حدّه» and «حدّ» have a shadda over the د, «اوّله» over the و, «خطّ» over the ط; «الاستواء» ends in an alif followed by a small hamza on the line (L02, L03, L04), where p. 75 (frame 081) prints «الاستوى*».
@obs L03: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 22ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, at its beginning, followed by the note mark b: folio 21 of the manuscript holds no text (note b); transcribed as a margin note before the line, with the mark b at the beginning of the line.
@obs L03: the passage «⟨يمرّ الى طول قسد {z} والعرض يج ل خلف خطّ الاستواء⟩», between angle brackets, with the note mark c before the closing bracket and d after it, is supplied by the editor from S, which shows how the sea of China and the Dark Sea are joined; the manuscript lacks it (note d). The editor's note c identifies the point with point 1146 (frame 081, L12-L13), whose latitude p. 75 prints «يح ل», 18° 30′; here the latitude is printed «يج», with the two dots of the ي below the tooth, merged into one bar, 17 by 8 px, and the dot of the ج in the bowl, 13° 30′. Transcribed as printed.
@obs L04: the words «⟨خلف خطّ الاستواء⟩», between angle brackets and followed by the note mark e, are supplied by the editor; the manuscript has the word «شمال», north, above the latitude (note e). L05: the latitude «⟨كح {z}⟩», between angle brackets, with its line inside them, followed by the note mark f, is restored by the editor after S where the manuscript has a gap (note f). L09: «و⟨يمرّ⟩», the word «يمرّ» between angle brackets and followed by the note mark h, is supplied by the editor, which the manuscript lacks (note h).
@obs L06: «ويماسّ جبل نهر سفس المحيط بياجوج», and it touches the mountain of the river سفس, which surrounds Gog, with a shadda over the س of «ويماسّ» and all the dots of «جبل», «نهر», «سفس» and «بياجوج». L05: «على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān; point 1263 (L05) is given by its latitude only, «يمرّ بعرض».
