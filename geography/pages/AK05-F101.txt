@obs The description of the island of p. 94 (frame 100, L15-L16) goes on: L01 begins with the running number of its last point there, (١٤٥٤), and the number of Suhrāb, [٩٧٤]; then «تمرّ الى طول عح {z} والعرض ط {z} (١٤٥٥)», to the longitude 78° 0′ and the latitude 9° 0′; and «وتصير الى الموضع الّذى ابتدأنا منه وهو عند طول عد ل والعرض ح ك» (L01-L02), and it comes to the place from which we began, which is at the longitude 74° 30′ and the latitude ح ك, without a running number; the editor's note a identifies it with point 1453. Here «ابتدأنا منه» stands in this order, where the other islands have «منه ابتدأنا».
@obs L02-L04: «سبع جزائر مقدار ربع جزء الى نصف جزء كلّها مقدار ثلٰثة اجزاء فى ثلٰثة اجزاء وسطها عند طول ڡب {z} والعرض ٮو ل (١٤٥٦)», seven islands of a quarter of a degree to half a degree, all of them within three degrees by three degrees, their middle at the longitude ڡب {z} and the latitude ٮو ل.
@obs L04-L06: «جزيرة الميذ وهى الكرل فيها ثلٰث مدن وسطها عند طول فر {z} والعرض ٮٮ {z} (١٤٥٧) [٩٧٦] وهى تحيط بالمدن الّتى فيها وبمصبّ انهارها وبجميع ما فيها», the island الميذ, which is الكرل, on which are three towns, its middle at the longitude 87° 0′ and the latitude ٮٮ ō; and it encloses the towns on it, the mouths of its rivers and all that is on it. «الميذ» has the two dots of the ي and the dot of the ذ; «الكرل» has no dots.
@obs L06-L15: single islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٤٥٨)-(١٤٦٦): of one and a half degrees by as much again, «جزء ونصف فى مثله» (L06); of three degrees by one and a quarter, «ثلٰثة اجزاء فى جزء وربع» (L07); of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L08, L09, L10, L12); of a third of a degree, «ثلث جزء» (L11); «جزيرة مدوّرة مقدار اربعة اجزاء وربع فى مثله» (L13), a round island of four and a quarter degrees by as much again, whose middle is at the longitude 101° 0′ and on the equator, «والعرض خطّ الاستواء» (L14), given in words in the place of the numerals; and of one and a half degrees by one and a quarter, «جزء ونصف فى جزء وربع» (L14-L15). L15 ends with «جزيرة مقدار جزء ونصف», continued on p. 96. Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs L07-L08: the note mark c stands after «جزيرة» of L07 and again after the latitude of L08: the text between the two marks, island 1459, stands in the margin of the manuscript (note c-c).
