@obs The description of the island ٮولى goes on from p. 87 (frame 093), whose last words, «تمرّ بمصبّ نهر وتصير», L01 continues with «الى طول»: its coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets. L01: «الى طول لب ك والعرض سح ى (١٣٤٧)», to the longitude 32° 20′ and the latitude سح ى; L01-L02: «تمرّ على صورة القوارة تمرّ بعرض سد م (١٣٤٨)», along the shape of the qawwāra, at the latitude 64° 40′ (point 1348, given by its latitude only); L02-L03: «وتصير الى طول كو ك والعرض سح {z} وهو الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا», and it comes to the longitude 26° 20′ and the latitude سح {z}, which is the place from which we began. This last point has no running number; the editor's note d identifies it with point 1344, the first point of the island (frame 093, L14-L15), at the longitude «كو ك» and the latitude «سج {z}», printed there with the dot of the ج.
@obs L03-L14: small islands, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», running numbers (١٣٤٩)-(١٣٦٠), without numbers of Suhrāb; each entry ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk. L03-L04: «خمسة عشر جزيرة صغار* فى مقدار جزءين ونصف فى جزءين وسطها عند طول لب ل والعرض سٮ {z}», fifteen small islands within a size of two and a half degrees by two degrees, their middle at the longitude 32° 30′ and the latitude سٮ {z}; then single islands of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L04, L05, L07, L08, L09, L10, L11), a third of a degree, «ثلث جزء» (L06), two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L10, L12), and a sixth of a degree, «سدس جزء» (L13).
@obs L12: the middle of island 1358 is not given: «وسطها عند ⟨. . . . . .⟩», six dots, evenly spaced, between angle brackets, followed by the note mark i: the manuscript has a gap there (note i). Transcribed with the dots and the brackets.
@obs L14-L16: «جزيرة سقيديا فيها مدينة اوّلها عند طول مب ل والعرض نط م», the island سقيديا, on which is a town; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 42° 30′ and the latitude 59° 40′; then «تمرّ على صورة القوارة تمرّ بعرض ٮط {z}» (L15), along the shape of the qawwāra at the latitude ٮط {z}; «وتصير الى مصبّ نهر عند طول مو {z} والعرض ٮط مه» (L15-L16), to the mouth of a river at the longitude 46° 0′ and the latitude ٮط مه; «وتمرّ على صورة القوارة تمرّ بعرض س ل» (L16), at the latitude 60° 30′, and the coast goes on to p. 89. «سقيديا» is printed with all its dots.
@obs The running numbers run (١٣٤٧)-(١٣٦٣) without a gap; the numbers of Suhrāb, [٩١٤]-[٩١٨], stand after (١٣٤٧), (١٣٤٨) and (١٣٦١)-(١٣٦٣).
