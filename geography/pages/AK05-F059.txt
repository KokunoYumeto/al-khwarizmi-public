@obs The page is the left-hand page of the opening pp. 52-53. L01-L12 give the boundaries, colours and directions of the mountains of p. 52 (frame 058), entries 749/750-771/772; the two entries whose descriptions take two lines on p. 52 have their row at the height of the second line: L03, entry 753/754, stands beside p. 52, L04, and L05, entry 757/758, beside p. 52, L07, and the page has no line at the height of p. 52, L03 and L06. L13, «عرضه ما *», ends the heading of p. 52, L15, the fifth clime and its latitude, 41; L14, «الإقليم الخامس *», ends «* الجبال الّتى فى», the mountains in the fifth clime; L15 gives the boundaries, colour and direction of entry 773/774 (p. 52, L17). Each row gives, from the right, the degrees and the minutes of the longitude and of the latitude of the first boundary, the same for the last boundary, the colour and the direction, in the columns of p. 39 (frame 045); the page has no heads. The headings stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their beginnings on p. 52; transcribed as headings.
@obs L13: «عرضه» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ض and the ه; the kashida is not transcribed. The latitude «ما» has a line above it, 93 px long, 36 px above the letters, transcribed with {ov:…}, as the latitudes of the earlier climes (frames 045, 049, 053, 057). L14: «الإقليم», with a hamza below the alif.
@obs The colours are «كحلىّ», kohl-dark (L01); «اصفر», yellow (L02); «وردىّ», rose (L03, L06, L09, L12, L15); «حديدىّ», iron-coloured (L04); «اصفر اغبر», yellow and dusty (L05); «عكر امغر», turbid and red-ochre (L07); «اغبر», dusty (L08); «احمر», red (L10); and «لازورد», lapis blue (L11). The directions are «شمال», north (L01, L02, L04, L05, L08, L10-L12), «مغربىّ», western (L03, L15), and «جنوب», south (L06, L07, L09). «كحلىّ», «وردىّ», «حديدىّ» and «مغربىّ» end in a ى without dots with a shadda over it; «اغبر» (L05, L08) has the dot of the غ above and the dot of the ب below, and «امغر» (L07) the dot of the غ.
