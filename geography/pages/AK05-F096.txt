@obs The islands in the sea of Tangier go on from p. 89 (frame 095), whose last word, «اربع», L01 continues with «جزائر كلّ جزيرة مقدار ثلث جزء فى ثلث جزء كلّها فى مقدار جزءين فى جزءين وسط ذٰلك عند طول كو م والعرض لح {z} (١٣٧٦)», four islands, each of the size of a third of a degree by a third of a degree, all of them within two degrees by two degrees, the middle of that at the longitude 26° 40′ and the latitude لح {z}.
@obs L02-L06: «جزيرة قيرش فيها مدينتان اوّلها عند طول كح ن والعرض لح ن», the island قيرش, on which are two towns; the first point of its coast is at the longitude كح ن and the latitude لح ن (point 1377); then «تمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», points 1378-1380, and «تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول كح ن والعرض لح ن», back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note d identifies it with point 1377. «قيرش» has the two dots of the ق, the two dots of the ي below and the three dots of the ش; the editor's note b gives the forms of the name at three other entries.
@obs L06-L09: «جزيرة سرحس فيها اربع مدن اوّلها عند طول كح ك والعرض له مه», the island سرحس, on which are four towns; the first point of its coast is at the longitude كح ك and the latitude 35° 45′ (point 1381); then points 1382-1384, and «وتمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأ⟨نا⟩ وهو عند طول كح ك والعرض له مه», back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note f identifies it with point 1381. «سرحس» has no dot in the ح.
@obs L10-L16: «اربع جزائر مقدار ثلثى جزء فيما بين طول لٮ {z} (١٣٨٥) الى طول لر ك (١٣٨٦) وهى صفّ واحد وأوسطها على عرض م {z}», four islands of the size of two thirds of a degree, between the longitude لٮ {z} and the longitude لر ك, in one row, their middles on the latitude 40° 0′; two islands of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L11, L12), points 1387 and 1388; «ثلث جزائر مقدار ثلث جزء ثلث جزء وهى فيما بين طول لج {z} (١٣٨٩) الى طول له ك (١٣٩٠) وسطها على عرض لد ل», three islands of a third of a degree by a third of a degree, between the longitude 33° 0′ and the longitude 35° 20′, their middle on the latitude 34° 30′; an island of a third of a degree, point 1391, and an island of half a degree, «نصف جزء» (L15-L16), point 1392; then «جزيرة ڡلڡوسس فيها مدينة», the island ڡلڡوسس, on which is a town, continued on p. 91. «ڡلڡوسس» has no dots: two closed loops, the second with a white counter, joined by the ل and followed by the و and the two س.
@obs The running numbers run (١٣٧٦)-(١٣٩٢) without a gap; the numbers of Suhrāb, [٩٢٨]-[٩٣٥], stand after (١٣٧٧)-(١٣٨٤), without a gap. Each island or group ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk (L02, L06, L09, L11-L16); the star of L02 has its inner ring broken open. L04: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 24ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between [٩٢٩] and «تمرّ»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs L09: «ابتدأ⟨نا⟩», the نا between angle brackets, followed by the note mark e; L15: «⟨طول⟩», between angle brackets, followed by the note mark h: the manuscript lacks the words between the brackets, and the editor supplies them (notes e and h). Transcribed with the brackets.
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «كلّها» (L01), «صفّ» (L11), «تمرّ», «وتمرّ» and «اوّلها» (L02, L06) with a shadda; «الّذى» (L05, L09) with a shadda over the ل; «ذٰلك» (L01) with a dagger alif above the ذ; «جزءين» (L01, twice) with a hamza between the ز and the ي; «جزء» with a hamza on the line after the ز; «ابتدأنا» (L05) and «ابتدأ» (L09) with a hamza over the alif; «وأوسطها» (L11) with a hamza over the alif; «الّذى», «فى», «ثلثى» and «وهى» end in a ى without dots.
