@obs The places in which the boundaries of the countries are written go on from p. 101 (frame 107), whose last words, «بلاد بمملكة البربر وسط الكتاب عند طول كو {z}», L01 continues with «والعرض ٮط {z} (١٥٥٢)», the latitude of the land in the kingdom of the Berbers. Then the countries, each with the longitude and latitude of the middle of its writing, «وسط كتابها عند طول … والعرض …» or «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …», with the degrees and the minutes written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}: «بلاد العربيّة العامرة وهى بلاد اليمن واليمامة والبحرين وعمان» (L01-L02), inhabited Arabia, which is the land of the Yemen, al-Yamāma, al-Baḥrayn and ʿUmān; «بلاد السند» (L03), Sind; «بلاد الهند الّتى خارج من نهر جنجس» (L03-L04), the land of India that lies beyond the river جنجس; «بلاد الصين» (L05), China; «بلاد طنجة» (L06), Tangier; «بلاد مرطانيه» (L06-L07); «بلاد افريقيّة» (L08), Ifrīqiya; «بلاد مرماوقى» (L08-L09); «بلاد مدين» (L09-L10), Midian; «بلاد كرمان» (L10-L11), Kirmān; «برّيّة كرمان» (L11-L12), the desert of Kirmān; «بلاد كابل» (L12-L13), Kābul; «بلاد سوريه وهى الشام» (L13), Syria, which is al-Shām; «بلاد فارس» (L14), Fārs; and «بلاد هراة» (L15), Herat, whose entry ends on p. 103. Each country ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs The running numbers run (١٥٥٢)-(١٥٦٦) and the numbers of Suhrāb [٥٠١]-[٥١٥], each after its running number, without a gap; [٥٠٤] stands at the beginning of L05. L07: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 28ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «والعرض» and the latitude «كو {z}»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
