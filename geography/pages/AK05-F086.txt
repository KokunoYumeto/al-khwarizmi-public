@obs L01-L06 end the Great Sea. The running text goes on from p. 79 (frame 085), whose last words, «ويمرّ بمدينة», L01 continues with the name «الفس»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٢٢٧)-(١٢٣٠), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٨١٣]-[٨١٦]. L02: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 20ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, between «والعرض» and the latitude «ٮح {z}»; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs The places of the coast: L01: «ويمرّ بمدينة مارا», past the town of مارا; L03: «يمرّ بمصبّ نهرين», past the mouth of two rivers; L04: «ويمرّ بجدّة والجار», past Jidda and al-Jār; L05: «يمرّ ⟨ب⟩قرب مدين وأسفل مدينة القلزم ويصير الى الموضع الّذى منه بدأنا», it passes near Madyan and the lower part of the town of al-Qulzum and comes to the place from which we began, L06: «عند طول نه ن والعرض كح {z}», at the longitude 55° 50′ and the latitude 28° 0′, the first point of the Great Sea, point 1121 (frame 080, L07), as the editor's note g says; this last point has no running number. The shapes of the coast are «القوارة» (L02-L03) and «الطيلسان» (L04-L05). L06 ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
@obs L07-L08: the heading of the next section, in the large type, between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks: «* بحر خوارزم وبحر جرجان وطبرستان والديلم واحد *», the sea of Khwārazm and the sea of Jurjān, Ṭabaristān and al-Daylam are one. «وطبرستان» is printed with a long kashida, the stretched joining stroke, between the ت and the alif, and «واحد» with one between the ح and the د; the kashidas are not transcribed.
@obs L09-L14: the course of the new sea from point to point, with the editor's running numbers (١٢٣١)-(١٢٣٩) and the numbers of Suhrāb [٨٣٩]-[٨٤٧]: [٨١٧]-[٨٣٨] do not appear on the page. L09: «الابتداء من طول عد م مماسّ* لجبل يون والعرض مح ه», the beginning is from the longitude 74° 40′, touching the mountain يون, and the latitude 48° 5′. This is entry (1231), at which, the editor notes on p. 58 (frame 064, note i), the mountain of the Gate of the Khazars and the Alans is called جبل يون.
@obs L09: «مماسّ*», with a shadda over the س: an asterisk with six solid arms stands after it, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10).
@obs Angle brackets, transcribed as printed: L03: the longitude is printed «س⟨ه {z}⟩», an initial س before the opening bracket and «ه {z}» within the brackets, all under one line, followed by the note mark c: the manuscript is damaged here, and the editor restores the text after S (note c). L05: «⟨ب⟩قرب», the ب between angle brackets, followed by the note mark e, before «قرب»: the editor supplies the preposition of «بقرب», near, which the manuscript lacks (note e).
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ», «ويمرّ» and «بمصبّ» with a shadda; «بجدّة» with a shadda over the د; «وأسفل» and «بدأنا» with a hamza over the alif; «الّذى» with a shadda over the ل; «الابتداء» with a hamza on the line after the alif. L01: «الفس», with the dot of the ف; the editor's note a gives the form of the manuscript, «القس». L04: «والجار», with the dot of the ج; L05: «قرب», «مدين» and «القلزم» with all their dots; L09: «لجبل يون», with the dots of the ج and of the ب, the two dots of the ي and the dot of the ن.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals, transcribed so: a tooth without its dot in the degrees of the latitude, «ٮج» (L01), joined to a ج with its dot, and «ٮح» (L02), which read with a ي give 13 and 18; «ڡا» (L12, the degrees of the longitude), an upright loop without a dot joined to an alif, 81 or 101, between the longitudes «عط», 79 (L11), and «فز», 87 (L13); «مٮ» (L14, the degrees of the latitude), the bowl of the ب without its dot, 42, where L13 prints «مب» with the dot.
