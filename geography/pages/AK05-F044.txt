@obs The table of the mountains is set across the opening of pp. 38-39: each line of p. 38 is continued by the same line of p. 39 (frame 045). P. 38 holds the numbers and the names; p. 39 the longitude and latitude of the first and of the last boundary of each mountain, its colour and a direction. The headings run across the opening in the same way: L01, «* الجبال الّتى خلف», is continued on p. 39 by «خطّ الاستواء *», the mountains beyond the equator; L13, «* الإقليم الأوّل», by «وعرضه ٮو *», the first clime and its latitude; and L14, «* الجبال الّتى فى», by «الإقليم الأوّل *», the mountains in the first clime. On p. 38 the headings stand at the inner edge of the page, where they continue; transcribed as headings.
@obs L02: the heads of the four number columns are printed vertically, reading upward, each over its column: from the right «(عدد الحدّ الأوّل)» and «(عدد الحدّ الآخر)», the number of the first and of the last boundary, between parentheses, and «[عدد الحدّ الأوّل]» and «[عدد الحدّ الآخر]» between square brackets; the head «الاسماء», the names, is printed horizontally, at the height of the middle of the vertical heads. Transcribed as one row of heads, the two heads between parentheses in the first cell and the two between square brackets in the second. «الحدّ» with a shadda over the د, «الأوّل» with a hamza over the alif and a shadda over the و, «الآخر» with a madda over the alif.
@obs Each mountain has two running numbers in one pair of parentheses, «(٥٤٠ ٥٤١)», the numbers of its first and its last boundary, and two numbers of Suhrāb in one pair of square brackets, «[١٠٣٩ ١٠٤٠]»; the running numbers go on from the last city, entry 539 (frame 043), and the numbers of Suhrāb run [١٠٣٩]-[١٠٦٠]. Each name begins with «جبل».
@obs The folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 11ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type in the right margin beside the heading of the first clime (L13).
@obs L04: «ٮانقلوس»: the first letter after «جبل» is a short tooth without a dot joined to the alif; the ن has its dot and the ق its two dots. L03: «لورحس» and L05: «حسفارس»: the ح has no dot. L12: «علدس»: after «علد», whose د is a short upright with its foot on the line, the س has a first stroke 20 px high and two low teeth before its bowl, the form of the isolated س on frame 043 (L07, «طاس»), where its first stroke is 13 px.
