@obs The islands in the sea of Tangier go on from p. 92 (frame 098). L01-L05: «جزيرة قبرس فيها ثلٰث مدن اوّلها عند طول ند ن والعرض له ى», the island قبرس, on which are three towns; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 54° 50′ and the latitude 35° 10′ (point 1425); then «تمرّ الى طول … والعرض …», point 1426, and point 1427, given by its longitude only, «تمرّ الى طول نح مه» (L02); «وتمرّ على صورة الطيلسان تمرّ بطول نط ō» (L03), along the shape of the ṭaylasān, at the longitude 59° 0′ (point 1428, given by its longitude only); «وتصير الى موضع ⟨طوله⟩ نح م والعرض له ٮه» (L03-L04), and it comes to a place whose longitude is نح م and latitude له ٮه (point 1429); and «تمرّ الى الموضع الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول ند ن والعرض له ى» (L04-L05), back to the place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note c identifies it with point 1425. «قبرس» has the two dots of the ق and the dot of the ب; the editor's note a gives the reading of the manuscript.
@obs L05-L08: «جزيرة اوّلها عند طول مو ن والعرض لط ى», an island without a name and without a size; the first point of its coast is at the longitude 46° 50′ and the latitude 39° 10′ (point 1430); then point 1431; «وتمرّ على صورة الطيلسان تمرّ بطول مح ن» (L06-L07), along the shape of the ṭaylasān, at the longitude مح ن (point 1432, given by its longitude only); and «وتصير الى موضعه الّذى منه ابتدأنا وهو عند طول مو ن والعرض لط ى» (L07-L08), and it comes to its place from which we began, without a running number; the editor's note d identifies it with point 1430. L08-L09: «جزيرة مقدار ثلث جزء وسطها عند طول مح ō والعرض م ل (١٤٣٣)», an island of a third of a degree, the last of the islands of the sea of Tangier.
@obs L10: the heading of the next section, in larger type, centred: «* جزائر بحر القلزم *», the islands of the sea of al-Qulzum, with the note mark e after «بحر» and a small open star with six points at each end.
@obs L11-L16: the islands of the sea of al-Qulzum, each with its size and the longitude and latitude of its middle, «وسطها عند طول … والعرض …»: of three quarters of a degree, «ثلٰثة ارباع جزء» (L11); of two thirds of a degree, «ثلثى جزء» (L12, L13, L14, L16); and of a quarter of a degree, «ربع جزء» (L15); running numbers (١٤٣٤)-(١٤٣٩). L16 ends with «جزيرة مقدار», continued on p. 94.
@obs L04: «⟨طوله⟩», between angle brackets, followed by the note mark b; L11: «⟨طول⟩», between angle brackets, followed by the note mark f: the manuscript lacks the words, and the editor supplies them (notes b and f). Transcribed with the brackets.
@obs The running numbers run (١٤٢٥)-(١٤٣٩) without a gap. The numbers of Suhrāb stand after (١٤٢٥)-(١٤٣٢), [٩٥٤]-[٩٦١], after (١٤٣٤) and (١٤٣٥), [٩٦٢] and [٩٦٣], and after (١٤٣٩), [٩٧٠]: they pass from 963 to 970; points 1433 and 1436-1438 have none. L02: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 25ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type at the beginning of the line; transcribed as a margin note before the line. Each island ends with a small open star with six points, transcribed as an asterisk.
