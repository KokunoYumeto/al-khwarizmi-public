@obs L01-L06: the description of the mountain that surrounds the island of the Yāqūt, «صفة الجبل المحيط بجزيرة الياقوت», is set as running text in the type of the tables, the full width of the page; it goes on at the top of p. 43 (frame 049, L01-L06), from page to page and not across the opening. The headings L07-L08 and the rows L09-L16 run across the opening as on pp. 38-39 (frames 044-045): L07, «* الإقليم الثانى», is continued on p. 43 by «عرضه كد *», the second clime and its latitude, 24; L08, «* الجبال الّتى فى», by «الإقليم الثانى *», the mountains in the second clime; and each row by the same row of p. 43, which gives the boundaries, the colour and the direction. The headings stand at the inner edge of the page, next to their ends on p. 43; transcribed as headings.
@obs L01: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 11ᵇ)», is printed in Latin type at the beginning of the line, within the text block, and is followed by a small open six-pointed star, transcribed as an asterisk, with which the description begins, as the headings L07 and L08 begin with the same star. The folio «(fol. 12ᵃ)» is printed in the right margin beside the heading of the second clime (L07).
@obs L01: «اقصا*», L02: «محيط*» and L03: «استوى*»: an asterisk with six round-ended arms, as on frame 046 (L10), stands after the word, raised: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (introduction, frame 184, p. XXIX). «اقصا» ends in an alif; «استوى», without the article, in a ى without dots.
@obs L02: «⟨وهو⟩»: the word stands between angle brackets, followed by the note mark a: the editor supplies it (note a).
@obs L02, L05: «المظلم», and L01: «المحيط»: the م after the ل of the article is a small loop at the foot of the ل, to the right of its stem, so that the following ظ or ح stands next to the ل; in «المشرق» (L01) the م is a loop on the line to the left of the ل.
@obs L03-L06: the coordinates in the text are written with letters, each pair of degrees and minutes with a line above it, transcribed with {ov:…}: L03, the longitude «ڡسر» with the zero, the latitude «ٮد ل» and the latitude «ط» with the zero; L04, the latitude «ه» with the zero and the longitude «ڡع» with the zero; L05, the latitude «د» with the zero and the longitude «ڡعٮ» with the zero; L06, the latitude «ح ل» and the longitude «ڡعح ل». The running numbers (٥٩٦)-(٦٠٠) and the numbers of Suhrāb [١٤٥٧]-[١٤٦١] follow the points of the description.
@obs Letters printed without their dots in the numerals of the text, transcribed so: the loop of ف or ق without dots in the longitudes, ڡسر (L03), ڡع (L04), ڡعٮ (L05), the last with a final ب without its dot, and ڡعح (L06); ٮد (L03), a tooth without its dot joined to a د, 14. In ڡسر the editor reads ر as ز, 7 (introduction, frame 196, p. XVII), so 167; transcribed ر as printed. The isolated ه of L04 is a small round ring, the round form of the letter. The ح of L06, alone and in ڡعح, shows no dot in the bowl; the same form is a ج without its dot, 3. Transcribed as printed.
