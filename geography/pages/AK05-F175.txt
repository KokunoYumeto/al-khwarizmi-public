@obs The plate reproduces in halftone the map of the Nile on fol. 30ᵇ and 31ᵃ of the manuscript, two facing pages, which the editor's list of plates (p. 162, frame 168) gives with south at the top. It is printed on a folding sheet wider than the page; no caption «TAFEL 3» is seen in the frame.
@obs The map shows at the right a half disc with streams that run from it into two round lakes, and from the lakes into one river, which winds to the left across straight lines with writing beside them, the boundaries of the climes, to a dark rectangle at the left edge; beside the river near the rectangle stands a dark triangle; writing stands along the river and beside the lakes. The text of the Nile, from the mountain of the Moon to the sea, is on pp. 106-109 (frames 112-115). The editor's list names three places of the map that the text does not name: «الفسطاط», «الجيزة» and «الريف».
@obs The plate is reproduced as an image (the crop of the frame and its derivative); its handwritten text is not transcribed.
