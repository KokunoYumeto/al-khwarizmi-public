@obs The table goes on from frame 041 without a heading: the places beyond the seventh clime, entries 511-526. Entry 517 has no number of Suhrāb; the others have [٤٦٨]-[٤٧٣] (entries 511-516) and [٤٧٤]-[٤٨٢] (entries 518-526). Entry 517 is the word «مدينة» alone, and entry 520 is «مدينة على جبل».
@obs L01: «اعسوٮڡٯ»: after «اعسو», the ع, the three teeth of a س and a و, a tooth without a dot; a loop with a counter, joined on the line, the loop of ف or ق without dots, as the ق of «سيقاں» (L14), where the م of this font is a small filled loop (L12, «الامسا»); then the final ق without its dots: two upright horns, 22 px high, joined in a band below their tips and open below it, the right one standing on the joining stroke, the left one running down into the bowl, the form of the ق with its two dots in «امق» (frame 016, L11); a final ن after a tooth has the tooth and the right side of the ن rising apart and meeting only on the line (frame 147, L05, «عين»).
@obs L02: «درڡطٯ»: د and ر; a loop without dots joined to the ط; after the ط, the final ق without its dots, as at the end of L01. The text prints the same form at point (2139), with the editor's variant reading درڡطس (frame 147, L10). The editor's note a gives the form of entry (2307), «درقطٮر», where the ق has its two dots and the name ends in a tooth joined to a ر.
@obs L04: «ڡاططر»: the loop of ف or ق without dots joined to the alif; then two ط and a ر, whose tail is printed apart from its head.
@obs L08: «السٮس»: after «ال», seven teeth before the bowl of the final س, a group of four and a group of three with a wider gap between them (16 px, where the teeth stand 10 to 13 px apart), where «السس» has six, three and three, as in the form that the editor's note f gives as a variant reading. Transcribed السٮس.
@unres L08: the seven teeth of «السٮس» are of nearly the same height (5 to 8 px), so it does not show which of the first four is the tooth added to the three of the س: the name may be read السٮس or الٮسس. Transcribed السٮس.
@obs L13: «اراٮٮاسا»: after «ارا», two teeth without dots joined to an alif, the first 17 px high and the second 7 px, the alif standing 31 px from the first tooth, where a single tooth joined to an alif stands some 12 px from it (frame 041, L04, «ٮالى»); then «سا», three teeth and an alif.
@obs L15: «ڡطرٮرا»: the loop of ف or ق without dots, a ط and a ر joined to it; then a short tooth without a dot joined to a ر, the two strokes forming a notch at the top, as on frames 015 (L03, L04) and 021 (L08); and the alif.
