@obs The running text goes on from p. 78 (frame 084), which ends with point (١٢٠٧) [٧٩٣]: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١٢٠٨)-(١٢٢٦), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٧٩٤]-[٨١٢], without a gap.
@obs The places of the coast: L02-L03: «يمرّ بمدينة النيرون ومدينة الديبل», past the town of al-Nīrūn and the town of al-Daybul; L03-L04: «ويمرّ بمدينة تيزونائن وسيراف وجنّابا ومهروبان وعبّادان», past the town of تيزونائن, Sīrāf, Jannāba, Mahrūbān and ʿAbbādān; L05: «يمرّ بمدينة البحرين», past the town of al-Baḥrayn; L08: «يمرّ بمدينة عمان», past the town of ʿUmān; L10: «ويمرّ بأسفل مدينة», below a town whose name is not given; L15-L16: «ويمرّ بمدينة ساوانى», past the town of ساوانى; L16: «ويمرّ بمدينة», continued on p. 80. The shapes of the coast are «الطيلسان» (L02, L05, L08, L09) and «القوارة» (L07, L12, L14, L15). Point 1214 (L07) is given by its longitude only, «يمرّ بطول», and points 1217 and 1221 (L09, L12) by their latitude only, «يمرّ بعرض».
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «يمرّ» and «ويمرّ» with a shadda; «وجنّابا» with a shadda over the ن and «وعبّادان» with a shadda over the ب; «بأسفل» with a hamza over the alif; «تيزونائن» with a hamza on the tooth before the final ن; «ساوانى» ends in a ى without dots. «النيرون», «الديبل», «سيراف» and «مهروبان» have all their dots.
@obs L03: «تيزونائن» is set without a word space: after the ز and after the و, which do not join the next letter, the gaps are 10 and 7 px, where the space between words is about 27 px. Transcribed as printed. The editor's note e gives the form of the manuscript for its last part only, «ناين».
@obs L01: the note mark b stands between the degrees and the minutes of the second longitude, «صب» and «كه», under the same line.
