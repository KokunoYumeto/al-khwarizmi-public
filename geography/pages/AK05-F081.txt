@obs The running text goes on from p. 74 (frame 080), whose last words, «ويمرّ على صورة», L01 continues with «الطيلسان»: the coast from point to point, with the degrees and the minutes of each longitude and latitude written with letters under one line, transcribed with {ov:…}, the editor's running numbers in parentheses, (١١٣٣)-(١١٥٠), and the numbers of Suhrāb in square brackets, [٧١٩]-[٧٣٦], without a gap. L06: the folio of the Strasbourg manuscript, «(fol. 19ᵃ)», is printed in Latin type within the line, at its beginning; transcribed as a margin note before the line.
@obs The places of the coast: L02: «وهٰذا الموضع هو اوّل حدّ بحر* الأخضر», this place is the first boundary of the Green Sea; L04: «يمرّ بأسفل مدينة عند طول», below a town whose name is not given; L05-L06: «ويمرّ على صورة القوارة ⟨بقر⟩ب اسفل مدينة الطيب ومماسّ* لأسفل مدينة فتانا», along the shape of the qawwāra near the lower part of the town of al-Ṭīb, and touching the lower part of the town of فتانا; L08: «ويمرّ الى اسفل مدينة راڡاطا», to below the town of راڡاطا; L13: «وهٰذا الموضع مماسّ للبحر المظلم ويمرّ البحر الأخضر الى», this place touches the Dark Sea, and the Green Sea passes to; L15-L16: «يمرّ على صورة الطيلسان», along the shape of the ṭaylasān, and «ويمرّ على صورة», continued on p. 76.
@obs L07-L09: the latitudes of points 1140-1142 are said to be «خلف خطّ الاستوى*» (L07) and «خلف الاستوى*» (L08, L09), beyond the equator. L10-L11: a remark between two small open stars with six points, transcribed as asterisks: «* وهٰذه العروض الّتى نذكرها هى خلف خطّ الاستوى* الى ان تجوز⟨ه⟩ فنذكر ذٰلك *», and these latitudes that we give are beyond the equator, until you pass it; then we shall say so.
@obs L02: «بحر*»; L06: «ومماسّ*»; L07, L08, L09, L10: «الاستوى*»: an asterisk with six solid arms stands after the word, raised like the note marks: the editor's sign for a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (frame 046, L10). L13: «مماسّ» has no asterisk.
@obs Angle brackets, transcribed as printed: L04: the latitude «⟨ه يه⟩», under its line with the brackets, followed by the note mark c: the manuscript is badly damaged there, and the editor supplies it from S (note c). L11: «تجوز⟨ه⟩», followed by the note mark g: the editor supplies the pronoun, which the manuscript lacks (note g).
@obs Signs in the text, transcribed as printed: «وهٰذا» (L02, L13), «وهٰذه» (L10) and «ذٰلك» (L11) with a dagger alif above the ه or the ذ; «اوّل», «حدّ», «خطّ», «مماسّ», «يمرّ» and «ويمرّ» with a shadda; «الأخضر» (L02, L13) and «لأسفل» (L06) with a hamza over the alif; «بأسفل» (L04) with a hamza over the alif; «الّتى» with a shadda over the ل; «الاستوى» and «هى» end in a ى without dots.
