# al-Khwārizmī, *Kitāb ṣūrat al-arḍ* — Mžik's edition (1926) in the Baghdad reprint (1962), line by line

Muḥammad ibn Mūsā al-Khwārizmī's *Book of the image of the earth*, his tables of the coordinates of cities, mountains, seas, islands and rivers, drawn from Ptolemy's *Geography*. It is transcribed from the edition of Hans von Mžik (Leipzig: Otto Harrassowitz, 1926; Bibliothek arabischer Historiker und Geographen 3), from the Strasbourg manuscript, in the offset reprint of Baghdad, 1962, as held by Columbia University Libraries (G93 .K48) and photographed by Arabic Collections Online (columbia_aco004109; [hdl.handle.net/2333.1/xd254h2x](https://hdl.handle.net/2333.1/xd254h2x)) in 218 frames. Every printed line and every row of the tables of al-Khwārizmī's text is one line here, keyed to its frame and line (AK05-F###-L##).

## What is reproduced

- **al-Khwārizmī's text** as the edition prints it: the headings, the names, and the coordinates of the tables, which are written with letters (the abjad numerals), cell by cell; the sign for zero of the tables, a small circle with a stroke above it, is written `{z}`. Hamza, shadda and the other signs are transcribed where they are printed.
- **The editor's apparatus in the text**: his running numbers in parentheses, the numbers of the same entries in Suhrāb's *ʿAjāʾib al-aqālīm* in square brackets, the angle brackets with which he marks what he supplies, the asterisk with which he marks a form against the grammar of the classical language that he keeps from the manuscript (introduction, p. XXIX), his note marks, and the folios of the manuscript that he prints in the margin.
- **The numerals** of the tables are written with letters (abjad). The editor keeps the defective writing of the manuscript (introduction, p. XXI), and so does the transcription: the loop of ف or ق printed without dots is written ڡ, the final ب without its dot ٮ, and ر where the print has ر for 7 (p. XVII). A tooth without its dot is written ٮ also after the loop of ق or ف, where it is a short spike above the joining stroke with no part below it (ڡٮح, قٮط), and the medial م a diamond that stands out above and below the stroke, or a م set under the ق. A tooth without its dot joined to a final ه is written ٮه: it is the form that the print gives with the dot of the ن as نه, 55, and with the two dots of the ي as يه, 15; the isolated ه, 5, is a small round ring. Letters printed with a line below them are written `{u:…}`.
- **The title pages** of the reprint and of 1926, Arabic and German, the series title page and the imprint, line by line.
- **The five plates**, the manuscript's maps and its last written page, as images: an untouched crop of each frame and a grey derivative at half size, in `figures/`, with their coordinates, operations and hashes in `figure_provenance.tsv`. Their handwritten text is not transcribed.
- **Errors of the print** are kept in the line. A critical note, kept apart from the transcription (`critical_findings.tsv` and the last page of the PDF), proposes the correction with its evidence. So far there are twenty-two. Five are the editor's own corrections, from his additions and corrections on p. 163 (frame 169): the name of entry 225, printed ٮرقه, is برقة (frame 23, L13); the island of entry 248, printed قٮرس, is قبرس (frame 24, L18); the name of entry 275 is المصّيصة, with a shadda (frame 26, L08); the degrees of the latitude of al-Andalus, entry 419, are لح, 38, not لج, 33 (frame 35, L07); and the latitude of point 1221 of the coast of the Great Sea, printed بٮ ن, is يٮ ن, 12° 50′ (frame 85, L12). The other seventeen: the running number of entry 280 is printed (٧٨٠), between (٢٧٩) and (٢٨١) (frame 26, L13); the bracket that opens the number of Suhrāb of entry 349 has the form of the closing one (frame 30, L14); the middle digit of the running number of entry 384 has not printed (frame 32, L17); the direction of entry 675/676 is printed جبوب, with the dot of the ن below the line, for جنوب, south (frame 53, L11); the degrees of the first longitude of entry 931/932 are printed ضه, 805, with a small dot over the ص, for صه, 95 (frame 71, L02); the folio «(fol. 18ᵃ)» is printed with a comma after «fol» (frame 78, L01); ## What is not reproduced

- **Hans von Mžik's German introduction and footnotes.** They are his own writing; he died in 1961, so they are protected in the EU until the end of 2031. Each page records where they stand and how many printed lines they take; each page of the introduction also names the topics that it treats and the entries and notes that it cites. Readings of the manuscript that the footnotes report are recorded in our own words (`witness_variants.tsv`).
- **Copy matter** of the Columbia copy: the binding, the stamps and the pencilled numbers (`copy_provenance.tsv`).
