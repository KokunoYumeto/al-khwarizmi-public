I. Arabic (and some Persian) words
@c 0275L03
**A.**
@e 0275L04
*Aakrab* see *Akrab*.
@e 0275L05..0275L08
*Aalemet* = *ʿalāmāt* (pl. of *ʿalāma*) *Notulae*, signs, marks, signatures, here the numbers of the days of the week (1 . . . 7 = Sunday to Saturday), T. 69.
@e 0275L09
*Aard* see *Ard*.
@e 0275L10..0275L11
*Abaad* = *abʿad*, farther away (*longinquior*), T. 73, 74.
@e 0275L12
*Achir* = *ākhir*, (the) last, second, T. 2, 3 etc.
@e 0275L13..0275L15
*Acofra* = *aṣ-ṣufr* (*Ta’rīkh aṣ-ṣufr*), the Spanish era, also called simply the era (in the text *Era*), 3, 35, T. 1.
@e 0275L16
*Acrab* see *Akrab*.
@e 0275L17..0275L19
*Aded* and *Adet* = *ʿadad*, number, T. 21 etc.; perhaps it sometimes also means the plural *aʿdād*.
@e 0275L20
*Adel* (?) T. 10.
@e 0275L21
*Adhel* = *al-dhel*, see *Dhel*.
@e 0275L22
*Aguze* = *adjzā’* (pl. of *djuz*), parts, T. 73.
@e 0275L23..0275L24
*Ahemar* = *aḥmar*, red, here also the name of the planet Mars, besides *Mirrīkh*, T. 39–42.
@e 0275L25
*Ajuze* see *Aguze*.
@e 0275L26..0275L28
*ʿAḳd*, node (of the orbits of the planets); *ʿaḳd al-shamālī*, the northern node (of the orbit of the moon) 41.
@e 0275L29..0275L31
*Akrab*, 1) = *aḳrab*, nearer (*propinquior*) T. 75, 76; 2) = *ʿaḳrab*, scorpion (the 8th sign of the zodiac) T. 59$^{b}$.
@e 0275L32..0275L33
*Anāhīd*, Persian name of the planet Venus, XXII, T. 45.
@e 0275L34
*Arafa* (?) 15 (N. 20).
@e 0275L35..0275L36
*Ard* = *ʿarḍ*, latitude of a heavenly body, or geographical latitude of a place, 19 (N. 1), T. 22, 79.
@e 0275L37
*Arhik* T. 15 (N. 7) see *Achir*.
@e 0275L38..0275L39
*Arin* = *Uzain* = *Udjain*, VII, XVIII, 1, 8, 19, 33, etc.
@e 0275L40..0275L41
*Artifa* = *irtifāʿ*, altitude (of a heavenly body), 18, 21, 22, etc. T. 60.
@e 0275L42
*Aṣbaʿ*, finger, inch, an Arabic measure of length, 43.
@e 0275L43
*Asceir* see *Sceir*.
@e 0275L44
*Ascowa* see *Shoa*.
@e 0275L45
*Ashoa* see *Shoa*.
@e 0275L46..0275L47
*Asmir* = *as-sair*, or *al-masīr*, the course, the path (of a heavenly body), XXI, T. 9.
@e 0275L48..0275L49
*Athofen* = *aṭ-ṭūfān*, the Flood (*diluvium*) 5, 35, T. 1.
@e 0275L50
*Aub*, corrupted from *Aug*; see this.
@e 0275L51..0275L52
*Audel*, probably corrupted from *ʿarḍ el*, latitude of the . . .[^n] T. 10.
@n Suter writes «Breite des . . ., oder Breite der . . .», with the German article of the genitive in its two forms.
@e 0275L53..0275L56
*Aug* = *audj* (*awdj*), *apogeum* = the greatest distance of a planet from the earth, 9, 10, 14; *aug el-musteri muadala* = the corrected apogee of Jupiter, T. 36; see also *Muadala*.
@e 0275L57
*Autarid* see *Otari[d]*.
@e 0275L58
*Avvel* see *Awel*.
@e 0275L59
*Avtarid* see *Otari[d]*.
@e 0275L60
*Awel* = *awwel*, (the) first, T. 2, 3, etc.
@e 0275L61..0275L64
*Azabaa* = *aṣābiʿ* (pl. of *aṣbaʿ*), fingers (*digiti*), the measure for the diameter of the sun and the moon, also for the earth's shadow in eclipses of the moon, T. 73.
@e 0275L65
*Azofar* see *Acofra*.

**B.**
@e 0275L67
*Bāb*, gate, chapter, 58.
@e 0275L68..0275L69
*Baheram* and *Bahram* = *Bahrām*, Persian name for the planet Mars, XXII, T. 39–42.
@e 0275L70..0275L71
*Batiae*, probably the feminine of *baṭī’* = *baṭī’a*, slow, T. 24.
@e 0275L72

@e 0275L73..0275L74
*Beied hakht* = *Bēdukht*, Persian name for the planet Venus, XXII, T. 45.
@e 0276L01..0276L02
*Bergis* = *Birdjis*, Persian name for the planet Jupiter, T. 33, 36.
@e 0276L03..0276L05
*Bi*, joined with the article *bil*, Arabic preposition, in, on, with, for example *bilfelek*, in (or on) the sphere (see *felek*).
@e 0276L06
*Bhukti* (Sanskrit) enjoyment, pleasure, 78.
@e 0276L07
*Bood* = *buʿd*, distance, T. 73.
@e 0276L08..0276L10
*Buht* (Persian), in astronomy the true (unequal) daily motion of the sun, the moon and the five planets, 22, 78, 90.
@e 0276L11..0276L12
*Buiut* = *buyūt* (pl. of *bait*), houses, especially the twelve astrological houses, 25, T. 79.
@e 0276L13..0276L15
*Burug* = *burūdj* (pl. of *burdj*), castles, houses, especially the twelve signs (houses) of the zodiac, 11 (N. 40), 18 (N. 23), T. 79.
@e 0276L16
*Buruga* see *Burug*.
@e 0276L17
*Burus* see *Burug*.

**D.**
@e 0276L44..0276L46
*Dakaicae* = *daḳā’iḳ* (pl. of *daḳīḳa*) minutes (the 60th part of the degree or of the hour) XXIII, XXV; 3, 4, 36, etc.
@e 0276L47
*Dakaik* see *Dakaicae*.
@e 0276L48
*Dakeca* see *Dakaicae*.
@e 0276L49..0276L50
*Darega* = *daradja* (pl. *daradj*) degree (the 360th part of the circumference of the circle) 11 (N. 40).
@e 0276L51..0276L52
*Dareis*, corrupted from *daregis*, dat. plur. of *darega*; see this.
@e 0276L53..0276L55
*Dehi* = *dalw* (*dalu*), large jug, bucket, the sign of the Water-bearer (*Aquarius*) in the zodiac, T. 59.
@e 0276L56
*Delidhe* see *Thelitha*.
@e 0276L57..0276L58
*Dhel* = *ẓill* shadow, then the trigonometric function of the *cotangent*, T. 60.
@e 0276L59
*Dhirāʿ*, ell (a measure of length) 43.
@e 0276L60..0276L61
*Djebr*, restoration; *al-djebr wa’l-muḳābala* = algebra, VIII; see also *Muḳābala*.
@e 0276L62..0276L64
*Dulheia* and *Dulheiga* = *Dhū’l-ḥidjdja*, the twelfth month of the Muslims, the month of the pilgrimage, XIV, XV, 2, T. 2, 3, etc.
@e 0276L65..0276L66
*Dulkada* = *Dhū’l-ḳaʿda*, the eleventh month of the Muslims, XIV, XV, 2, T. 2, 3, etc.
@e 0276L67..0276L69
*Dulkarnain* = *Dhū’l-ḳarnain*, the two-horned (*bicornus*), the Arabs' epithet for Alexander the Great, 3, 35.
@e 0276L70
*Dulkeda* and *Dulkidda* see *Dulkada*.

@e 0276L87..0276L97
*Fi*, joined with the article *fil* (= *fi’l*), Arabic preposition, in, on, for example *fiaard* = *fī ʿarḍ*, in latitude (with a following genitive) T. 79 (here *Cordubae* has dropped out after *fiaard*) — *filcaat* and *filacaat* = *fī’l-sāʿa*, in the hour, or *fī’l-sāʿāt*, in the hours, T. 5 — *filcinun* = *fī’l-sinīn*, in the years, T. 69 — *fil dakaik elcaat* = *fī daḳā’iḳ al-sāʿa*, in the minutes of the hour, T. 5 — *filiaum* = *fī’l-yaum*, in the day, T. 5 — *filshohor* = *fī’l-shuhūr*, in the months, T. 9 — *fil zahat* see *filcaat*.
@e 0276L98..0276L99
*Fudhul* = *fuḍūl* (pl. of *faḍl*), excesses, differences, 27 (N. 19), T. 73.
