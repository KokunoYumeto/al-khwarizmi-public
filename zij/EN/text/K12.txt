My reason for choosing this day is that the determination of the visibility of the lunar crescent is really of importance only for this day, for with the end of 29 *Shaʿbān* (at 6 o'clock in the evening) the month of fasting, *Ramaḍān*, begins. The fast, however, does not begin at 6 o'clock, but at the moment when the lunar crescent first becomes visible. This can happen some time before or after 6 o'clock. Since the weather does not always permit the appearance of the lunar crescent to be observed, computation and the tables must be called in to help.
