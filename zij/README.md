The copy is that of the Wellcome Library, scanned by the Internet Archive (b24872489, 296 JP2 images, 0000–0295). Every printed line is one line here, with its anchor `AK07-J####-L##` (JP2 image, line); the lines of the footnotes have the anchors `AK07-J####-N##`.

## What this text is

The Arabic *Zīj al-Sindhind* of al-Khwārizmī is lost. Suter's book edits a derivative line of it: the recension of Maslama al-Majrīṭī (d. 1007/08), who converted the tables from the Persian era of Yazdegerd to the Hijra and in part from the meridian of Arin to that of Cordova, in the Latin translation ascribed to Adelard of Bath (twelfth century), from manuscripts at Chartres, Oxford, Paris and Madrid. It is not al-Khwārizmī's text, and the transcription never presents it as such.

- **Spaces before colons, semicolons, question and exclamation marks**: the edition sets a thin space before them (measured on the scan, `tools/marksp.py`: 8–16 px, where a word space is 20–40 px); the page files write a space there and the build keeps it as a thin space (U+2009).
- **Dashes**: the edition has a long dash (39–47 px on the scan in the type of the text, 29–43 px in the smaller type of the notes and of the references below the chapter headings) and a short one (25–28 px in the smaller type); they are written — and –, and the measurement of a doubtful one is given on its page.
- **Tables** are transcribed row by row, each row one line with its cells separated by «|» (ours, not printed); a head that spans several columns stands in the first of them. Arithmetic that can be recomputed is checked and recorded on the page.

## What is not reproduced

- The images. The Internet Archive marks the item «It is possible this Item is protected by copyright and/or related rights»; the transcription reproduces only the text of the 1914 edition, which is in the public domain: Bjørnbo died in 1911 and Suter in 1922, and Besthorn was born in 1847.
- **Copy matter** of the Wellcome copy (the bookplate «Ex Libris R. M. Burrell», the shelfmark, the library's stamp, a price in pencil) and the matter of the digitisation (the colour cards, the notice): recorded on each page (`copy_provenance.tsv`).
