@obs L07-L31, the collected years. Checked against al-Khwārizmī's parameters, Brahmagupta's (p. 42, JP2 0074): 57 753 300 000 revolutions of the moon and 488 105 858 of its apogee in 1 577 916 450 000 days, a daily motion of the moon of 13° 10′ 34″ 52‴ and of its anomaly of 13° 3′ 53″ 58‴. Thirty Arab years, 10 631 days, move the moon by 1s 7° 50′ 4.9″ and its anomaly by 9s 23° 57′ 27.2″. From the radices 3s 27° 45′ 17″ (117° 45′ 17″) and 3s 14° 36′ 1″ the rows agree with the multiples of these steps to within 3.7″ and 3.9″, the seconds growing by 4 or 5 and by 26 or 27 (with the readings of notes b-e and the editor's 7 of note f, where C and O have 50). The row 690 has the sign 9 where the step requires 8: between 7s 20° 7′ 2″ (660) and 10s 5° 47′ 10″ (720) it is 8s 27° 57′ 6″ (computed 8s 27° 57′ 8.8″); the rows 600-720 are those that O's second hand added (note a-a); no note bears on the 9.
@obs L07-L36, the single years. Checked: the year p moves the moon and the anomaly by the days of the first p Arab years, 354 days and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29 (Tab. 2, p. 110, JP2 0142): all 30 rows of the anomaly agree to within 3.7″, and 29 rows of the moon to within 1.5″ (with the readings of notes g-q, s-v and x-z; C's 16 and 14 of notes o and q are nearer the computation, 16.2″ and 13.9″, than O's 15 and 13, which the edition prints). The year 30, 1s 7° 50′ 4″ and 9s 23° 57′ 27″, is the step of the collected years.
@obs L32 and N07, the year 26 of the moon: the edition prints 2s 26° 56′ 44″ against the 20″ of C and O (note r). The computation gives 2s 26° 56′ 20.5″: the year 26 is a leap year, and its 355 days move the moon on from the year 25, 2s 29° 19′ 57″, by 11s 27° 36′ 22″, to 2s 26° 56′ 19″-20″; the seconds 44 are those of a common year of 354 days (57″ less 13″), while the degrees and minutes 26° 56′ are those of the leap year. The year 27, 354 days later, 2s 11° 22′ 7″, follows from 20″ (20″ less 13″), not from 44″. The manuscripts' reading is right and the edition's emendation is wrong; the table is transcribed as printed.
