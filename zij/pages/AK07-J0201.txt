@obs L06-L35, the arguments. Each row gives the four arcs that have the same sine, x, 180° − x, 180° + x and 360° − x, in signs and degrees (Suter, p. 69, JP2 0101, L24-L30): in the left half 0s x, 5s (30 − x), 6s x and 11s (30 − x) for x = 1° to 30°, in the right half 1s x, 4s (30 − x), 7s x and 10s (30 − x) for x = 31° to 60°, the last row 1s 0°, 5s 0°, 7s 0°, 11s 0° and 2s 0°, 4s 0°, 8s 0°, 10s 0°. The sines of 61° to 90° follow on p. 170.
@obs L06-L35, the sines. Checked: each value is 60 · sin x to within 1.2″ for x = 1° to 60°: within 0.9″ in every row except at 26° (18° 18′ 7″ for 18° 18′ 8.2″) and 33° (32° 40′ 41″ for 32° 40′ 42.0″), and 30° 0′ 0″ at 30°. The edition's readings in notes b-h, where C and O agree on a different figure, are those of the computation within 0.8″: 14° 30′ 55″, 28° 10′ 6″, 33° 33′ 6″, 34° 24′ 53″, 41° 40′ 46″, 45° 16′ 58″ and 49° 44′ 32″; at 8° (note a), where O has 50 and C 1, the edition prints 8° 21′ 2″, and the computation gives 8° 21′ 1.4″.
@obs L06-L35, compared with al-Battānī's sine table (Nallino, II, 55-56): the two tables agree at every whole degree from 1° to 60° except at 3°, 7°, 9°, 11°, 16°, 17°, 19°, 26°, 33°, 39°, 46°, 47°, 51°, 58° and 60°, where al-Khwārizmī's value is 1″ less, 3° 8′ 24″ against 3° 8′ 25″ and so on; in these fifteen places the computation lies between the two values, nearer to al-Battānī's in nine and to al-Khwārizmī's at 17°, 39°, 46°, 47°, 58° and 60°. Suter took al-Battānī's value where the manuscripts' reading differed from it by more than 1″ (p. 69, JP2 0101, L40-L44), so that all the edition's readings of notes a-h are al-Battānī's values; Suter and Björnbo explain the differences of 1″ by the halving of Ptolemy's chords with odd seconds rounded up or down (p. 70, JP2 0102, L01-L05).
@obs Notes 1-3: the units of the manuscripts: «Partes» in C and O and «Gradus» in N for the parts of the radius, «Dak.» (dakaicae) in C and O and «Min.» in N, «Elth.» or «Elthen.» (eltheniae, the seconds; Suter, p. 69, JP2 0101, L31-L32) in C and O and «Sec.» in N. Italics: «Partes» and «sive». L03, «Elgeib (CO» without its closing parenthesis, and «numerornm»: see the critical notes.
