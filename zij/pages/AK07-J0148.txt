@obs L06-L35. Checked against al-Khwārizmī's sidereal year, 365.2584375 days (p. 42, JP2 0074), a daily mean motion of 59′ 8″ 10‴ 21⁗ (p. 65, JP2 0097): the 30 days agree with n × 59′ 8.17″ to within 0.8″, some values rounded and some cut off (with the readings of notes a and b). The hours, with the hourly motion 2′ 27.84″, agree to within 0.6″, except the 22 hours: 54′ 52″ against 54′ 12.5″, 40″ too large, between the correct 51′ 45″ and 56′ 40″ of the 21 and 23 hours. The «partes horarum» are the sixtieths of an hour, 60 of them the 2′ 28″ of one hour: the values agree with p × 2.464″ to within 0.5″, except 4 parts, 8″ against 9.9″, and 34 parts, 1′ 25″ against 1′ 23.8″. No note of the apparatus bears on these three values; they are recorded here, without critical notes.
