@obs L06-L22: the oblique ascensions from the noon shadow at the equinox of a gnomon of twelve digits: the shadow times 114, divided by 115, is the argument of Aries; the shadow times 113, divided by 116, that of Taurus; a third of the shadow that of Gemini. Each argument is subtracted from the right ascension of its sign for the sign and its opposite companion, and added for the other pair. L13: «race elhamel», the head of the Ram (raʾs al-ḥamal), the first point of Aries; L16 «bilmustakim», in the right sphere (bi-l-mustaqīm), in italics.
@obs Checked against the ascensional differences for the obliquity of 23° 51′ (p. 19, L07-L08): the difference grows by about 0.986 degree per digit of the shadow over Aries, 0.799 over Taurus and 0.326 over Gemini; the rule for Aries (114/115 = 0.991) and the rule for Gemini (1/3) agree with these to about one and two in a hundred, the rule for Taurus (113/116 = 0.974) does not; 113/141 would give 0.801. To be compared with Suter's commentary on the chapter (p. 73).
