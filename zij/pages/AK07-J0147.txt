@obs L05-L34. Checked against al-Khwārizmī's sidereal year, 1 577 916 450 000 days in 4 320 000 000 years (p. 42, JP2 0074), a mean motion of 360° in 365.2584375 days: 30 Arab years, 10 631 days, give 1s 7° 57′ 3.5″, a common year of 354 days 11s 18° 54′ 13.1″ and a leap year 11s 19° 53′ 21.3″. From the radix 3s 23° 25′ 48″ (113° 25′ 48″, p. 33, JP2 0065) the collected years from 30 to 570 are the radix and multiples of 1s 7° 57′ 3.5″ to within 2.4″, the seconds growing by 3 and 4 in turn (with the readings of notes c-g and i). The rows 600-720, which are those of O in a second hand (note a-b), lie 11-13″ higher: they continue from the 3s 24° 30′ 5″ to which that hand corrected the row 570 (notes h and k-l) by 1s 7° 57′ 4″ each, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25 seconds. The second hand's corrections of the seconds from 180 to 570 (note k-l), 13, 17, … 57, 1, 5, also grow by 4; the first, 14 at 150, does not follow that step.
@obs L05-L34, the single years, and L05-L16, the months. Checked: the year p gives the mean motion in the days of the first p Arab years, 354 days and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29 (Tab. 2, p. 110, JP2 0142): all 30 rows agree to within 1.5″, the values cut off rather than rounded (with the readings of notes m-p); the year 30, 1s 7° 57′ 3″, is the step of the collected years. The months give the motion in 30, 59, 89, … 354 days, the months of 30 and 29 days in turn: all 12 agree to within 1.1″ (with the reading of note q).
@obs L37-L48. Checked: the mean sun at which the true sun enters each sign, with the solar apogee 77° 55′ (p. 9, JP2 0041) and the rule sin q = sin 2° 14′ · sin (mean − apogee) for the equation q (p. 45, JP2 0077): the values agree to within 2′ 48″, except Scorpius, whose 1° 26′ is 10′ 14″ too small; O's reading 1° 36′ (note r) agrees to within 14″. The note s, Ms. C's marginal note on the entries of Libra and Capricorn, is the editor's apparatus; its values are not those of the table.
