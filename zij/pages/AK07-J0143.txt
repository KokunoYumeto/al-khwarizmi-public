@obs L06-L17. Checked: the Persian year has twelve months of 30 days and five added days after Ābān, the eighth month, 365 days in all. A month of 30 days moves the weekday on by 2 (30 = 4 × 7 + 2), Ābān with its 35 days by 0, and a year by 1 (365 = 52 × 7 + 1), so that the columns 1-7 are the seven years of the weekly cycle. The first value, 3, is Tuesday 16 June 632, the first day of Farvardīn of the year 1 of Yazdegird (1 for Sunday; p. 37, JP2 0069): all 84 values as printed, with the readings of notes a and b. The year Y of the era takes the column (Y − 1) mod 7 + 1.
@obs N02: «Chordesme M —», the dash at the end of the line printed broken in two short pieces (9 and 13 px over 40 px); read as the long dash. N03: «Haharirááe», with two acute accents, «(sic)» the editor's. N04: «Bahamāme», with the macron.
