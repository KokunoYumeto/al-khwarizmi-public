@obs L07-L41. The beginnings of the houses for the ascendant at 1° to 30° of Gemini, by the rule of p. 194. Checked with the obliquity 23° 35′ and the latitude 38° 40′: the degrees and minutes agree within 2′ in 103 of the 150 places and within 3′ in 127; the first rows lag behind the computation, as the last rows of p. 195 do, by 3.8′ to 13.7′ at 1° (L07) and 2.8′ to 11.1′ at 2°, most in the fourth house, decreasing to 4°, and the fourth house lags by 2′ to 4′ also at 5°-10° and 24°-30°. The names of the signs, however, are one sign later than the ascendant in Gemini requires: for the ascendant at 1° of Gemini the second to the sixth house begin at 23° 37′ of Gemini, 15° 52′ of Cancer, 8° 59′ of Leo, 16° 7′ of Virgo and 24° 53′ of Libra (the table: 23° 33′, 15° 45′, 8° 45′, 15° 58′, 24° 49′), so that the heads should read Gemini [et Cancer], Cancer [et Leo], Leo [et Virgo], Virgo [et Libra] and Libra [et Scorpius], and the words within the table Cancer for «Leo», Leo for «Virgo», Virgo for «Libra», Libra for «Scorp.» and Scorpius for «Sagitt.».
