@obs L02-L28: the eclipses of the moon: with the time of the syzygy and the argument of latitude, the table shows whether an eclipse is possible («aptum»); the diameter of the shadow is divided into twenty digits of sixty minutes, the diameter of the moon into twelve; the partial phases, the immersion, «elzukut» (al-suqūṭ), Latin «casus», and the emersion, «elingile» (al-injilāʾ), «respiratio», and the totality, «elmukdh» (al-mukth), «mora». For the moon at its greatest distance the shadow has twenty digits, at its least distance twenty-one; between them both tables are entered and the difference taken. L15: «arabes» is letter-spaced. L28: «(anomaliam)», in italics within round brackets, explains «argumentum».
