@obs L04-L18, the five columns of numbers. Checked: the row r and the column c hold 140 (r − 1) + 28 c, the multiples of 28 from 28 to 2100, all 75 as printed (with the readings of notes a and b). @obs L04-L20, the eras. Checked: in years of 365 days and months of 30 days, from the era of Nabonassar (26 February 747 BC), the «rex nabuchodonosor» of L05, to Yazdegird (16 June 632) are 503 425 days, 1379 years 3 months 0 days; from Philip (12 November 324 BC), 348 665 days, 955 y 3 m 0 d (L06); from the Flood (18 February 3102 BC), 1 363 597 days, 3735 y 10 m 22 d (L04: 23). In the Julian calendar, from Alexander (1 October 312 BC) to Christ (1 January AD 1) are 311 y 3 m 0 d (L15), from Christ to the Hijra (16 July 622) 621 y 6 m 15 d (L14). The rows agree with one another: L18 and L09, 3725 y 11 m 19 d + 9 y 11 m 4 d = 3735 y 10 m 23 d (L04); L11 and L09, 945 y 3 m 26 d + 9 y 11 m 4 d = 955 y 3 m 0 d (L06); L13 and L16, 659 y 6 m 17 d + 273 y 3 m 0 d = 932 y 9 m 17 d (L10), with the readings of notes g and h, against the 759 and 223 of the manuscripts; L15 and L14 give 932 y 9 m 15 d, two days less than L10. N08: from Christ to Yazdegird the Julian calendar counts 631 y 5 m 15 d.
