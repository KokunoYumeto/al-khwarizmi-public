@obs L07-L41. The beginnings of the houses for the ascendant at 1° to 30° of Sagittarius, by the rule of p. 194 (JP2 0226). Checked with the obliquity 23° 35′ and the latitude 38° 40′: the first rows continue the excess at the end of p. 201 (JP2 0233), at 1° (L07) by 3.3′, 10.1′, 15.6′, 8.7′ and 4.9′ in the second to the sixth house, decreasing to 5°; in right ascension these excesses stand roughly in the proportion 1 : 2 : 3 : 2 : 1 of the shares of the night arc and the day arc in the rule, as an error in the night arc of the ascendant would give. From 5° to 30° the second, third, fourth and sixth houses agree within 4.7′. The fifth house falls behind the computation from 9° (L15) to 29° (L40), most at 22°-23° (L32-L33), 23.6′ (7° 53′ of Taurus for 8° 16.6′ at 23°): its steps are 71′ to 74′ from 7° to 16° and 68′ to 70′ from 16° to 26°, where the computed ones fall evenly from 75′ to 67′; from 26° the steps, 74′ and three times 72′, bring it back to the computation, 2.3′ above it at 30°. So the fifth house enters Taurus one row later than the computation (at 17°, L24-L25, where the computation gives 0° 2′ at 16°). No note bears on the figures of this column except at 27° (note h).
