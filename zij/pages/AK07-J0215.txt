The printed steps are 13m 44s or 13m 45s except from the Radix to 61, 13m 35s and 13m 55s, with the 14m 46s at 31 that Suter's example quotes (p. 81, L36): see the critical note. Each conjunction follows the opposition of the same row of Tab. 70 (p. 184, JP2 0216) by half a synodic month, 14d 18h 21m 59s at the Radix and 14d 18h 22m 0s to 2s in the other rows; at 31 the opposition, 14d 5h 52m 57s, gives 29d 0h 14m 56s. At 481 (note d) the edition's 40, O's reading, gives the steps 12m 45s and 14m 45s and an interval of 14d 18h 21m 2s from the opposition; C's 41 gives 13m 45s twice and 14d 18h 22m 2s.
