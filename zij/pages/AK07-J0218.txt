@obs L06-L17. Checked: the time is k synodic months of 29d 12h 44m 3s, the time of the row of Zafar with its day subtracted, less the days of the k months elapsed, 30 and 29 in turn (30, 59, 89, … 325); where the days exceed the synodic months, after an odd number of months, a day is subtracted and 24 hours added («Dies subtrahendi» 1 at Zafar, Rabe elakhir, Jum. elakhir, Scabin, Scauel and Dulheia). The times agree to the second in every row except at Scauel (L15): see the critical note.
@obs L06-L17, continued. The mean longitude is the motion of the sun in the k synodic months, k · 29° 6′ 19.4″, the month values of Tab. 4 (p. 115, JP2 0147) less the day subtracted plus the motion in the hours: within 2″ in seven rows; at Rabe elawel 1s 28° 12′ 29″ for 1s 28° 12′ 39″, at Jumedi elakhir 4s 25° 31′ 56″ for 4s 25° 31′ 37″, at Scabin 6s 23° 44′ 54″ for 6s 23° 44′ 16″, at Ramadh 7s 22° 50′ 54″ for 7s 22° 50′ 36″, and at Dulkada 9s 21° 3′ 53″, the 53 of C and O, for 9s 21° 3′ 15″, where N has 13 (note e); no note bears on the other four. The anomaly is k · 25° 49′ 0.6″ within 3″, its seconds running 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and then 7 to Dulheia where the law gives 1, 1, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 7; the argument of latitude is k · 1s 0° 40′ 14″ within 1″ in every row. Twelve synodic months give 11s 19° 15′ 53″, 10s 9° 48′ 8″ and 0s 8° 2′ 48″, within 2″ of the 11s 19° 15′ 55″, 10s 9° 48′ 10″ and 0s 8° 2′ 50″ of the year 1 in Tab. 71 (p. 185, JP2 0217).
