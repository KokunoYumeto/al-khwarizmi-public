@obs Tab. 57a (L01-L05): the twelve signs, «Sagittar.», «Capricor.» and «Aquar.» abbreviated, each over two columns, «Gr.» and «Min.», the minutes of Aries and Pisces with note 1; double vertical rules divide the signs. The rows «X», «XX» and «XXX» are the three decans (facies) of each sign (Suter, p. 68, JP2 0100, note 1). Each printed row is one line here, the cells separated by our «|»; a head followed by an empty cell spans the two columns. L02, «Facies» and the units in smaller bold type.
@obs The apparatus of Tab. 57a (N01-N02) stands in the print below that table, before the heading of Tab. 57b; the apparatus of Tab. 57b (N03) at the foot of the page. Here both follow the lines of the page.
@obs L03-L05. Checked: the table is symmetric about the equinoxes: the values of the first, second and third decans of each sign are those of the third, second and first decans of the sign symmetric to it, Aries and Pisces, Taurus and Aquarius, Gemini and Capricornus, Cancer and Sagittarius, Leo and Scorpius, Virgo and Libra, in all thirty-six values with the readings of the edition. The least value, 9° 18′, stands at the second decans of Taurus and Aquarius, the greatest, 21° 57′, at the third decan of Virgo and the first of Libra. The oblique ascensions of signs symmetric about the equinoxes are equal, a property of the same form. Suter did not find how the values were computed (p. 68, JP2 0100, L24-L25); in his example of the crescent of 29 Shaʿbān 540 he takes the 9° 21′ of Pisces X (L03) and remarks on the 9° 19′ of Aquarius XXX (L05) (p. 68, L08-L10).
@obs Notes on the readings. The symmetry decides between the readings: O's 19 of Taurus X (note a, against C's 29) answers to the 9° 19′ of Aquarius XXX; C's 10 of Gemini XXX (note b, against O's 9) to the 10° 37′ of Capricornus X; the 57 of Libra X (note c, against 17) to the 21° 57′ of Virgo XXX; the 21 of Libra XX (note d, the edition's against the 20 of C and O) to the 21° 4′ of Virgo XX; the 15 of Scorpius XXX (note e, O and N, C corrected from 16) to the 15° 58′ of Leo X. Note 1: the units of the minutes are «Min.» in N, «Dak.» (dakaicae) in O and «Puncta» in C, and C and N repeat the line of the units, «hanc lineam iterant C et N».
@obs Tab. 57b (L07-L18). L07-L10, the introduction, followed by the siglum «(C)», and L11, «Ex quorum ductu quid producatur (O).», O's shorter version, indented; «enatemus(?)» with the editor's question mark; «sunt ;» with the thin space of the edition. The table: a head row and seven rows, the orders of the factors in the first column and the head row, the order of the product where a row and a column meet, «angulari conventu» (L10); double horizontal rules below the head row. Each printed row is one line here, the cells separated by our «|». Checked: in all forty-nine cells the order of the product is the sum of the orders of the factors, Gradus 0, Dakaicae 1, Secundae 2 and so on to Duodecimae 12. Suter: the table does not belong to chapter 22 and is mentioned nowhere in the text; he set it here to fill the page (p. 68, JP2 0100, L29-L32).
