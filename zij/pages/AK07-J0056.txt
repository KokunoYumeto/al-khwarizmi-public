@obs L01-L26: the true places of the sun and the moon at the mean syzygy; the interval between them, increased by half a sixth of itself, a twelfth, is added to the place of the moon and the twelfth to the place of the sun when the sun is ahead, and subtracted when the moon is; the same to the argument of latitude; the interval with its twelfth, divided by the moon's hourly motion, gives the hours to be added or subtracted. Checked: the moon gains on the sun about 12;11 degrees a day, so that while it closes an interval the sun moves on about one twelfth of it, which the twelfth added to the interval allows for. L26: the hours times 0;32,40 correct the moon's anomaly. Checked: the daily motion in anomaly, about 13° 3′ 54″, divided by twenty-four gives 32′ 40″; the reading of C, 32′ 50″ (note 40), does not.
