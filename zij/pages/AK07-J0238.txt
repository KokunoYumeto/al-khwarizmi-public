@obs The page number «206» stands at the head of the page, centred, with a rule below it; at the left the number of the table, «Tab. 91», in bold sans serif, underlined. The page has no signature. @obs The page holds three fields (Suter's «Feld», area), one for each ascendant: «Quinque5) gradus Arietis» (L03), «Decem11) gradus Arietis» (L15) and «Quindecim12) gradus Arietis» (L23), 5°, 10° and 15° of Aries rising. In L03 «latitudinis 38 G. 30 M.6)», of the latitude 38° 30′, stands at the right of the same line; here in a cell of its own. Under each heading, in one centred line, the numbers for the sextile, «Tezdiz7)» (tasdīs), the quadrature, «Tarbea8)» (tarbīʿ), and the trine, «Tethlith9)» (tathlīth) (L04, L16, L24); headings and numbers in bold. The first field has the heads of the columns: «Gradus aequales», printed upwards in two lines, here «Gradus» in L05 and «aequales» in L06, and six pairs of «Gr.», «Min.» under «Aries Libra Horoscopus10)», «Taurus Scorpius Horoscopus», «Gemini Sagittar. Horoseopus», «Cancer Capricorn. Horoscopus», «Leo Aquarius Horoscopus» and «Virgo Pisces Horoscopus10)», where C and O have «Matale», maṭāliʿ, ascensions (note 10). Here each head is set from the top, one printed line to a line (L05-L07); the units in L08; the cells separated by our «|»; a head followed by an empty cell spans both columns. The second and the third field have no heads; their columns stand under those of the first. Double rules divide the fields.
@obs The use (Suter, p. 100, JP2 0132, L30-L37, and p. 101, JP2 0133, L01-L36): in the field of the ascendant one enters with the longitude of the planet, its degrees in the first column and its sign at the head; to the number found one adds the number of the aspect at the head of the field, and the longitude that belongs to the sum in the same field is the place of the left aspect. A sum larger than the last number of the field is diminished by it and sought again, the columns then serving for Libra to Pisces. The right trine, quadrature and sextile are the points opposite the left sextile, quadrature and trine.
@obs The numbers at the head of a field are a third, a half and two thirds of the last number of the field, that at 30° of Virgo, 180° from the beginning of Aries: 147° 9′ (L14) gives 49° 3′, 73° 34.5′ and 98° 6′, printed 49° 3′, 73° 34′ and 98° 6′ (L04); 148° 36′ (L30) gives 49° 32′, 74° 18′ and 99° 4′, as printed (L24). In the second field the quadrature 73° 46′ and the trine of note f, 98° 22′, are a half and two thirds of 147° 33′, whose third is 49° 11′, while the table has 147° 22′ at 30° of Virgo (L22), which would give 49° 7.3′, 73° 41′ and 98° 14.7′; the printed «41° 11′» and «98° 32′» stand for 49° 11′ and 98° 22′ (critical notes). For Tab. 113 Suter quotes the sextile 48° 19′ and the last number of the field, 144° 58′ (p. 101, JP2 0133, L23-L25), likewise a third.
@obs The columns as ascensions. Suter (p. 100, JP2 0132, L11-L27) compared the first row with the oblique ascensions for the latitude 38° 30′. In the columns of Aries and Taurus the three fields agree with these within 16′ (computed with the obliquity 23° 35′: 3° 0′ at 5°, 18° 32′ at 30°, 40° 42′ at 60°; the table has 2° 58′, 2° 55′ and 2° 54′, 18° 25′ in each field, 40° 45′, 40° 35′ and 40° 39′), except at three places of the second field (L18, L20, L21; below). In Gemini the columns run ahead of the oblique ascensions, by 44′ to 54′ at its end (69° 41′ computed; 70° 35′, 70° 25′, 70° 29′), and in Leo and Virgo they fall far below them, to 147° 9′, 147° 22′ and 148° 36′ at the end of Virgo, where the oblique ascension is 180°. The steps of the columns are those of the oblique ascensions near the ascendant (2° 58′, 2° 55′ and 2° 54′ for the first 5° of Aries; computed 3° 0′), about those of the right ascensions near the lower meridian, which lies at about 3°, 5½° and 8° of Cancer for the three ascendants (5° 10′ to 5° 45′ for the steps from 25° of Gemini to 10° of Cancer; the right ascensions 5° 27′), and about those of the oblique descensions at the end of Virgo (3° 23′, 2° 52′ and 3° 8′ for the last 5°; computed 3° 0′). The rule by which the table was computed is not reconstructed here; Suter could not explain the deviations (p. 100, JP2 0132, L24-L26).
