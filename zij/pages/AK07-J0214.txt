@obs L06-L35. The equation of time from Libra 1° to Pisces 30°: it reaches its greatest value, 34m 28s, at Scorpius 9° and 10° and falls to 0m 0s at Aquarius 22°, as Suter says (p. 80, JP2 0112, L22-L26); at Pisces 30° it is 7m 48s, 20 s less than the 8m 8s of Aries 1° (p. 181). The seconds are multiples of 4 except near the greatest value, at Libra 29° and Scorpius 5°, 7°, 8°, 11° and 12° (33m 54s, 34m 22s, 34m 26s, 34m 27s, 34m 27s, 34m 26s), and near the least, at Aquarius 11°, 17°, 18°, 20°, 21°, 23°, 24°, 26°, 28° and 29° (1m 3s, 0m 10s, 0m 6s, 0m 2s, 0m 1s, 0m 1s, 0m 2s, 0m 6s, 0m 10s, 0m 14s). Checked by the law of p. 181: within 2′ of arc (8 s of time) in every row except Sagittarius 26° and 27° (L31-L32), 20m 16s and 19m 40s, 15.7 s and 11.6 s more than the law (20m 0.3s and 19m 28.4s); from Sagittarius 24° to 29° the steps of the column are 28, 24, 36, 40 and 36 seconds, where the law gives 32 in each.
@obs Notes on the readings. Checked by the law and the steps of the columns: the edition's readings are the nearer, and the variants depart from the law by about one minute of time or more, at Libra 18° and 23° (notes a and c, O's 32 and 33 against C's 31 and 32), Scorpius 23° (e, O's 33 against C's 32) and Sagittarius 30° (f, the edition's 17m 52s against C's 18 and O's 27; 17m 51.9s by the law); C omits the minutes at Libra 22° (b). At Capricornus 27° (h) O's 48, 2.6 s from the law, against C's 43, 7.6 s, and at Pisces 23° (m) O's 32, 5.8 s, against C's 39, 12.8 s. At Libra 28°, Sagittarius 29°, Aquarius 4°, Pisces 1° and 7° (notes d, g, i, k and l) the readings differ by 1 to 4 seconds, within the precision of the table; at Libra 28° C's 49 is 0.4 s nearer to the law than O's 48, at the others the edition's reading is the nearer.
