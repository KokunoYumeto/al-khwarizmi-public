@obs Below the section heading the glossary runs in two columns divided by a vertical rule; transcribed column by column (@cl, @cr), the left column first (L03-L39), then the right (L40-L74). Each entry begins at the edge of its column, and its further lines are indented. The letter heads «A.» (L03) and «B.» (L66) are set in bold, centred in their columns, «B.» after a blank line. In each entry the transcribed words of the text, their Arabic forms and the Latin words are in italics; references: arabic numerals are the pages of the text and the commentary, roman numerals those of the introduction, «T.» the tables and «N.» the footnotes.
