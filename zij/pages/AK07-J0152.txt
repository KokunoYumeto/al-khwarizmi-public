@obs L06-L35, the years and months. Brahmagupta's parameter, 146 567 298 revolutions of Saturn in 1 577 916 450 000 days, gives a daily mean motion of 2′ 0″ 22‴ 52⁗ and in 30 Arab years (10 631 days) 11s 25° 29′ 30.8″. Checked: the table rests on a slightly larger motion, about 2′ 0″ 22‴ 58⁗ a day, 11s 25° 29′ 48″ in 30 years (the single year 30, L35): with it the 30 single years agree to within 1.6″ (the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29), and the collected years, which fall by 4° 30′ 12″ or 4° 30′ 11″ in each row from the radix 3s 27° 58′ 37″ (117° 58′ 37″), agree to within 2″ from the radix to 540 (with the editor's 37 of note a, where C, M and O have 57, and the readings of notes b-i); against Brahmagupta's parameter the collected years run ahead by 17″ in each row, 5′ 16″ at 540. The row 570, 1s 3° 24′ 56″, is 1° larger than the step requires, 1s 2° 24′ 55″; no note bears on it. The months, 30, 59, … 354 days, agree with either motion to within 1″ (with the reading of note k); the year of the months, 11° 50′ 15″, is the single year 1.
@obs L22-L33, the anomaly («Heza») of Saturn, the excess of the sun's mean motion over Saturn's (p. 56, JP2 0088): 57′ 7.8″ a day with Brahmagupta's parameters.
