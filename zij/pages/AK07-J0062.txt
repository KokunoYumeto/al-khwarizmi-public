@obs L01-L05: the table of the houses is entered with any degree of the ascendant from Aries on, and gives the beginnings of the second to the sixth houses; the seventh corresponds to the first, the eighth to the second, and so on, so the other six are not given. Checked: the cusps of opposite houses are 180 degrees apart.
@obs L06-L31: chapter 37, the rays of the planets, sextile («exagonalis»), quartile («tetragonalis») and trine («trigonalis»): the table is entered with the degree of the planet, or the next greater one, in the column of equal degrees; its six areas («areae») serve the ascendant in the fifth, tenth, fifteenth, twentieth, twenty-fifth and thirtieth degree of its sign; the value under the planet's sign is taken, the number of the ray written at the head of the page is added, and the sum, looked for in the same page, gives with the sign and the degrees at its head the place of the ray. For an ascendant between two of these degrees both columns are used and their difference is divided by five (continued on p. 31). Checked: the columns are five degrees of the ascendant apart, so that a fifth of the difference of two neighbouring columns is the change for one degree.
