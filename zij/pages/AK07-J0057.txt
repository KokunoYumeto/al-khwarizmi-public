@obs L01-L11 end chapter 31 (pp. 23-24, JP2 0055-0056). L03: the computations are for the situation of Cordova, «pro situ Cordubae», the meridian of Maslama's recension (see Suter's introduction, p. VII, JP2 0011, L14-L18). L04-L07: the difference of longitude between one's place and Cordova, an hour for every fifteen degrees, is subtracted from the hours of Cordova for a place to the east and added for a place to the west. Checked: local time at a place east of Cordova is ahead of Cordova's, so that an event computed in hours of Cordova falls later by the local reckoning; the hours would be added for a place to the east and subtracted for one to the west, the opposite of the text. Suter's commentary on chapter 31 (p. 84, JP2 0116, N02) says the same: «oriente» and «occidente» are to be exchanged. L08-L11: the table of the equation of days, for turning unequal days into equal ones and back.
@obs L12-L32: chapter 32 stands in square brackets, opened before «Scientia» in the heading and closed after «supradiximus.» (L32); note 28: O omits it. The heading, in bold, centred, in two lines; in the right margin «C 83r» beside its first line and «N 56r» beside its second. «tezwiet elbuiut», the equalization of the houses (taswiyat al-buyūt); «casa» and «domicilium» the house; L15 and L18 «felek elmustakim», the right sphere, in italics.
@obs L14-L32: the cusps of the houses: from the right ascension of the ascendant two seasonal night hours (in equatorial degrees) are added for each of the next three cusps, and two day hours for the following ones; the seventh cusp must come out opposite the ascendant. Checked: two seasonal hours of the day are a sixth of the arc of the day, and two of the night a sixth of 360 degrees less that arc, which is 60 less two day hours, as L30-L32 say.
