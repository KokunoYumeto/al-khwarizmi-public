@obs The table: a thick and a thin rule above the head, a double rule below it; vertical rules divide the fourteen columns; short rules under the numbers of the months and under their days; no rule closes the table at the foot. The heads of the first and the last column, «Anni egyptiorum residui» and «Anni bissextiles», are printed turned through a quarter, reading upwards, beside the three rows of the heads of the months; here they stand in the first row (L02). «buri-mihez» and «bur-muda» are printed in two lines in their narrow columns (L04-L05). In the last column «bissex-tus» and «bis-sext.» are each divided over two rows (L07-L08, L31-L32), the note number 9 after each. Each printed row is one line here, the cells separated by our «|».
@obs L06-L33. Checked: the twelve months have 30 days, and the last, «muzre» with «35 1/4», carries the five or six added days of the year, so that each nota of a row is the one before it plus 2 (30 = 4 × 7 + 2), all 336 values as printed with the reading of note a. From one row to the next the first nota moves on by 1 after a year of 365 days and by 2 after a year of 366 days: the notae imply the leap years 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22 and 26, the rows marked «bissext.» in the last column. The notae of the first column agree with the weekdays of 1 Thoth of the Coptic years 18-45 of the era of Diocletian (1 for Sunday): 1 Thoth of the year 18, 29 August 301, a Friday, 6.
@obs N05-N06: Ms. N's marginal note in a second hand: the Egyptian year begins on the fourth day before the Kalends of September, 29 August, without the leap day, and on the third, 30 August, with it, «secundum ysidorum».
