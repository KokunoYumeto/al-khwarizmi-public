{i:moon ; for Mars and the other planets are prescribed that for the conjunction, that for}

@p 44. {i:To the mean place of the planet apply half the equation of the conjunction,}
{i:likewise half the equation of the apsis ; to the mean place of the planet apply the whole}

@obs L09-L39: the procedure of the Sūrya-Siddhānta as Burgess's commentary explains it: half the equation of the conjunction (the equation of the centre, in Suter's terms) is applied to the mean place; from that place half the equation of the apsis (the anomaly) is found and applied; from that result the whole equation of the apsis is found and applied to the original mean place, and from this last place the whole equation of the conjunction. Suter's summary, L30-L39, follows the same order.
