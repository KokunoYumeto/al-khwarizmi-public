@obs L07-L36. The node moves backwards; the table gives its motion as a growing quantity, which is subtracted from 360° to give the place of the node, as Suter computes on p. 59 (JP2 0091, «360° — 279° 41′ … da der Drachenkopf retrograd ist»), with the values 92° 39′ for the end of the year 510 and 184° 42′ for 29 years, those of L24 and L35. Brahmagupta's parameter, 232 311 168 revolutions of the node in 1 577 916 450 000 days, gives 3′ 10″ 48‴ 20⁗ a day and 6s 23° 27′ 34.2″ in 30 Arab years (10 631 days). Checked: the table rests on a slightly larger motion, 3′ 10″ 48‴ 23⁗ a day, 6s 23° 27′ 42.6″ in 30 years (the single year 30, 6s 23° 27′ 43″): with it the collected years from the radix 7s 23° 47′ 38″ agree to within 1.5″ (with the readings of notes a-d), the single years, 354 days and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29, to within 1.5″ (with the readings of notes e-g), and the months to within 1.1″ (with the readings of notes h, i, k and l; the edition's 15 of note k, N's reading against the 35 of C and O, is required, 6° 15′ 15″). Against Brahmagupta's parameter the collected years run ahead by about 8.8″ in each row, 2′ 39″ at 570.
