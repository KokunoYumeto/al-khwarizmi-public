@obs L07-L42. The beginnings of the houses for the ascendant at 1° to 30° of Libra, by the rule of p. 194 (JP2 0226). Checked with the obliquity 23° 35′ and the latitude 38° 40′: the degrees and minutes agree within 4.1′ in every place except the third house from 20° to 30° (L28-L42); all five columns lag by 2′ to 4.1′ from 8° to 19° (L16-L27). The third house steps from 22° 13′ at 19° to 24° 16′ at 20° (L27-L28), 2° 3′ where the other steps are about 1° 3′, and from there to 30° it is one degree more than the computation, within 1′ (24° 16′ for 23° 17′ at 20°, 4° 48′ of Capricorn for 3° 48.6′ at 30°); so the third house enters Capricorn at 26° (L34-L35, 0° 35′), where the computation gives 27° (0° 38.4′). No note bears on these eleven values.
@obs The names of the signs: the second house enters Sagittarius at 27°, the fourth Aquarius at 26°, the fifth Aquarius at 2° and Pisces at 29°, the sixth Pisces at 3° and Aries at 30°, as the computation gives (Aquarius 0° 3′ at 2° in the fifth house, Aries 1° 6.6′ at 30° in the sixth); only the third house enters Capricorn a row early, as above. The manuscripts have the following sign each time (notes f, m and p: Pisces, Aries and Taurus for «Aquar.», «Pisces» and «Aries»), as C and N have in the heads and the names of p. 196 (JP2 0228), and omit «Pisces» at 29° in the fifth house (note g).
