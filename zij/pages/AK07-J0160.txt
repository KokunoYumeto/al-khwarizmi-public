@obs L06-L35. The anomaly of Mercury moves by the excess of the conjunctions of Mercury over the sun's revolutions, with Brahmagupta's parameters (17 936 998 984 and 4 320 000 000 in 1 577 916 450 000 days) 3° 6′ 24″ 8‴ a day (p. 56, JP2 0088), 8s 27° 22′ 19.8″ in 30 Arab years (10 631 days). Checked: the 30 single years, 354 days and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29, agree to within 0.5″ (with the readings of notes d-f), the year 30 being 8s 27° 22′ 20″; the months, 30, 59, … 354 days, agree to within 0.2″ (with the reading of note g), the year of the months, 0s 19° 46′ 24″, being the single year 1. The collected years step from the radix 2s 8° 53′ 10″ (68° 53′ 10″) by 8s 27° 22′ 20″, the rounded value, to 480, and run ahead of the computation by up to 3.6″ (with the readings of notes a-c); 510 and 540 step by 19″ and 20″.
@obs L25, the row 570, 3s 18° 57′ 20″: the seconds are 9 less than the step of the table gives from the row 540, 3s 18° 57′ 29″, and 5.7″ less than the computation from the radix, 3s 18° 57′ 25.7″. No note bears on them.
