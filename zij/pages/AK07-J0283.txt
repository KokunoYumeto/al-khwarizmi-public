@obs Below the heading the index runs in two columns divided by a vertical rule; transcribed column by column: the left column L02-L39, the right L40-L77. The letter head «A.» (L02) is set in bold, centred in the left column. Each entry begins at the edge of its column, its further lines indented; the subentries that begin with a dash («— domorum», L15; «— plani», L43) are indented less than the further lines. Arabic numerals are the pages of the text and the commentary, roman numerals those of the foreword and the introduction, «T.» the tables and «N.» the footnotes. Words in italics: «Hidjra» (L18), «acofra» (L24), «Heza» (L59); the Greek «ἀναπλήρωσις» (L38) in the slanted Greek type of the edition.
@obs The semicolons of L19, L20 (three), L22 (two), L23, L24, L25, L46 and L53 stand after a thin space (7-13 px); written with a space.
