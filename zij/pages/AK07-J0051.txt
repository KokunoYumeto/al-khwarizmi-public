@obs L06-L17: the ascension of a sign in the right sphere: the sine of the obliquity, 23° 51′ (elgeib primum), the sine of its complement (secundum), the sine of the declination of the last degree of the sign (tertium) and the sine of its complement (quartum); the third times the second, divided by the first, times sixty, divided by the fourth, is the sine of the ascension, whose arc is taken. Checked: this is 60 sin δ cos ε / (sin ε cos δ) = 60 tan δ / tan ε, the sine of the right ascension α for a radius of sixty, since tan δ = tan ε sin α. L20-L27: the ascensions of Taurus and Gemini are found by subtracting those of the signs before them; Pisces, Libra and Virgo share the ascension of Aries, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius that of Taurus, Cancer, Capricorn and Sagittarius that of Gemini.
