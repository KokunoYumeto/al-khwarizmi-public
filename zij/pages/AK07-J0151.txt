@obs L07-L30, the hours. Checked against the daily motions of al-Khwārizmī's parameters (Tab. 6, p. 117, JP2 0149): the moon 32′ 56.45″ and the anomaly 32′ 39.75″ in an hour; all 24 hours of the moon and of the anomaly agree with n times these motions to within 0.5″, rounded to the second (with the readings of notes a-i). The 24 hours, 13° 10′ 35″ and 13° 3′ 54″, are the motions of one day (Tab. 7, p. 118, JP2 0150).
@obs L07-L36, the parts. The «partes horarum» are the sixtieths of an hour, 2 to 60 in steps of 2, the moon 32.94″ and the anomaly 32.66″ to a part; 60 parts are the motions of one hour. Checked: the moon agrees to within 0.8″ in all rows but the 26 parts, 14′ 12″ against 14′ 16.5″ (its neighbours 13′ 10″ and 15′ 22″); the anomaly agrees to within 0.5″ in all rows but the 42 and 44 parts, 23′ 52″ and 24′ 57″ against 22′ 51.8″ and 23′ 57.2″, the minutes one too large, the same minutes as the moon's in those rows (with the readings of notes k-n). No note of the apparatus bears on these three values; they are recorded here, without critical notes.
