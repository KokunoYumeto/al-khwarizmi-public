@obs L07-L36. The table gives the shadow, «umbra», of a gnomon of 12 digits for each altitude, «artifa» (al-irtifāʿ), from 1° to 90°, in digits and minutes, «Dak.» (daqāʾiq): the cotangent 12 cos α : sin α of Suter's commentary (p. 77, JP2 0109, L08), here written digits;minutes. Checked against 12 cot α: within 0.5′ in 62 of the 90 rows and within 1′ in 85; the deviations of more than 1′ are at 8° (85;22 for 85;23.1), 14° (48;9 for 48;7.8, the largest, 1.24′), 62° (6;24 for 6;22.8), 71° (4;9 for 4;7.9) and 78° (2;32 for 2;33.0). The shadow is 12;0 at 45° and 0;0 at 90°. Compared with al-Battānī's shadow table (Nallino, II, 60) the values agree except in nineteen rows, where they differ by 1′: they are 1′ less than al-Battānī's at 8°, 9°, 13°, 18°, 21°, 29°, 36°, 44°, 49°, 53°, 56°, 57°, 58° and 60°, 1′ more at 14°, 32°, 54°, 62° and 71°; al-Battānī's value is the nearer to the computation in each of them except at 29°.
@obs Notes on the readings. In twenty of the twenty-one notes on the figures the edition's value departs from both manuscripts; only at 65° (note t) does it follow C, 5;36 against O's 5;26 (5;35.7 computed). Suter applies to this table the rule of the sine table (p. 77, JP2 0109, L24-L25): the reading of the manuscripts where it differs from al-Battānī's by one unit of the last place, al-Battānī's value where the difference is larger (p. 69, JP2 0101, L40-L44). Checked: the edition's values are al-Battānī's and agree with 12 cot α to within 0.6′ at 1° (note a, the minutes 29 for the 23 of C and O), at 2°-5° (notes b, c, e and f, the digits 343, 228, 171 and 137 for C's 388, 243, 181 and 158 and O's 383, 273, 181 and 158; notes d and g, the minutes 58 and 10 for 47 and 25), at 10°, 19°, 20°, 22° and 24° (notes h, i, k, l and m, the minutes 3, 51, 58, 42 and 57 for 34, 45, 52, 45 and 55), at 33°, 35° and 42° (notes o, p and r, 29, 8 and 20 for 23, 6 and 18), at 65° (note t) and at 67° and 89° (notes u and v, 6 and 12 for 4 and 52); at 39° and 59° (notes q and s), where C's 48 and 12 are 1′ from al-Battānī's 49 and 13 and O's 58 and 22 further, the edition has al-Battānī's value (14;49.1 and 7;12.6 computed). The exception is 29° (note n): the edition's 21;39, for the 47 of C and O, is the computed value, 21;38.9, where al-Battānī has 21;40. The other eighteen differences of 1′ from al-Battānī's table stand in rows without a note, with the reading of the manuscripts, as the rule requires.
