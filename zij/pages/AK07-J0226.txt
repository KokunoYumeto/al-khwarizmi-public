@obs L09-L40. The beginnings of the second to the sixth house, in degrees and minutes of their signs, for the ascendant at 1° to 30° of Aries, by the rule of chapter 32 (Suter, p. 97, JP2 0129, L01-L19): the right ascension of the ascendant increased by one, two and three sixths of the night arc of the ascendant for the second, third and fourth houses, and by the half night arc and one and two sixths of the day arc for the fifth and sixth, each converted back into a longitude; the seventh house begins at the point opposite the ascendant. Checked: the 150 values agree within 2′ in 140 places with the beginnings so computed with the obliquity 23° 35′, al-Battānī's value, and the latitude 38° 40′ (Suter, p. 97, N03: 38° 30′ for Cordova, from the tables of the projection of the rays); with al-Khwārizmī's obliquity 23° 51′ no latitude fits as well, the best, 38° 6′, leaving deviations up to 10′. The larger deviations: the second house at 9°-13° of the ascendant (L19-L23), 2.3′ to 3.5′ more; the fourth at 19°-22° (L29-L32), 2.2′ to 3.4′ more; and the sixth at 3° and 4° (L12, L14), Virgo 0° 25′ and 1° 18′ for 0° 19.8′ and 1° 10.8′, 5.2′ and 7.2′ more, the steps of the column being 53′, 54′, 53′, 44′ and 52′ from 1° to 6° (the computation 51′ in each step).
