@obs L01-L10: the passage in square brackets is the addition of a later hand in C to chapter 21 (note 1, «man. secunda»), the only part of the text in Arabic figures, as Suter says on p. XXIII (JP2 0027, L12-L14): the solar motions after Ptolemy's Almagest, the year 365;14,48 days, the motion in a day, an hour, a month of thirty days, an Egyptian year of 365 days and eighteen Egyptian years. «minutis» are minutes and «minutiis» seconds; the editor's «(sic)» (L02) marks «minutiis» where the series would lead one to expect «secundis». L08: «1350 (sic! recte 355)», the editor's correction in the line, «355» being the degrees of eighteen years after the full circles are cast off (L09-L10).
@obs Checked: 360 degrees divided by 365;14,48 days give 0;59,8,17,13,12,30… a day, the text's 0;59,8,17,13,12,31 «fere» (L02-L03); a twenty-fourth of it, 0;2,27,50,43,3,1, the hour (L03-L04); 365 times it, 359;45,24,45,21,8,35, the Egyptian year (L06-L07); eighteen times that, 6475;37,25,36,20,34,30, less seventeen circles, 355;37,25,36,20,34,30 (L08-L09), as Suter corrects. Thirty times the daily motion is 29;34,8,36,36,15,30, where the text has 35 thirds (L05); see the critical note.
@obs L11: the heading of chapter 22, in bold, centred; in the left margin beside it «C 71r, O 128v», and beside L12 «N 36v», the folios at which the chapter begins. L01: «Ptolomaeum» is letter-spaced, «almagesti» in italics; L18 and L20 «shemeli», northern, and «genubi», southern, in italics.
