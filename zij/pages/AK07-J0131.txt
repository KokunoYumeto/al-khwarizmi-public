@obs L26-L35 and N06. Checked: in the triangle ABC with the right angle at C, the hypotenuse AB = 60° and the sides AC = b and BC = x, the relation cos c = cos a · cos b reads cos 60° = cos x · cos b, whence cos x = cos 60° : cos b. With the chord crd θ = 2R sin (θ/2), crd² θ = 2R² (1 − cos θ), so that crd² 60° − crd² b = 2R² (cos b − cos 60°) and crd² x = 2R² (cos b − cos 60°) : cos b; with «cos b» taken as the cosine in the circle of radius R, R cos b, this is the formula of L27. Nallino (I, 307-308) carries out the same reduction; on I, 307 he reports al-Battānī's rule: the square of the chord of the latitude taken from 3600, the remainder divided and its square root taken.
