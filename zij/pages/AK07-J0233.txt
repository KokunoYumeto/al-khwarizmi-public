@obs L07-L40. The beginnings of the houses for the ascendant at 1° to 30° of Scorpius, by the rule of p. 194 (JP2 0226). Checked with the obliquity 23° 35′ and the latitude 38° 40′: the degrees and minutes agree within 2′ in 115 of the 150 places and within 3′ in 133. The fourth house runs ahead by 2.1′ to 3.5′ from 7° to 16° (L13-L22) and lags by 2′ to 2.6′ at 20°-22°. From 26° (L35) the columns run ahead of the computation, increasingly, at 30° (L40) by 4.4′, 11.4′, 19.0′, 10.3′ and 5.5′ in the second to the sixth house, the counterpart of the lag at the end of Tab. 80 for Taurus (p. 195, JP2 0227).
