@obs L06-L35. The rows are the single years 1-30, whose values are added to a row of Tab. 69 or Tab. 70 (pp. 183-184) for the conjunction or the opposition; Suter's example adds the row of 17 (L22), 5h 43m 50s, 5s 27° 30′ 35″, 7s 16° 38′ 40″ and 4s 16° 48′ 10″ (p. 82, JP2 0114, L24), and the 8h 48m 7s of the year 1 (L06) is quoted on p. 82, L05. The time is the excess of 12 synodic months a year over the days of the years, 354 in a common year and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29 of Tab. 2 (p. 110, JP2 0142); where the months fall short of the days, a day is subtracted and 24 hours are added. Checked: the column «Dies subtrahendi» has 1 in exactly these rows; the times follow p · 8h 48m 27.1s less the leap days (0h 13m 33s at 30) within 10s in every row except at 1, 4, 5, 21 and 29: with 27, 13 and 36 for the printed 7, 53 and 56 the first, the second and the fourth agree (see the critical notes); at 5, 20h 15m 14s, the law gives 20h 2m 15s, and at 29, 15h 29m 22s, it gives 15h 25m 6s.
