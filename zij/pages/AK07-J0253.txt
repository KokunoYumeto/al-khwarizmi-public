The second and the third field have no heads. Double rules divide the fields.
@obs The numbers of the first field (L08-L13) are those of the third field of p. 220 (JP2 0252, L24-L29), the field there of 15° of Scorpius, 62 of the 72 equal; the ten differences are listed on that page. @obs The numbers at the head of the second field are a third, a half and two thirds of its last number, 182° 3′ (L21): 60° 41′, 91° 1.5′ and 121° 22′, printed 60° 40′ (O's 60° 41′, note f), 91° 1′ and 121° 22′ (L15).
