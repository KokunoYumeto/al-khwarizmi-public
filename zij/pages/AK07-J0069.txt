Checked: counted from the epoch of the tables, Thursday 15 July 622 (p. 33, JP2 0065), 1 Ramaḍān 529 falls on Friday 14 June 1135 and 1 Muḥarram 520 on Tuesday 26 January 1126, as the examples and the text say; counted from the civil epoch, Friday 16 July 622, both would fall a day later. The notae of the two examples agree with the calendar: Ramaḍān has the nota 6 and Muḥarram 1, and the first eight months have 236 days, five weekdays more than whole weeks; the year 19 has 6 and the year 10 has 1, and the nine years from the beginning of the tenth to that of the nineteenth have 3190 days, also five weekdays more.
