@obs L06-L35. The anomaly of Venus moves by the excess of the conjunctions of Venus over the sun's revolutions, with Brahmagupta's parameters (7 022 389 492 and 4 320 000 000 in 1 577 916 450 000 days) 36′ 59″ 34‴ 14⁗ a day (p. 56, JP2 0088), 2s 14° 30′ 53.8″ in 30 Arab years (10 631 days). Checked: the 30 single years, 354 days and 355 in the leap years 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29, agree to within 1.1″ (with the readings of notes h-l), the year 30 being 2s 14° 30′ 53″. The collected years step from the radix 1s 16° 0′ 3″ (46° 0′ 3″) by 2s 14° 30′ 53″ or 54″, about half a second less than the computed step, so that they fall behind the computation by up to 8.9″ at 570 (with the readings of notes a-g, and Björnbo's correction 13 of note b, where C has 27 and O and N 18).
@obs L08, the row 60, 6s 15° 1′ 30″: the seconds are 20 too small; the radix and two steps give 6s 15° 1′ 50″ (computed 6s 15° 1′ 50.7″), and the row 90, 8s 29° 32′ 43″, follows from 50″, not from 30″. No note bears on them.
@obs L06-L17, the months: 30, 59, … 354 days, the months of 30 and 29 days in turn. Checked: they agree with the computation to within 1.7″ (with the reading of note m), except Zafar, 1s 6° 22′ 32″ against 34.7″ computed, and Jumedi I., printed 3s 1° 14′ 17″, computed 3s 1° 14′ 56.4″: its seconds are 40 too small, and Jumedi II., 3s 19° 7′ 45″, follows 29 days later from 57″, not from 17″. No note bears on them. The year of the months, 7s 8° 15′ 29″, is the single year 1, 7s 8° 15′ 28″, to the second.
