@obs L01-L18: fractions other than minutes and seconds: the numbers are turned into the kind of the lowest order in them, «in genus ultime differentie», seconds into seconds, thirds into thirds (see the critical note on L03), and for parts such as fifths or sevenths into those parts; the product is raised to whole numbers by dividing it by the product of the kinds. Three sevenths by four ninths: the sevenths and ninths stand in the first place of the fraction, as minutes do, and their product in the place of the seconds; it is divided by seven times nine; the result, twelve parts of sixty-three parts of one (see the critical note on L18).
