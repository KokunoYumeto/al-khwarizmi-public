@obs The page number «( 18 )» stands at the head of the page, centred. The page has no footnotes and no signature.
@obs L01-L13: the places of the fractions: the first place is that of the degrees, «graduum», where the whole number stands, the second that of the minutes, the third of the seconds, the fourth of the thirds, and so on to the ninth and tenth place. A whole number by a whole number gives a whole number, a whole number by a fraction a fraction of the same kind: two degrees by two minutes, four minutes; three degrees by six thirds, eighteen thirds. Minutes by minutes give seconds; the products of seconds by seconds, thirds by thirds and fourths by fourths are printed as thirds, fourths and fourths (see the critical note). The rule: the places of the two factors are joined, and the number is found as for whole numbers.
@obs L14-L24: six minutes by seven minutes, forty-two seconds, minutes being parts of sixty and their products parts of 3600; seven seconds by nine minutes, sixty-three thirds, which are one second and three thirds, seconds being parts of 3600 and their products with minutes parts of 216000, «ducentis milibus et xvi. milibus», which are thirds, 216000 being sixty times 3600.
