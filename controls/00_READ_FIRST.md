# al-Khwarizmi - one-author Tier-A source Project

This is an additive, source-gated preparation Project for the essential surviving mathematical corpus associated with Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi. Uploading it executes nothing. It contains no newly made transcription and no publication action.

## Exact boundary

The Project maps six Tier-A reader lanes without pretending that lost Arabic originals survive:

1. **Algebra:** the complete existing 1939 Arabic-edition packet is preserved byte-for-byte inside `11_ALGEBRA_EXISTING_PACKET_EXACT.zip`. Its four 400-ppi source archives contain 130 physical frames. Production coverage remains 0/130; the first untouched unit is frame 001.
2. **Arithmetic:** the Arabic Indian-arithmetic original is lost. The controlling runnable witness here is Boncompagni's 1857 Latin *Algoritmi de numero Indorum* in 30 full-color BSB/MDZ frames, with a lower-resolution Internet Archive copy as comparator. Do not call the Latin witness an Arabic original. The BnF authority note warns that the mid-twelfth-century `Dixit Algorizmi` tradition is composite and includes material deriving from Boethius and Nicomachus.
3. **Geography:** the controlling payload is the complete 218-frame Columbia/Arabic Collections Online copy, catalogued as Baghdad 1962 but physically containing a 1962 reprint title around Hans von Mzik's complete 1926 Leipzig edition. Preserve physical order. Its Arabic body is followed by plates and reverse-bound German preliminaries because of right-to-left binding.
4. **Zij:** the Arabic *Zij al-Sindhind* is lost. `40`-`42` preserve a 296-image Internet Archive scan and 294-page navigation PDF of Suter's 1914 edition of the Majriti recension in Adelard's Latin translation. It is a derivative recension with Suter/Majriti/Adelard layers, never al-Khwarizmi's recovered Arabic text.
5. **Practical astronomy, astrolabe, and sundial material:** `50` supplies Charette and Schmidl's 98-page critical study/edition/translation and `51` a 20-page historical comparator. These establish a defensible fragment and attribution map. They do not replace the unavailable primary pixels for Berlin Landberg 56 fols. 77v-97v or Ayasofya 4830 fols. 183-200 and 231v-235r. This lane is evidence-ready but primary-pixel-held.
6. **Jewish calendar:** the target treatise occupies physical PDF pages 2-10 of the 320-page Hyderabad 1948 compilation; page 1 is compilation context and pages 11-320 are other works. De Blois shows that the printed text perpetuates manuscript errors and adds numerical errors. The unique Patna/Khuda Bakhsh 2468 manuscript pixels remain unavailable. The printed witness is runnable only with that warning.
